# Slideshow 01 — lose weight 6 months

- **Hook:** I couldn't lose weight for 6 months in the gym then I started doing this
- **Lane:** fat loss
- **Audience:** first account — people who want more steps, want to lose fat, or want consistent steps
- **Core desire:** make walking non-negotiable so the fat-loss effort outside the gym finally sticks
- **Product condition shown:** active walking (selected scrolling apps unlock only while she is actively walking)
- **End action:** fill-in-the-blank reply — drop your daily step goal as a number
- **Styles used:** `gym-mirror-selfie-bright-commercial`, `walk-pov-overhead-hard-sun`; new looks for the post-gym scroll failure and the lock-screen payoff
