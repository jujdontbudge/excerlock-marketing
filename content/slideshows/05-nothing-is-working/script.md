Written to `research/slideshows/script-writing-guide.md` (the captain's walkthrough plus his image and reference preferences). Face photos: 2 (slide 1 new, slide 2 the generated before picture). Screenshots: 1 slide (slide 3), plus the captain's CTA screenshot. One organic reference per slide, none repeated in any deck. Images here are references and prompts only; nothing is generated until the captain says go.
