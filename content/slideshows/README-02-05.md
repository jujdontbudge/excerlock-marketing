## The story of each deck

**02, how i get my steps in as a phone addict.** A girl in a gym mirror says she is on her phone all day and still gets her steps, and proves it on slide 2 with her steps and her screen time side by side. Then she gives the three ways she does it: a walk after every meal, ten minutes on the treadmill before and after every lift, and getting up ten minutes earlier to walk. In the middle she mentions the trick that makes it work for a phone addict: ExcerLock blocks her apps unless she is moving, so she scrolls on the treadmill. The deck ends on the morning-walk tip; the old rule slide was cut as filler.

**03, i didn't want to get 10k steps today but...** She is at the gym not wanting to walk, then remembers the before picture and how she used to be. That is why she now hits 15k every day, and she shows how: steps are the whole day added up (a Notes cheat sheet of where they come from), ExcerLock blocks her screen time until she is on the treadmill or walking, a 30 to 40 minute walk after dinner, a 30 minute incline walk at the end of every gym session, and a walking pad in front of the TV. Three help slides after the CTA, as the captain asked.

**04, why you're not hitting your steps (it's not your legs).** She says it is not your legs, and shows her steps screenshot to prove she knows. Then four "your doing this, do this instead" slides: sitting through lunch (walk 30 minutes of it), skipping the treadmill after lifting (incline 12, speed 3, 20 minutes), sitting at the desk for 8 hours (walk every hour, furthest printer and bathroom), and parking as close as you can (park as far as you can). In the same "ur not" voice, slide 5 says you are not using ExcerLock to block scrolling unless you move. The payoff: it's not your legs, it's your thumbs.

**05, want to lose fat but nothing is working? this is what i did.** She shows herself now, then the before picture with "gym 5x a week and NOTHING was moving". What she did: walked 10,000 steps every day instead of hours of cardio (month screenshot as the receipt), ate in a deficit with 1g of protein per lb, and lifted first with 20 minutes of incline walking after. The reason the 10k actually happened is ExcerLock blocking TikTok unless she walks. The deck ends on the last value slide; the old "other 23 hours" closer was cut as filler.

## Self-check against the walkthrough, slide by slide

Checklist from the guide: (1) value slides deliver the title literally in the title's frame; (2) slide 2 short, casual, a reason to swipe; (3) every value slide a real tip from a real post, cited; (4) CTA one aside in the captain's patterns, never "i use excerlock" as the frame, image left blank; (5) slide lengths vary; (6) a 13-year-old can retell it; (7) max two faces with an exact reference each, at most two screenshots, one reference per slide, none repeated; (8) no statistic as a value slide, no em dashes, lowercase and casual. Image preferences: slide 1 gym/walking mirror pic, proof real, value photo on-message, treadmill after faces are spent, morning walk faceless, no filler closer.
