## The story of each deck

**02, how i get my steps in as a phone addict.** A girl who is on her phone all day says she still gets her steps, and proves it on slide 2 with her steps and her screen time side by side. Then she gives the three ways she does it: a walk after every meal, ten minutes on the treadmill before and after every lift, and getting up ten minutes earlier to walk. In the middle she mentions the trick that makes it work for a phone addict: ExcerLock blocks her apps unless she is moving, so she scrolls on the treadmill. The rule at the end: if you have time to be on your phone you have time to walk.

**03, i didn't want to get 10k steps today but...** She wakes up not wanting to walk, then remembers the before picture and how she used to be. That is why she now hits 15k every day, and she shows how: steps are the whole day added up (a Notes cheat sheet of where they come from), ExcerLock blocks her screen time until she is on the treadmill or walking, a 30 to 40 minute walk after dinner, and a 30 minute incline walk at the end of every gym session. The last slide is her 15,860 average with the line "ignore your feelings and just get it done".

**04, why you're not hitting your steps (it's not your legs).** She says it is not your legs, and shows her steps screenshot to prove she knows. Then four "your doing this, do this instead" slides: sitting through lunch (walk 30 minutes of it), skipping the treadmill after lifting (incline 12, speed 3, 20 minutes), sitting at the desk for 8 hours (walk every hour, furthest printer and bathroom), and parking as close as you can (park as far as you can). In the same "ur not" voice, slide 5 says you are not using ExcerLock to block scrolling unless you move. The payoff: it's not your legs, it's your thumbs.

**05, want to lose fat but nothing is working? this is what i did.** She shows herself now, then the before picture with "gym 5x a week and NOTHING was moving". What she did: walked 10,000 steps every day instead of hours of cardio (month screenshot as the receipt), ate in a deficit with 1g of protein per lb, and lifted first with 20 minutes of incline walking after. The reason the 10k actually happened is ExcerLock blocking TikTok unless she walks. The last slide: the gym was never the problem, the other 23 hours were.

## Self-check against the walkthrough, slide by slide

Checklist from the guide: (1) value slides deliver the title literally in the title's frame; (2) slide 2 short, casual, a reason to swipe; (3) every value slide a real tip from a real post, cited; (4) CTA one aside in the captain's patterns, never "i use excerlock" as the frame; (5) slide lengths vary; (6) a 13-year-old can retell it; (7) max two faces, each with an exact organic reference, screens real; (8) no statistic as a value slide, no em dashes, lowercase and casual.

## Notes for the captain

- The bracketed step counts ("thats like 1,200 steps x 3 meals", "thats 2,500 free steps") are sizing lines under a tip, not statistics standing as a value slide; they come from the @autumn.wlf cheat sheet and @gymmyworm's mile figures. Easy to cut if they read as stats.
- Deck 04 has 8 slides because the "your doing X" frame needed four instead of three to cover lunch, gym, desk and car without doubling up; the captain's range for this deck was 7 to 8.
- The before picture (`before-final.png`) appears on 03 S2 and 05 S2 on purpose: one consistent person. The lock-screen walking POV from slideshow 01 is reused only on 02 S7; decks 03, 04 and 05 each get a different phone scene (treadmill, bed, walking pad).
