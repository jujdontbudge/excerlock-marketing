# ExcerLock hook bank (draft)

A clean, deduplicated bank of the ExcerLock hook candidates from the worksheet in `research/slideshows/hooks.md` (§ "ExcerLock hook worksheet"), ingested from the Google Doc worksheet on 2026-09-16. This is a draft for review, not approved copy: the hook wording is the author's raw brainstorm with spelling normalised, and no hook here is cleared for publishing. Judge each hook against the product truth in `strategy/excerlock-slideshow-questions.md` before use; ExcerLock can prove consistency, attendance, steps, and screen-time/lock behaviour — not fat loss.

No two hook lines in the export are word-for-word identical, so nothing was merged.

## How to read the tables

- **Hook** — cleaned text; `(unfinished)` marks fragments the author left incomplete.
- **Lane** — `gym` = gym-girl audience, `walking` = walking-girl audience, `both` = works for both, following the audience split in `research/video-analysis-595/report.md` §9 (9a gym girls, 9c walking girls).
- **ExcerLock mechanism** — the unlock condition the hook implies: gym arrival/stay/time, step target, walking duration, active walking — or `none stated`.
- **Claim check**:
    - `ok` — provable from the product (consistency, attendance, steps, screen-time/lock behaviour) with no numeric promise beyond what a real screenshot can show.
    - `needs proof` — a specific number; usable only with a real Apple Health / app record showing that exact number.
    - `rewrite` — a weight-loss, fat-loss, or body-transformation promise. ExcerLock can prove consistency, not fat loss; these conflict with the "do not copy" list in `research/video-analysis-595/report.md` §10 and the workspace's truthful-representation rule.
- **Suggested safe rewrite** — present only on `rewrite` rows. These are suggestions, not approved copy: they keep the hook's mechanism (specificity, borrowed credibility, curiosity gap, transformation tease) but promise only what ExcerLock can show.

## Gaps

Patterns from the worksheet still without a hook, one line each, to be filled later:

- **News** — blank; worksheet shape is "At last: \[new result or solution\]", so an arrival/unlock announcement could fit.
- **Specific-person personalization** — blank; needs one exact person, situation, and problem (e.g. a gym girly or a desk-worker step counter).
- **Secret knowledge** — blank; needs "this feels illegal to know" framing aimed at a gym or step method.
- **Repeatable signature** — blank; needs one fixed opening line reused on every post.
- **Engagement bait — use cautiously** — blank; only worth filling with a question that yields useful answers, not comments alone.
- **Reuse a psychological mechanism in a new scenario** — blank; needs suspicion → discovery moved to a gym or steps scenario.
