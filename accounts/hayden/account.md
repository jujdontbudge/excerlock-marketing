### Don'ts

- Don't make her plain, ungroomed or "ordinary" to look organic. Why: the captain rejected Pro v1 as "possibly the ugliest girl" he had seen. Organic means the photo's treatment, never a plainer person.
- Don't swap her into a reference that hides her waist (a loose or long top, a square front stance, a far low camera). Pick another reference. Why: that combination made Pro v1 boxy and ordinary.
- Don't give her flat, limp or tucked hair. Why: long, voluminous hair is a major part of the inspiration's appeal.
- Accessories follow the selected scene, not a retired casting render. Why: the earlier non-canonical render carried another character’s phone case.
- Don't copy @lilyterpfr: not her face, hair colouring, rooms, boyfriend, family, hooks, outfits or posts, and never use her photos as references. Why: Hayden is a distinct fictional woman, never a lookalike.
- Don't make her look under 18, bodybuilder-heavy, stage-lean, surgical or unhealthily underweight. Why: she must read as a believable young adult.
