Hayden is a new TikTok account with no decks yet. Her character is being designed with the same process as Zoey's and must look clearly different from Zoey. Inspiration account: @lilyterpfr (https://www.tiktok.com/@lilyterpfr, 507 public posts from 2022-07-24 to 2026-09-17, analysed 2026-09-22; full analysis in the archive at `data/hayden-inspiration-analysis.md` and `data/hayden-reference-appearance-reanalysis/report.md`).

**Design status: not approved.** The captain chose candidate D as the direction, but no render is canonical yet, and her own phone case is not chosen. Open question: the archive backlog (2026-09-23) says the captain later "abandoned the earlier Hayden designs, moved to a fresh character, and approved dark-haired Kinsley". Whether Kinsley replaces candidate D for this account is not confirmed; ask the captain before making any Hayden image.

## Audience

Walking girls. The inspiration account is an approachable American gym and lifestyle creator whose page reads as a person first and a fitness account second.

## Character

### Direction: candidate D, made more attractive and slimmer

The captain picked candidate D ("the last is best") and asked for her to be organic and realistic, "a bit skinnier, almost unrealistic waist but nothing too dramatic that it is obviously edited". The corrected target (reanalysis, 2026-09-22):

- Clearly an adult, 23, a friendly all-American gym girl.
- Face: soft oval face, blue-grey eyes that read at selfie distance, groomed darker sandy brows, long mascara lashes, a small slightly upturned nose, full lips with tinted balm, light bronzer and blush, a light dusting of sun freckles over the nose and cheeks, and a broad natural smile with slight eye crinkles. Pretty enough to stop a scroll, with natural asymmetry.
- Hair: long, past the shoulder blades, honey blonde with darker roots and brighter face-framing pieces, loose lived-in waves with volume at the crown, worn down and over one shoulder.
- Skin: warm light tan, visible pores, freckles, a slight sheen.
- Body: slimmer overall than candidate D, with an unusually narrow but still plausible waist, visibly exposed; a gentle hip curve; long toned legs and rounded athletic glutes in proportion to the slimmer torso; slim toned arms; narrow shoulders. Healthy and believable.
- Clothes that show her shape: a fitted cropped top in a light or bright colour, low-to-mid rise shorts or leggings, white crew socks, sneakers, a thin gold necklace and small hoops.
- Casting picture: a bedroom mirror selfie, phone held at chest-to-chin height and angled slightly down, body turned about 25 degrees with the weight on the back leg and a hip pop, filling 75 to 85% of the frame height, head to mid-shin, little headroom.

### Don'ts

- Don't drift her blue-grey eyes, sun freckles, broad natural smile, honey-blonde hair or darker roots. Why: those traits define the direction the captain selected.
- Don't make her plain, ungroomed or "ordinary" to look organic. Why: the captain rejected Pro v1 as "possibly the ugliest girl" he had seen; its prompt applied realism to her instead of the photo. Organic means the photo's treatment, never a plainer person.
- Don't hide her waist: no loose or long top, no high waistband with a long top, no square front stance with arms hanging, no low camera far away. Why: that combination made Pro v1 boxy and ordinary even though the prompt asked for a narrow waist.
- Don't make the waist so extreme that the ribs, pelvis, clothes, mirror, doorframe or room lines warp. Why: the captain does not want the picture to look obviously edited.
- Don't give her flat, limp or tucked hair. Why: long, voluminous hair is a major part of the inspiration's appeal, and Pro v1 lost it.
- Don't give her another account's props (e.g. a leopard phone case). Why: Pro v2 inherited Zoey's leopard case and was marked non-canonical for it.
- Don't show her phone until the captain has chosen her own case design. Why: a visible phone always needs a case, and her case must be hers, defined here first.
- Don't copy @lilyterpfr: not her facial geometry, hair colouring, expressions, LED-selfie look, boyfriend, family, rooms, lash-serum hook, outfits or posts. Why: Hayden is a distinct fictional woman, never a lookalike or impersonation.
- Don't attach candidate D, a creator frame or any source photograph when generating her casting anchor; describe her in words only. Why: an attached photo makes the model edit or copy that photo instead of creating a new anchor. Candidate D is an internal benchmark for the reviewer only.
- Don't repaint candidate D into a new scene with legacy Nano Banana 2.5. Why: that path smoothed the face and skin, regularised the scene and looked more synthetic than candidate D ("not realistic what so ever").
- Don't use a rejected render as a reference: `hayden-final-anchor-v1.png`, `hayden-strict-edit-2.5-v2.png`, `hayden-pro-v1.jpg` (see `assets.md`). Why: the captain rejected each one.
- Don't make her look under 21, bodybuilder-heavy, stage-lean, surgical or underweight. Why: she must read as a believable adult gym and lifestyle creator.
