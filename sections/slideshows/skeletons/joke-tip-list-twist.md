# Joke tip list with a twist (heartbreak twist)

**Structure:** a sincere "N tips" title, a run of plain, believable numbered tips, with tip 3 swapped for a heartbreak line, then more straight tips as if nothing happened.

## Slide count
7: a title, 2 straight tips, the heartbreak twist as tip 3 on slide 4, then 3 more straight tips. Source: [@gazellleee, 7 slides](https://www.tiktok.com/@gazellleee/photo/7549800558354271504) (2.6M views, 82.7K saves).

All photos are vertical 9:16 phone shots. Positions below are measured from the top of the frame.

## Slide by slide

### Slide 1: title
- **Role:** looks completely sincere, so nothing hints at the twist.
- **Text:** "6 [topic] tips for [demographic]"
  - `[topic]`: what the tips are about (e.g., gym, walking, fat loss).
  - `[demographic]`: a specific gym stage or identity the viewer recognizes, not a broad audience or an emotional label. Options from the tagged hooks: beginners, beginner gym girls, girls starting from zero, lazy girls, tall girls. Don't call the viewer anxious; the captain rejected that label.
- **Placement:** centred, upper third, small, on empty space above the subject.
- **Image:** full-body mirror selfie, face covered by the phone. Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@gazellleee/photo/7549800558354271504 slide 1.

### Slide 2: tip 1
- **Role:** an ordinary, believable tip that starts the dull rhythm.
- **Text:** an 8-12 word tip.
- **Placement:** upper third, small.
- **Image:** top-down POV of own torso and outfit holding a drink, no face. Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@gazellleee/photo/7549800558354271504 slide 2.

### Slide 3: tip 2
- **Role:** another plain tip; still nothing unusual.
- **Text:** an 8-12 word tip.
- **Placement:** upper part of the frame, clear of the subject.
- **Image:** top-down photo of a home-cooked plate. Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@gazellleee/photo/7549800558354271504 slide 3.

### Slide 4: tip 3, the heartbreak twist
- **Role:** breaks the list with a heartbreak, dressed up as just another numbered tip.
- **Text:** verbatim exactly as on the source: `3. remember how you felt the night you found out everything`
- **Placement:** lower third, the same small caption as every tip.
- **Image:** dark, tilted night-time selfie (car or bedroom), face visible for the first time, a small knowing look. The mood flip from bright to dark is half the joke. Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@gazellleee/photo/7549800558354271504 slide 7.

### Slide 5: tip 4
- **Role:** back to plain tips as if nothing happened; the deadpan return makes the twist funnier.

### Slide 6: tip 5

- **Text:** a 1-3 word tip.
- **Placement:** around face height, small.

### Slide 7: tip 6
- **Role:** ends on the longest, most sensible tip, still deadpan.
- **Text:** a 10-14 word tip.
- **Placement:** lower middle, in empty space beside the subject, small.
- **Image:** full-body mirror selfie on a bright gym floor. Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@gazellleee/photo/7549800558354271504 slide 6.

## Hook
Rigid template: "6 [topic] tips for [demographic]". Gentle, helpful, deadpan. The hook's job is to hide the joke.

## Images
- **Tip slides:** casual phone photos from the poster's own life: mirror selfies in the gym or at home with the face half covered by the phone, a top-down plate, a POV shot holding a drink. Organic and unposed, but the body in them is aspirational: it quietly shows the result. Bright to neutral daylight or gym light. The image loosely goes with the tip or is just a lifestyle shot; it doesn't need to illustrate it.
- **Twist slide (slide 4):** the image flips too. Dark, low-light, night-time selfie (car, bedroom, night out), a tilted angle, face visible, a small knowing look. The mood change between image and text is part of the punchline.
- Same person throughout, face never fully posed, never looks like stock.

## Text style
- Plain TikTok sans (classic caption font), white with a thin black outline, small to medium size.
- Placed wherever the photo has space, usually centre or upper-middle; it moves between slides.
- All lowercase, numbered "1." to "N.", no emojis, no end punctuation.

## Fixed vs open
- **Fixed:** rigid hook format; numbered lowercase format kept the same on every slide including the twist; plain tips before the twist; the twist is on slide 4 and is fixed text verbatim ("3. remember how you felt the night you found out everything"); the image mood flips on the twist slide.
- **Open:** the topic of the list, which tips, the setting of the photos.

## Why it works
The straight tips build a rhythm and a bit of boredom; slide 4 breaks it with a heartbreak people recognise, which reads as funny and real at once. The vague wound lets every viewer fill in their own story. Going straight back to plain tips afterwards keeps the deadpan going. It also admits the honest motive behind many glow-ups, which feels more human than the advice.

## Porting
- "6 walking tips for lazy girls": plain walking habits, tip 3 is the heartbreak line, then back to walking tips.
- "6 gym tips for girls starting from zero": normal routine tips with the heartbreak line as tip 3.
- "6 fat loss tips for beginners": diet and walking basics with the heartbreak line as tip 3.
- "6 gym tips for tall girls": standard training tips with the heartbreak line as tip 3.
