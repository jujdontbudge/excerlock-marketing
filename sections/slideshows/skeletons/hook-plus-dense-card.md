# Hook plus one dense text slide (plan breakdown card)

**Structure:** a casual selfie with a quoted beginner's wish, then one notes-app style card that answers it with a full, number-packed breakdown.

## Slide count
2: a quoted wish, then one answer card. Source: [@autumn.wlf, 2 slides](https://www.tiktok.com/@autumn.wlf/photo/7441340934559943982) (839.8K views, 17.3K saves). **Slide count is locked at 2** — this is a deliberate exception to the house 5-7-slide default; the format is one hook plus one dense card.

Both slides are 3:4. Positions below are measured from the top of the frame.

## Slide by slide

### Slide 1: quoted wish
- **Role:** the viewer hears their own thought: a goal with a number and the admission of being stuck.
- **Text (template, rigid):** `"i want to [goal with a number] but idk what to do"`. Keep the lowercase texting voice, the straight quotes and the `but idk` admission exactly; only the goal-with-a-number slot changes (lose 5kg before summer, walk 20k steps a day). 9-13 words.
- **Placement:** centred, just below the middle, over hair or shoulder, not the face. Small plain white text, no outline.
- **Image:** sideways, cropped candid selfie holding an ordinary gym object. Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@autumn.wlf/photo/7441340934559943982 slide 1.

### Slide 2: the plan card
- **Role:** the whole answer on one screenshot; numbers turn a big goal into small sums, so people save it.
- **Text:** an all-caps title naming the goal, then 3 short labelled sections of dash bullets, every bullet with a number. About 75-90 words. Keep the numbers internally consistent (the per-day and per-week figures must add up to the goal); every claim is real arithmetic, not invented stats.
- **Placement:** left-aligned, filling the top four fifths of the card; title bigger and bold.
- **Image:** no photo: a black dark-mode notes-app card, rendered as text (not an AI image). Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@autumn.wlf/photo/7441340934559943982 slide 2.

## Hook
A quoted first-person wish, the voice of the viewer themselves, not the poster. Gentle, a bit helpless. The poster answers as a knowledgeable friend on slide 2, never as a coach giving orders.

## Images
- **Slide 1:** a casual, imperfect selfie: odd angle, tilted or sideways, face cropped (only mouth or hair visible), holding something ordinary. Indoors, warm light, organic. It says "a normal person like you asked this".
- **Slide 2:** no photo. A plain black background like a notes-app screenshot in dark mode.
- The contrast between a messy human photo and a clean, info-heavy card is the point.

## Text style
- **Slide 1:** plain TikTok sans, white, no outline, medium size, centred; straight curly quotes around the line; lowercase.
- **Slide 2:** off-white system sans on black, left-aligned, filling the top two-thirds. Title in bold caps with a colon; section labels italic and underlined; bullets with long dashes; key numbers underlined. No emojis.

## Fixed vs open
- **Fixed:** the slide-1 hook template `"i want to [goal with a number] but idk what to do"` (rigid); two beats: a quoted wish, then the answer card; the answer is all specifics and numbers; the card's notes-app look (dark, left-aligned, labels + bullets); everyday examples that add up to the goal.
- **Open:** the goal and its number, the section labels, the bullets inside each section, the setting of the selfie.

## Why it works
The quote names a goal the viewer already has plus the exact moment they get stuck. The card removes that feeling in one screen with numbers they can check, so it gets saved and screenshotted. Everyday examples make a big number feel reachable without effort.

## Porting
- "i want to lose 5kg before summer but idk where to start": card with daily deficit, steps and weeks.
- "i want to go to the gym 4x a week but i always quit": card with a weekly split, session length and the minimum week.
- "i want to walk 20k steps a day but i have a desk job": card with steps per hour, walking breaks and commute swaps.
- "i want visible abs but idk what that takes": card with body-fat range, protein grams and daily steps.
- "i want to hit 1 million steps this month": card with daily target, per-walk counts and catch-up days.
