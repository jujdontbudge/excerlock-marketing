# How to become addicted to walking / gym

1. How to become addicted to walking / gym
2. make it something you want to repeat >>>
3. try different workouts until you find one you actually enjoy. you don’t have to love everything.
4. start with one walk around the block. it’s easier to begin when the goal feels small.
5. keep track of your workouts and try to beat your old score when you can.
6. pair your walk or gym session with something you enjoy, like grabbing a coffee afterwards.
