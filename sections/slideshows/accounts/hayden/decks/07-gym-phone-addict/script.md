# How I consistently go to the gym while being a phone addict

1. How I consistently go to the gym while being a phone addict
2. make the gym part easier first >>>
3. pick a set gym time. try mornings if you keep talking yourself out of going later.
4. plan your workout the day before, so you’re not deciding what to do when you get there.
5. bring a notebook for your workout log, so you don’t need your phone to check your lifts.
6. keep your gym essentials in your bag, so you’re not hunting for them before you leave.
