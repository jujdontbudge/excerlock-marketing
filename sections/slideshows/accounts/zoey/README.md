## Rules for Zoey's decks

- **Slide 1 picture.** Her world is the gym and the walk. Slide 1 is an aesthetic gym or walking mirror selfie of her holding the phone: a commercial gym, studio, locker-room or gym-bathroom mirror. Don't open on a phone-in-bed or doomscroll picture. Why: slide 1 has to attract, and a good-looking gym picture is what this audience stops for.
- Don't copy anything from @rrachfitness https://www.tiktok.com/@rrachfitness/photo/7378132607768349985 slide 2. Why: the captain banned it; it was removed from every deck.
- Don't use a Notes-style or cheat-sheet source for her tips: @autumn.wlf's cheat sheet and @devamshagunput's Notes guide were rejected for her decks (`H-no-cheat-sheet`). Why: the app is the cheat.
- The proof slide uses her receipts from `sections/slideshows/assets.md`. For a steps-plus-screen-time proof, follow the layout of @caprilola67 https://www.tiktok.com/@caprilola67/photo/7430596096843320608 slide 3 (a real Screen Time page as proof). Why: the captain named it as the proof reference; a fake or decorative proof breaks the trust the deck spends.
- A walking tip gets a real walking photo that shows the tip, the way @savwright https://www.tiktok.com/@savwright/photo/7658869024830328094 slide 3 sits under deck 02's walk-after-meals tip. That photo is used, so find a new one of the same kind. Why: the picture is the context a 13-year-old reads before the words.
- After a deck's faces are spent, the treadmill picture is the default gym value image. Why: more faces read as a staged shoot, and the treadmill is the one gym scene that is always organic and always on-message for steps.
- Don't put a face on a morning-walk slide: an aesthetic morning-walk photo with nobody in frame. Why: the walk is the point, and a face there would be a third face.
- Don't add another "!!" aside line to decks 02 to 05. "free steps!!!" on deck 02 slide 5 is the one for that batch. Why: a repeated aside across her decks reads as a template (write-script's aside rule).
- Don't reuse these references, which show a watch: @bodybymij slide 1, @graceelizabethlifts slide 1, @chikussa.c slide 3, @imdaisypal slides 5 and 9. Why: `H-no-watch`; each was checked at full size and dropped.
- Don't reuse a reference already used in her decks. Why: repeats look templated. Used so far (decks 02 to 05): @estefanytrevino 7584332790783560990 s2, @savwright 7658869024830328094 s3, @fitnessa5 7540071199108386103 s2, @eliepeony 7434799539892112641 s5, @haifa.munster 7469573268274416939 s1, @iliana.rdz96 7654815554305363218 s3, @dan1elleb 7347370624723012896 s2, @chikussa.c 7627223439228603679 s4 and s5, @sandy_fit7 7681145441257377038 s1, @gabrielaaadcg 7640338557193719060 s1 and s2, @vs.secretx 7546985090132348182 s3, @lift.with.em 7637215971492580630 s1 and s2, @imdaisypal 7426835869820292398 s3, @gelou.fitness 7620413720165928212 s1, @lucieengland0 7608330902782938390 s2, @gabylrei 7663840390155341069 s4.
