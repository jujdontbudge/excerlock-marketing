# you can't be lazy and lose weight

1. you can't be lazy and lose weight
2. start with changes you can keep doing >>>
3. pick high protein, high fiber foods. you don’t have to starve yourself to eat fewer calories.
4. try not to drink your calories. look at your drinks as well as your food.
5. go on walks too. make regular movement part of your routine alongside the food changes.
6. don’t crash diet. choose a calorie deficit you can actually keep up with.
