# Sections

Each folder here is one kind of marketing work and a self-contained module: delete it and nothing else breaks.

| Section | What it makes |
|---|---|
| `slideshows/` | Organic-looking TikTok photo slideshows for our own accounts |

A section may use `core/`, `accounts/`, `library/` and `research/`. It never names another section, and nothing outside it names it. To add one (AI UGC, real UGC, clipping, influencer marketing), follow `core/workflows/new-section.md`.
