# Sections

Each folder here is one kind of marketing work and a self-contained module: delete it and nothing else breaks.

| Section | What it makes |
|---|---|
| `slideshows/` | Organic-looking TikTok photo slideshows for our own accounts |
| `real-ugc/` | Current creator-recorded video assignments and editable briefs |
| `creators/` | The creator pipeline: find, vet, contact, sign, track and pay UGC creators and influencers (code + dashboard) |

A section may use `core/`, `accounts/` and `research/`. It never names another section; root navigation may link to it. To add a new kind of work, follow `core/workflows/new-section.md`.
