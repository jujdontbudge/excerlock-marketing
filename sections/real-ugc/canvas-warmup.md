# Canvas account warm-up (3 days, then post)

Every creator who posts on a new canvas account warms it up for **at least 3 days** before her first video.
- **Day 1:** normal scrolling and commenting.
- **Day 2:** searching the niche.
- **Day 3:** normal scrolling with full engagement.

The first post goes up on day 4.

**Why:** this is the captain's rule (2026-10-12). He wants new accounts to look like a real person in the niche before they post anything promotional. The research on X found no real test of 3 days against same-day posting, so the captain's call stands. The counts below are common operator practice, not proven numbers. Evidence: `raw/canvas-warmup/reports/Canvas account warmup protocol.md`.

## Day 0: set up (10 minutes, no scrolling yet)
- **Device:** her own iPhone, her own Apple ID, her normal US home Wi-Fi or cellular. **VPN off.** Never log this account in on another phone, a laptop, or a scheduler.
- **TikTok:** create it with the login details ExcerLock gives her. Leave it a **Personal** account. Business loses the music library.
- **Instagram:** create it with the same kind of login, and switch it to a professional **Creator** account. Public. Skip or hide contact info.
- **Profile, on both apps:**
  - a clear photo of her face;
  - a name and username that sound like her;
  - a one-sentence bio about her walks or the gym, e.g. "walking my way to 15k a day".
  - No link, no "link in bio", no ExcerLock name in the username.

## Day 1: normal scrolling and commenting
Two sessions, morning and evening, **15–20 minutes each**, on the For You page and Instagram Reels.
- Don't search anything yet. Just scroll like a normal user.
- Watch videos she likes **all the way through**, and swipe past ones she doesn't. Skipping is a signal too.
- **Like about 10–15 videos** per session.
- **Comment on 3–5 videos** per session, with real sentences about the video, e.g. "this route is so pretty", not "nice" or emojis. Never mention ExcerLock.
- No follows, no saves, no reposts, no posting.

## Day 2: search the niche
Two sessions, **about 20 minutes each**.
- **Search terms**, using a few per session:
  - walking, hot girl walk, 12-3-30, treadmill walk;
  - gym girl, gym routine, fittok;
  - getting my steps in, 10k steps a day, step challenge;
  - screen time, phone addiction.
- Open videos from the search results and the hashtag pages. **Watch them to the end.**
- **Like about 15–20** niche videos per session.
- **Save about 5** per session: workouts or walks she'd actually come back to.
- **Comment on 3–5** per session, again with real sentences.
- **Follow 3–5 creators**: US women who post walking or gym content. If a follow doesn't stick after a refresh, stop following for the day.
- **Instagram:** also open Settings → **Your Algorithm** and add walking and fitness.
- No posting.

## Day 3: normal scrolling, full engagement
Two sessions, **about 20 minutes each**, back on the For You page and Reels without searching. By now the feed should be mostly walking and gym content. If it isn't, do one more day 2 session before moving on.
- **Like about 20** per session.
- **Comment on 5–7** per session.
- **Follow 5–10** niche creators in total today.
- **Save about 5–8** per session.
- **Repost 2–3** niche videos in total today, using TikTok's repost button and Instagram's repost.
- Spread the actions through the session. Don't do 20 likes in a row in one minute. No follow-then-unfollow.
- No posting.

## Day 4: first post, then the daily loop
- **Before every post:** about 10 minutes of normal niche scrolling, with a few likes and comments.
- Then post **one** video, her own new file, filmed that day:
  - her walk and her stake;
  - the app locking and unlocking, with the app name said out loud;
  - 3–5 accurate hashtags;
  - the **Paid partnership** label on.
- Post the Instagram Reel the same day from the **camera-roll original**, never the downloaded TikTok with its watermark.
- One video per app per day. Every day is a new file; never repost yesterday's, never copy another creator's.

## Never, during warm-up or after
- A VPN, a second device, a scheduler or a growth app.
- Bought likes or followers.
- A link in the bio.
- Weight-loss, detox or extreme-dieting claims.
- Deleting a post because it got a few hundred views.

## When an account is dead
Wait 24 hours before judging a post.

**Keep the account** if a post gets a few hundred views (200–500 is fine) with any For You traffic. That means the hook needs changing, not the account.

**Restart a TikTok only** if 3 posts in a row each show **all three** of these signs:
- views near zero;
- about **0% For You** traffic;
- no **Add to Story** button.

Then:
1. Stop posting, appeal in the app, and wait 2–3 days.
2. If it's still dead and not banned, make one new account and **redo the full 3-day warm-up**.
3. If that one fails too, her TikTok is done for the campaign.

**Never delete an Instagram over its view count.** Check **Account Status**, fix whatever it names, and keep posting.

## Pay clock
The 15 videos per 2 weeks start on **day 4**, the first post. Warm-up days are not paid videos.
