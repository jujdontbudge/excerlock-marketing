## Workflow
1. Read the assigned goal, formats and references; product facts come from `core/product.md`, hook permissions from `core/creative-rules.md`.
2. Inspect the actual reference. Keep the assigned page budget and creator-recording requirement.
3. Fill the brief in the captain's five sections, in this order: (1) campaign objective, one sentence: "after watching this video, the viewer should believe ___"; (2) key product messaging, 2-4 benefits, not features; (3) target audience insights: age, interests, behaviors, apps they already use; (4) content requirements: platform, length, aspect ratio, branding moments; (5) creative inspiration: 3-5 links to high-performing reference videos. Why: his outline (2026-10-07, `log/events.jsonl` E1610); an agent rewrite that dropped it had to be redone (2026-10-12). Use confirmed inputs. Leave unresolved quantity, timing, hooks and terms unresolved; examples are not additional assignments.
4. Export locally with the campaign generator. Verify text and layout. Remote document updates require the explicit `--sync` option and exact-target readback.
5. Record approved sources and output in the campaign asset manifest. Outreach, publication and payment require separate authorization.

Evidence stays in `research/`; superseded assignments stay in `history/`. Commercial work is a separate request, not a prerequisite to writing filming instructions.
