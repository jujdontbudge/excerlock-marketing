# Research competitors

Ordered steps for a competitor or creative research run. Each step names the skill that does it.

1. **Scope.** Agree the target (apps, creators, keywords), region, time window, platforms and the output with the captain. State the exact list before collecting.
2. **Find who is out there.** `core/skills/web-research/SKILL.md` for app stores and sites; start from `research/competitor-universe/report.md` so you don't redo it.
3. **Collect organic posts.** `core/skills/tiktok-research/SKILL.md`.
4. **Collect paid ads.** `core/skills/ad-research/SKILL.md`.
5. **Break down the videos.** `core/skills/video-analysis/SKILL.md`, one record per video.
6. **Write the report** in `research/<topic>-<window>/report.md`: Coverage first (sources, dates, filters, tool limits), then findings labelled with their evidence rank (`research/README.md`), then the records.
7. **Index it.** Add one row to the table in `research/README.md`.

Raw downloads stay in `raw/<task>/` and are never committed.
