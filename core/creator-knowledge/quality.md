# What a good creator video looks like

For reviewing creator videos, writing feedback, and later auto-grading and editing. Source: the formats doc (captain, 2026-10-13/14) and his creator-picking criteria (2026-10-14). Add every review the captain gives under "Captain's reviews" with the video link, so the bar is learned from real examples.

## Every video
- Raw: vertical 9:16, no captions, music or filters (we add those). 10–20 seconds, except format 1: at least 20 seconds.
- Looks spontaneous, not staged: she doesn't look straight into the camera; often only part of her face is in frame, like she just started recording.
- Starts with movement or a natural action (walking, running into the room, rolling her eyes, eating a chip), not a still pose.
- The phone and the app are what the video is about; a random video that doesn't show the app doesn't count for pay.
- Expressions stay natural and small: irritation, slight surprise, a sigh. **Never a big shocked face.**

## Format 1: walking around while scrolling (3rd person)
- Phone propped in one fixed spot, one wide shot.
- She walks laps inside (kitchen island, bedroom, living room) or walks past/around the camera outside, while scrolling, like she's getting steps in so her apps stay open.
- At least 20 seconds of steady walking. At least 2 of every 4 are outside.
- No ExcerLock name on screen.
- Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@haydenn.fit/video/7692111837885664543

## Format 2: walking outside or on a treadmill
- Selfie-style, phone in hand, casual, not looking at the camera.
- Nothing staged.
- Of every 4: at least 2 on a treadmill and 2 outside.
- References: https://www.tiktok.com/@anaswalks3/video/7639913402143395102 (outside), https://www.tiktok.com/@maddy_tubbs/video/7665111909015768333 (treadmill)

## Format 3: app demo, walking and stopping
- Opens on a quick, subtle face reaction (eye roll works well).
- Then her phone screen, filmed with a second phone over her shoulder (not a screen recording): she walks and scrolls, stops (apps lock), walks again (apps open).
- **The lock on stopping and the unlock on walking must both be clearly visible.** No clear lock/unlock = re-record.
- Of every 4: one inside, one outside, one on a treadmill, and one filmed by someone else in 3rd person.
- Reference demo: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1H4Yrs-bqEwHzumXjEWRlZfXz5IlVJSk4/view (copy the walk-and-stop part; keep the reaction subtler)

## Common reasons to ask for a re-record
- Looking straight into the camera the whole time.
- Over-acted reaction or shocked face.
- Captions, music, filters or edits already added.
- Wrong aspect ratio or too long/short.
- The app or the lock/unlock moment isn't visible (format 3).
