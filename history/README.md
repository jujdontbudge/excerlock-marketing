# Historical material

Cold evidence, superseded assignments and retired experiments. Nothing here is an active instruction or discoverable skill. The current captain assignment and canonical rule files control new work.

`cleanup-manifest.json` records original/destination paths and SHA-256 values for archived files. Account-specific rejected decks remain under the relevant section/account's archive rather than entering this universal history folder.
Historical paths/quotes are retained as provenance; they do not establish current tool support, copy permission, publication status or commercial agreement.
