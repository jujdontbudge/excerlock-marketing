# The 2 slideshow formats to run first

Picked from the playbook (raw/slidesite/playbook.html) and captain-hooks.md: easiest to make, strongest evidence.

## Format 1: "Someone asked me" → answer list
5 slides. A person photo on slide 1, aesthetic photos after. CTA on slide 4.

**Why:** a question from another person is the strongest hook pattern in the sources (third-person framing). The answer list earns saves, and slide 4 lands the app before people drop off.
**Easy because:** 1 selfie plus 4 Pinterest-style images. No face-matching between slides.

| Slide | Text | Image |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "how did you get so skinny" | selfie (mirror / walking), friend's text bubble or caption |
| 2 | if you've tried everything and still can't lose fat, read this | aesthetic walk / street (works on its own) |
| 3 | tip 1, under 15 words | aesthetic |
| 4 | I've been using this app ExcerLock, it locks my apps until I hit my steps | 2 app screenshots + App Store screenshot |
| 5 | tip 3 | aesthetic |

**Hook options:** "how did you get so skinny" · "how did you lose fat so quick" · "why do you go to the gym so much" · "you've changed" · "how are you staying consistent with the gym?" · "you can't be lazy and lose weight" · "you walked HOW many steps today"

**Variants:** winter arc ("how are you losing weight when it's freezing outside") · gym ("since when do you go to the gym every day??") · girl to girl (slide 1 "girl to girl, here's what actually worked")

## Format 2: 3-slide transformation (bedrot → walking)
3 slides, same person. CTA on slide 3 as the payoff (lockscreen notification).

**Why:** 3 slides means almost everyone reaches the CTA. The viewer works out *why* she changed from the notification, which makes it shareable.
**Easy because:** 3 images and 3 short lines. Make slide 2 by editing slide 1 so it's clearly the same person.

| Slide | Text | Image |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | day 1 of my winter arc | bedrotting, phone in face, dark room |
| 2 | day 30 | same girl outside walking / mirror selfie in gym clothes |
| 3 | day 60... | lockscreen: "ExcerLock: 20,000 steps ✓ apps unlocked" + a text from a friend ("you look so different??") |

**Text options for slides 1 → 2 → 3:**
- day 1 of my winter arc → day 30 → day 60...
- bedrotting era → 30 days later → how
- 3,000 steps a day → 20,000 steps a day → what changed
- 1 day after I said "starting monday" → 6 weeks later → the reason

**Rules:** the friend's text never names the app. The notification answers the question. Leave the bottom 30% of the slide 3 image empty for TikTok text.

## Both
- Type the text in TikTok, don't bake it into the image. Lowercase. Upper or middle third of the frame.
- If a post flops, repost the same slides with a new slide-1 line before giving up on it.
