# 4 pain-funnel slideshows (hooks from captain-hooks.md, round 2)

Structure for all four: 6 slides. 1 hook → 2–3 pain → 4 app (CTA) → 5–6 payoff.
Same girl on slide 1 and on slides 5–6. The middle slides need no face.
Text is typed in TikTok, lowercase, upper/middle third. Slide 2 also works as a hook on its own.

## Image rules (all decks)
- **Slide 1:** the girl, face readable at thumbnail size, matching the hook (mirror selfie in gym fit / walking outside). Slightly smug or caught-off-guard face for the quote hooks.
- **Slides 2–3 (pain):** POV objects, no person: couch + blanket + phone at 11pm, untouched gym bag by the door, step count "2,341" on a phone, cold dark morning window. The viewer should see their own life.
- **Slide 4 (CTA):** hand holding a phone showing the ExcerLock block screen ("TikTok locked: 6,200 / 10,000 steps"), shot outside or by the gym door so it looks used, not a render. Optional second image: App Store screenshot.
- **Slides 5–6 (payoff):** the same girl again: walking with coffee in a winter coat, gym mirror, step count "25,412".
