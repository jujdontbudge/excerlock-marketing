## 2. Ideas (assistant-written, built on the playbook's patterns)

Patterns used (see raw/slidesite/playbook.html): third person + doubt/changed mind; a specific pain in the viewer's own words; a hook that trails off ("..."/":") so the swipe finishes it; signs and symptoms; secret/"illegal"; borrowed credibility (a number); POV and girl-to-girl. Lowercase on purpose: it reads like a person, not a brand.

### "..." / ":" hooks (the hook trails off, so you have to swipe to finish it)
- how fast your stomach flattens when you walk 20k a day:
- what 30 days of 20k steps did to my body...
- things that happen when you stop skipping the gym:
- the moment i stopped being lazy was when...
- my winter arc rules (i haven't broken one):
- what nobody tells you about losing fat in the winter...

### Quotation hooks (someone else says it)
- "you look different. what are you doing"
- "since when do you go to the gym every day??"
- "you walked HOW many steps today"
- "how are you losing weight when it's freezing outside"
- my mom: "you've been walking a lot lately" (me, secretly):
- "you were the laziest person i knew."

### Third person + doubt + changed mind (strongest documented pattern)
- my boyfriend said i'd quit the gym by december. it's february
- my sister bet me i couldn't hit 20k steps a day for a month
- my trainer said i didn't need a new plan, i needed this
- my roommate laughed when i started walking every night. now she comes with me

### Specific pain, in their words
- i've been "starting monday" since august
- walking 3k steps a day and wondering why i can't lose fat
- you're not lazy. your couch is just closer than the gym
- the couch guilt at 9pm when you skipped the gym again
- eating in a deficit for 2 months and the scale hasn't moved

### Winter arc
- winter arc rule #1: no scrolling until i've hit 10k
- how to hit 20k steps when it's 20 degrees outside
- how i lost 12 lbs this winter without ever missing a gym day
- your winter arc ends the first time you skip. here's how to not skip

### Signs / secret / "illegal"
- 3 signs you're not lazy, you're just stuck on your phone
- the illegal way to trick your brain into walking 20k steps
- the lazy girl way to get a flat tummy by summer
- this feels illegal to know if you're trying to lose fat

### POV / girl to girl
- pov: you found a way to never skip the gym again
- pov: your phone won't let you scroll until you've walked
- girl to girl, the gym isn't the hard part. leaving the couch is
- girl to girl, stop raw-dogging fat loss without this

### Numbers (borrowed credibility)
- 14 lbs in 30 days. i only changed one thing
- 0 days missed since october 1st
- 600,000 steps this month. here's what i actually did
- how i went from 4k to 25k steps a day

Rule from the playbook: write a second hook for slide 2 that works without slide 1.
Example: slide 1 "my boyfriend said i'd quit the gym by december" → slide 2 "if you always quit by week 3, this is why".
