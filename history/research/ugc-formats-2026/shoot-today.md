# Shoot list: film it yourself today, swap in the AI person later

Every format here comes from [captain-picks.md](captain-picks.md). All of them need only you, a phone and the app.

## Film it so the swap works later
- **Front camera, one person in frame, face big and not covered.** A small or half-hidden face comes out smeared after the swap.
- **Hold each shot steady for 2–3 seconds.** Quick whip-pans and hands across the face break the swap.
- **No watch, no logos on clothes, no captions recorded into the video.** Text gets added in the editor.
- **Your expression is what carries over.** Overdo the shocked face; it's the hook.
- **App shots and screen recordings are filmed separately** (section A). No face in them, so they never need swapping.

---

## Shot list, grouped by location

### A. App shots, filmed with a second phone (do these first)
A screen recording alone can't show that you're walking, so the walking has to be in the shot. Film your phone with a second phone. No face in these, so they never need the AI swap.
1. **POV walking:** looking down at the phone in your hand while you walk, with the sidewalk and your feet moving underneath. Open the locked app: it unlocks. Stop: the ground goes still and it locks again. Keep any step counter or "moving" status in frame.
2. **POV standing still:** try to open the app on the couch: blocked.
3. **Gym door, over the shoulder:** walk into the gym with the phone in hand and the app unlocks.
4. **Step goal:** the step count ticks over the goal and the apps unlock.

**Screen recordings** are only close-up inserts to cut in after these so the screen is readable: the unlock, the step counter, and setting up which apps get locked.

### B. At home: face clips (about 30 minutes)
| # | Format | What to film | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shocked face → app** | Close-up, stunned face looking at the phone. Do 5 versions: jaw drop, hand over mouth, slow head shake, laugh-in-disbelief, "wait what". | 2–3 s each |
| 2 | **Text + face reaction** | Same as above but a calmer "i'm cooked" / "that's literally me" face. Hook text goes on top later. | under 6 s |
| 3 | **"My boyfriend sent me this"** | Tight close-up, speechless, then turn the phone so it's seen over your shoulder. | 2 s + 3 s |
| 4 | **"-A" callout** | Lying in bed scrolling, act like a text just came in, guilty look. | 5–6 s |
| 5 | **Talking head (FaceTime style)** | Pick 3 of your topics: how to lose fat, gym consistency, gym burnout, how you got into the gym, "getting your steps isn't that hard", a realization. Talk like it's a FaceTime with a friend. Car, bedroom or kitchen. | 15–30 s each |
| 6 | **"How did you lose weight?"** | Talking head answering that question, then hold the phone up to the lens. | 10–20 s |
| 7 | **"Not gatekeeping" listicle** | Phone in hand, fast: "3 apps that…" Point at the phone for each one. The app goes in the middle. | under 30 s |
| 8 | **ASMR yapping** | Talk to camera while your hands do something satisfying (cutting fruit, mixing a protein shake, skincare). | 45–60 s |

### C. Walk outside (about 15 minutes)
| # | Format | What to film |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Walking → unlock** | Selfie-mode walking shot, face in frame. Then a shot where you stop and look annoyed. Pairs with app shot A1 (POV walking). |
| 10 | **"Day 1 testing…"** | Walking talking head: "day 1 testing an app that won't let me scroll unless I'm walking." Do it once a day for a series. |
| 11 | **POV skit: "my girl I walk with"** | Prop the phone or have a friend film from beside you, scrolling happily while walking. |

### D. Gym (about 15 minutes)
| # | Format | What to film |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | **Shocked face → gym unlock** | Walking in the gym door, shocked face at the phone. Pairs with app shot A3 (gym door). |
| 13 | **POV skit: "my gym bro finally able to scroll"** | Between sets, scrolling with a smug face. Phone propped on a bench or filmed by a friend. |
| 14 | **Before / after** | "Before" = lying on the couch scrolling (film this at home). "After" = scrolling at the gym. Same framing in both. |

## Hooks to try on these clips
Same clip, different hook text. The hook is the cheapest thing to change.
- this girl really can't scroll unless she's moving
- what if your phone locked unless you were moving
- how long has this been a thing
- girl wtf is this app
- i could literally kiss the girl at my gym who showed me this
- you're making going to the gym 10 times harder than it needs to be
- stop trying to hit 10k steps without this
- nobody told me you could just triple your steps with this

All the hooks are in [captain-picks.md](captain-picks.md).
