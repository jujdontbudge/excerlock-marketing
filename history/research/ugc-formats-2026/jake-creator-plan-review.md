## Advice extracted

The scaling article recommends: a visibly useful product and coherent purchase funnel; recent baseline views and comment-based audience screening; internally calculated fee ceilings tied to customer economics; flat fees with prompt upfront payment; short briefs with creative freedom; limited multi-creator tests with predetermined decisions; funnel diagnosis and cautious attribution; a simple accountable deal pipeline; and repeating proven creator/concept combinations. It uses 20–25 recent posts for baseline estimates and 3–5 tests for a new niche. Reach is not unique people. Content-reuse permissions must be explicit. Management time is a real cost.

The channel-fit article proposes four checks: affordable creator supply relative to brand demand, natural product integration into existing content, formats with viral upside, and audience trust. It distinguishes production UGC, paid distribution and influencer relationships. Its fitness-market assessment and strong channel-selection rules are hypotheses for us to test, not universal guarantees.

## Review of our current plan

### 1. Selection: retain the 15k routing rule, add audience-fit verification

The captain's default is influencer for median >=15k and Canvas below it. Keep this as administrative routing, not proof of conversion. Before an influencer quote, inspect comments on five recent posts, actual viewer language/market, existing formats and the reason those viewers might want ExcerLock. A fitness account is necessary under current sourcing rules but insufficient to prove purchase intent. Do not broaden recruitment to non-fitness creators merely because Jake describes a successful dancer.

For pricing, use a consistent 20–25 comparable-post sample where available, report sample gaps, and inspect TikTok and Instagram separately. Do not retrospectively change an already agreed quote. Keep the median methodology internal while disclosing actual commercial conditions.

### 2. Brief: native integration rather than automatic talking head

Use the creator's existing successful format. A walking routine, treadmill session, gym-preparation video or relatable scrolling skit may fit better than talking head. Specify the viewer problem, angle, visible app action, prohibited claims, sponsorship disclosure and deliverables. Supply selected references, not the whole library. Compare the draft with the creator's ordinary content.

Canvas still starts with the captain's reaction and POV formats. Add controlled variation: change hook or setting without changing every element simultaneously. Record which combination was assigned and produced the result.

### 3. Product demonstration: establish a clear benefit

Use one verified unlock condition per piece; do not imply the whole phone is blocked. For active walking, show selected-app restriction, movement and access, with actual supplied evidence of the mechanic. Do not promise unmeasured relocking timing or invent testimonials. Verify current demonstrable footage before assigning a demo. Product demonstration is not proof of increased steps or weight loss.

### 4. Deal economics: preserve captain choices and disclose the difference from Jake

Jake prefers flat fees without variable upside costs and 100% upfront. Our selected structure is a guaranteed view package plus capped CPM, with 25% upfront. These are different risk allocations; the articles do not endorse ours. View make-goods are the captain's chosen protection, not something Jake establishes from baseline reach.

Influencer default remains four unique videos over two weeks, each on TikTok and Instagram, plus additional posts needed to fulfil the combined organic-view guarantee. Define a finite make-good window, analytics cutoff, eligible views, treatment of the remaining balance and shortfall outcome before signing. Those items remain undecided; do not silently insert terms.

The aggregate package guarantee and per-piece cap require an explicit allocation method. Track eligible views on each piece, cap them under the agreed 300k-view ceiling, then apply the agreed package threshold without counting it twice. Do not promise an automatic $300 payout at 300k views when CPM excludes a threshold. Count make-good content explicitly in bonus eligibility. No new formula has been approved here.

Canvas volume and the new 100-view rule remain ambiguous: exact package count and whether 100 replaces the old 2,000 threshold or is just eligibility must be settled before offers. Do not revive superseded terms as confirmed.

### 5. $1,000: treat as a learning limit, not a signing target

Do not fill the budget merely to sign creators. Commit only after audience fit and deliverables are checked. Record full base liability plus maximum contractual bonuses and rights fees before signing; upfront cash is not total exposure. A $300 cap per piece can overwhelm a small campaign budget. Do not imply a shared reserve limits individual contractual promises. Additional portfolio or creator-level caps would need captain approval.

### 6. Measurement: buy learning about customers, not only cheap views

Before posts go live, record baseline installs/trials/payers, per-creator links or codes where available, posting timestamps and an observation window. Combined platform views are payable impressions, not deduplicated people. Attribution may be incomplete; do not claim causation from a spike alone.

Assess in order: ordinary-view baseline, app-interest comments, store traffic, downloads, onboarding completion, mature trial conversions and paying customers. Renewal depends on commercial signals and reliable delivery, not just whether a creator fulfilled the view guarantee. Wait for the relevant trial cohort to mature before judging paid conversion.

### 7. Test structure and renewal

Test a small number of genuinely different creator/audience/angle combinations within maximum liabilities; do not adopt Jake's 3–5 guidance if caps make that unaffordable. Record hypothesis, execution, budget, observation window and repeat/change/stop criterion. Give a weak initial piece focused feedback, then see whether execution improves. A bad integration and an unsuitable audience are different failures. No automatic renewal; repeat the winning combination when evidence warrants it.

### 8. Operations

One simple creator record: owner, status, contact, fit rationale, internal baselines, signed package, liabilities, upfront/balance, accounts, deliverables, due dates, brief, draft review, post URLs, performance, guarantee shortfall, bonuses and renewal decision. Sequence: outreach -> reply -> reviewed offer -> contract -> tracker -> assets/brief -> draft -> posting -> measurement/make-goods -> settlement -> renewal. Start manually; automate reminders and handoffs only when needed. Evaluate management burden alongside economics.

## Bottom line

Keep two-week terms, both-platform posting, short personalized briefs and controlled Canvas tests. Strengthen influencer fit checks, use native formats and measure through purchase. Do not mistake a historical median or negotiated view guarantee for guaranteed acquisition. Preserve the captain's pricing choices while documenting where they diverge from Jake. Contract shortfall rules, bonus aggregation and budget exposure still need resolution before signing.
