## Hooks from [jonnyscales — 10 UGC Hooks That Always Work](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2102879882153730442) (all 10)
1. **Listicle:** [number] + [specific things] + [desired outcome]. "3 apps every college student should know about."
2. **Mistake callout:** "you're making [specific mistake] that's causing [undesirable outcome]." "your workout routine might be the reason you're not seeing progress."
3. **Don't do this, do this:** "stop [ineffective action]. start [better alternative]." "stop guessing how many calories you're eating. try this."
4. **Contrarian:** "everyone thinks [common belief], but [alternative]." "studying longer isn't always the answer."
5. **Secret insider:** "here's the [method/tool/shortcut] that [audience] should know about." "here's a free productivity trick that takes less than a minute to set up."
6. **Transformation:** "from [undesirable before] to [desirable after]." "turn an overwhelming to-do list into a schedule you can actually follow."
7. **Bold claim:** [strong, specific statement] + immediate proof. "your phone can do more than remind you to work out."
8. **Relatable pain:** "you know when [frustrating situation]? here's [solution]." "when you've been staring at your to-do list for an hour and haven't started anything…"
9. **Question:** "what if you could [desired outcome] without [painful process]?"
10. **Curiosity gap:** [intriguing statement] + missing info. "i wish someone had shown me this before…" / "there's a much easier way to … watch this."

## Hooks and formats from [mikey_starts — The Last Hooks Newsletter](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2103160089918193952) (all 5)
1. **ASMR yapping.** Talking head for 45–60 seconds while the hands do something satisfying (slime, skincare, cutting fruit), a 5–10 second screen recording, then back. Hooks: "This is your friendly reminder that…", "3 resources you NEED for…", "How to survive…", "Things I wish I knew before…"
2. **Testing the method.** Face to camera with the premise, then the screen recording, then the real result. Hook: "Day [X] testing [X] so you don't have to…"
3. **"Deleting this in 24 hours."** Urgent face to camera, then a screen walkthrough. Hook: "Have been keeping this a secret. Deleting this in 24 hours…"
4. **Unhinged AI tutor.** Give a wrong answer and the AI roasts you. Jump cuts between a deadpan reaction and the app yelling, with bold subtitles.
5. **"I emailed everyone."** An absurd large-scale experiment (emailed 15M people for $1), shown on a green-screen flowchart, then the tool behind it. Hook: a ridiculous "POV / what if" plus a huge number.

## Formats and hooks from [mikey_starts — FYP Leak #11](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2104672704271974904) (#3, #4, #5 only)
3. **"Xoxo, -A."** A 5–6 second clip of any everyday moment (in bed, makeup, studying). They act like a text just came in, and the on-screen text is a savage callout signed "-A" (Pretty Little Liars). Hook: a brutally honest callout that makes the viewer feel seen or attacked.
4. **"My boyfriend sent me this."** A tight close-up of a stunned face + the text "my boyfriend sent me this…", a 2-second reaction, then over the shoulder onto the phone screen. The same creator posted it 3 times and all 3 hit.
5. **"This guy is not gatekeeping."** A fast 3-item listicle to camera, holding the phone up, quick cuts to screen recordings. The featured app sits between two ordinary ones so it feels like a genuine hack. Hook: phone in hand + bold text teasing gatekept apps.
