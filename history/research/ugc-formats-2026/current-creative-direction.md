# Current UGC creative direction

The captain's supplied direction: videos for girls who want to lose fat, get a flat stomach, hit a body goal, walk more, attend the gym more, stay consistent, stop putting off exercise, or lower screen time.

Use three simple format guides: talking head, reaction and POV. No skits. Their count describes the formats, not an agreed production quota. The captain's definitions below override generic format examples and the earlier six-card interpretation.

## What each format means

- **POV:** a first-person app demonstration showing selected apps becoming available when the creator reaches the gym **or** starts treadmill walking. Use one of these conditions per video. The phone, real app behavior and qualifying action are central; do not substitute a step-goal video, generic workout montage or selfie introduction.
- **Reaction:** the creator reacts to their own walking and stopping while selected apps unlock and lock. Show the real walk → access → stop → restriction cycle and the creator's reaction to it, not an unrelated surprised-face clip followed by a generic demo. Use the actual recorded transitions without promising instant detection or an exact relock delay.
- **Talking head:** explain how to use the phone urge to follow through on gym goals, or give three practical tips with ExcerLock as one of the tips. These are options within one format guide, not extra concepts or a contracted quota. Keep natural delivery and the captain's exact approved hook; do not invent neuroscience or personal results.

Desired understanding: an app can block selected apps unless the user meets an exercise condition. Linking access to walking, treadmill use, gym attendance or a step goal gives the urge to use the phone a productive outlet and a reason to repeat the exercise over time.

Desired feeling: discovery, relief and possibility. The viewer should feel they found a hidden hack or missing tool that could help with their own fitness problem. Demonstrate the mechanism behind that feeling; do not turn the metaphor into promised fat loss, a guaranteed flat stomach, instant motivation, or a measured habit effect that has not been established.

Hook permissions and product facts remain in `core/creative-rules.md` and `core/product.md`. Select the captain's existing hooks verbatim. Do not write replacements.
