# X accounts: UGC video hooks and formats

Captured 2026-10-02. Follower counts are from `https://api.fxtwitter.com/<handle>` that day. View counts and revenue figures are what the account posted. They are not independently audited.

Lane: UGC video (talking-head, skits, green-screen, phone demos, creator-style ads and organic videos). Accounts that also teach slideshows are tagged **both**. Slideshow-only teachers were left for the other pass.

## Coverage

**Queries.** X keyword search (Latest), one semantic search, web search, and profile checks. Terms included `UGC hooks`, `UGC formats`, `viral UGC format`, `consumer app` plus `UGC` / `talking head` / `hook and demo` / `skit`, `hidden product demo`, `green screen reaction`, `steal this format`, `tech ugc`, `ugc engineer`, and `from:` on people who kept showing up (`jonnyscales`, `alexolim_`, `juliapintar`, `themariaines`, `wesocialgrowth`, `mikey_starts`, `jaxxdwyer`, `maubaron`, `johnvirality`, `fuckgrowth`, `pixclipper`, `simonecanciello`, `jacobrodri_`, `chasebfisher`, `Nick_5anchez`, `sophia_blairr`, `Jibran_05`, `drewlevinn`, `lightreelai`). Web queries covered "UGC hooks" account roundups and Lightreel / Jibran. One article was opened: [“Me on a random day” hook](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/me-on-a-random-day-hook-pulls-10m-views) (Social Growth Engineers, 2026-09-09).

**Funnel.** About 70 accounts were opened far enough to judge. 48 handles were sent to fxtwitter (some 404s on the first pass succeeded on a retry). **25 kept.** The rest are in `raw/x-hook-accounts/ugc-candidates.json` with a reason.

**Limits.**

- Following lists, X lists, and "who to follow" did not load logged out. Network mapping is from quotes, replies, and repeated names in search, not from a following graph.
- Search returns a slice of Latest/Top posts, not every tweet from an account.
- `JosephKChoi` and `alexishedley_` returned posts in search, then fxtwitter said user not found. They are not in the table.
- `jaxxdwyer` and `Jibran_05` were still posting about UGC inside the window. Their fullest format breakdowns are a few weeks older than 60 days. Dates are on the row.
- Three people with real breakdowns are under 1,000 followers and were cut: [@hollyfrick_](https://x.com/hollyfrick_) (436), [@EvoBradley](https://x.com/EvoBradley) (882), [@sixugc](https://x.com/sixugc) (794).
- Public conversation clusters around Playkit (`juliapintar`, `Nick_5anchez`) and Affiliate Network (`roman_khaves`, `fuckgrowth`, `johnvirality`, `danclipping`, `pixclipper`). That is where the examples are being posted, and it means the list is thicker there than across the rest of X.
- No logins, follows, likes, or posts.

## Ten hooks and formats that kept showing up

These are the patterns named, with a quote, on the accounts above. ExcerLock can drop a walk, a step goal, or a blocked app into the same shape.

1. **Late discovery.** The creator sounds annoyed they only just found it. "4 years at the gym and NOW I find this?!!!??" ([Colin Madden, 2026-09-08](https://x.com/ColinMaddenUGC/status/2097280776421126601)). Same family: "HOW LONG has THIS been a thing?!" ([Jonny, 2026-09-17](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100584898704629871)), "when did this come out??" ([Penelope Lopez, 2026-09-23](https://x.com/KCodes7777/status/2102766000294838653)), "bruh i've been shopping at aldi all my life and now i find this?!" ([pixclipper, 2026-08-19](https://x.com/pixclipper/status/2090016988223430845)).

2. **Hidden product demo.** The viewer watches someone use the app, and that use is the video. "the app demo IS the content" and "don't make UGC that explains your app" ([Jonny, 2026-09-17](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100584898704629871)). Jax calls hidden product demos "by far the most converting format" ([2026-07-21](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2079537655919112468)).

3. **Stacked open loops in one video.** Question hook, then a green-screen reaction, then "this is something they don't tell you," then a comment word. Jonny's SAT-tool breakdown, [2026-09-30](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2105295290244952435). The comment word in that example was "cooks."

4. **Pain first, app late.** A complaint the viewer already has, examples, then the app as the obvious fix. "This is NOT an okay reason to cancel plans" pulled 3.5M Instagram views and 1.4M TikTok views in his write-up ([Jonny, 2026-10-02](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2106005426198163911)). He maps the same shape onto fitness: "signs your 'healthy' diet is making fat loss harder."

5. **Skit where the app is the weapon, not the subject.** Classroom or workplace drama, product in a small slice of the runtime. Dan's AnswersAi read: overlay text "No AI??" then "half the class is using it," 45.5M views ([2026-09-11](https://x.com/danclipping/status/2098407309315617121)). Alex Olim describes the same idea as a workplace skit where the app is on screen for under a quarter of the video ([2026-05-02](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2050602294064845175)).

6. **Snapchat text, one second of face, then the screen.** "WOW, WHO MADE THIS SUPER APP" on a 5.6M-view clip: giant glasses, one second of the person, fifteen seconds of the app ([Penelope Lopez, 2026-09-26](https://x.com/KCodes7777/status/2103942962128318871)). Pixclipper's Aldi line is the same skeleton.

7. **Break up with an app people already trust.** "Breaking up with Pinterest because this new app is so much better," cited at 9M views, with the line rewritten for Duolingo, Notion, and MyFitnessPal ([Penelope Lopez, 2026-09-21](https://x.com/KCodes7777/status/2102154375288529006)).

8. **Short reaction instead of a tour.** PosePal "went from 3K views to 8.8M in one video after switching from talking formats to short reaction clips" ([Maria Inês, 2026-10-02](https://x.com/themariaines/status/2106017633804120302)).

9. **"Me on a random day…"** Ordinary day, then a decision. Social Growth Engineers quotes "Me on a random day in August…" and lists 6.2M, 4.7M, and 1.6M view cuts ([post](https://x.com/wesocialgrowth/status/2106018816669126879), [page](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/me-on-a-random-day-hook-pulls-10m-views)). They say it moves to "the day you finally start studying" or the day you fix a habit.

10. **Five beats once something wins: tension, early reveal, process, proof, verdict.** Alex Olim says that order, not a word-for-word copy, is what they rebuilt a creator pod around after one 1.5M-view outlier ([2026-09-30](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2105344839646589429)). Chase Fisher's commerce version is the same idea from the sales side: lock the hook, then only test "hook, pain point, product, soft CTA" ([2026-09-24](https://x.com/chasebfisher/status/2103199619781890184)).

## Gaps

The largest accounts in this lane are operators and newsletter feeds (Jacob Rodri, Jason Zhou, Simone Canciello, Mau Baron, Jake Castillo, Julia Pintar, Maria Inês, Alex Olim, Jibran, Vlad). Jonny Scales, the account in the brief, is number 24 by followers and posts the most literal beat sheets.

Missing on purpose: slideshow-only teachers; anyone under 1,000 followers, including Holly Frick, Brad at Evo UGC, and Joana at Affiliate Network, who were posting real format notes this month; course posts that hide the example behind a comment word; and product accounts that only book demos (SideShift's Nick Lawton and Drew Levin, Lightreel's brand account). Following graphs and X lists were not readable logged out, so people those accounts follow in private are not here. Kallaway-style short-form teachers did not turn up under that name on X in this pass.
