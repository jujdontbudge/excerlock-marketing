# X accounts: slideshow hooks and formats

Captured 2026-10-02. Lane is TikTok and Instagram photo-mode slideshows. Follower counts are from `https://api.fxtwitter.com/<handle>` on that date. View counts, download counts and revenue in the posts are the authors' claims. They were not checked against TikTok or Sensor Tower.

A row is here only if the account had at least 1,000 followers, a public post on this topic since about 2026-08-03, and a concrete example: a screenshot, a quoted hook, a view count or a slide-by-slide breakdown. Handles were opened through X search or fxtwitter. Nothing was posted, liked or followed.

## Coverage

**Queries.** X latest and top search for `slideshow hooks`, `tiktok slideshow`, `photo mode`, `slideshow format`, `best slideshow`, `carousel` plus `hook` and `app`, and the same phrases with `min_faves` from 15 to 80, mostly `since:2026-08-01`. Named-account searches for the three examples in the brief (`shortformsavage`, `justddev`, `jonnyscales`) and for authors already in `research/slideshow-articles.md` (`pounddz`, `sulfurscales`, `PerezHatesAI`). Web search for slideshow marketing threads and for "best twitter accounts" lists. fxtwitter profile reads for every handle below and for the rejects.

**What that returned.** 68 accounts were opened or judged from those results. 29 are in the table. 39 are in `raw/x-hook-accounts/slideshow-candidates.json` with a reject reason. A few of the rejects were search noise (fan posts, old news) and were not given a follower check.

**Limits.**

- Logged-out X does not show who an account follows, so "who they follow" and X lists were not covered. Discovery is keyword search, quote chains and web results.
- X search returns a short latest/top slice, not every match. Quiet accounts that do not use the word "slideshow" are missing.
- The first fxtwitter pass returned HTTP 404 for some real handles. Those were retried and then resolved. Counts below are from the successful retry.
- English-language search. A few French, Spanish and Portuguese accounts appeared only when the post also used "slideshow".
- Large app accounts such as [@jackfriks](https://x.com/jackfriks) (174,761) showed up in name search and were not kept: no recent slideshow-hook post was loaded for them.
- Two detailed slideshow playbooks, [@tatealax](https://x.com/tatealax) (3 May 2026) and [@adriamatz](https://x.com/adriamatz) (27 May 2026), are outside the 60-day window. Adrià still replies about hooks; the format article itself is older.
- UGC-only teachers were left for the other lane. [@jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales) (1,240, the brief's UGC example) had no slideshow post in this search.

## Formats that showed up more than once

These are patterns in the posts above. View and money figures stay the authors' claims.

1. **Specific pain on slide 1, product later.** "three months in the gym and my lower back is worse than when I started" ([@aleksascales](https://x.com/aleksascales/status/2093696427486523865)). Same order in [@ceonadz](https://x.com/ceonadz/article/2095490126948544600) and in [@sulfurscales](https://x.com/sulfurscales/status/2090128553082036542) (swap only the ad slide).
2. **The lifestyle list, with the app as one item.** "winter arc… plug your app into one of the slides" ([@justddev](https://x.com/justddev/status/2104945599590093265)). [@SDDFounder](https://x.com/SDDFounder/status/2092183047584047575) does it as a day-in-the-life routine.
3. **Known options, then yours.** "Apps every girl needs on her phone" ([@KCodes7777](https://x.com/KCodes7777/status/2104915988835647848)). [@adamtwtz](https://x.com/adamtwtz/status/2097925109155549689) uses the ranked version: "Top 5 gym apps (worst to best)".
4. **Numbered promise, then a soft app line.** "5 uncomfortable laws…" then numbered tips, then the app ([@_mattwelter](https://x.com/_mattwelter/status/2095966393053380778)). [@ceonadz](https://x.com/ceonadz/article/2095490126948544600) lists the same opener as "7 things that fixed my gut".
5. **Open loop on a person.** "I told my therapist X... and her answer made me cry for hours" ([@oganicboss](https://x.com/oganicboss/status/2104977747147723203)). [@ceonadz](https://x.com/ceonadz/article/2095490126948544600) names the skeleton: another person, doubt, they changed their mind.
6. **Text like a message, no hard CTA.** "Snapchat hook feels super personal" and the app name already on screen ([@GeorgeLampro20](https://x.com/GeorgeLampro20/status/2103218347995545931)). [@shortformsavage](https://x.com/shortformsavage/status/2105988667118612765): "write like you'd text a friend".
7. **Slide 2 has to work alone.** "if you've been eating clean for 3 months and still not seeing results" as slide 1, and a second hook on slide 2 because TikTok can re-serve from there ([@adriansolarzz](https://x.com/adriansolarzz/status/2101421282084487223)).
8. **Saved lists.** His five names: Hear Me Out, Non-Negotiables, Green flags, Ranked list, Before and after (same Adrian post). Dark-car POV plus a list is the version [@rsalimx](https://x.com/rsalimx/status/2104413987571917119) says pulls millions in self-improvement.
9. **Emotional setup, then a notification.** "AI image / emotional setup > personal story > text-message > notification CTA" ([@themariaines](https://x.com/themariaines/status/2103526216594846077)). [@jasonugc](https://x.com/jasonugc/status/2098458680207315156) is the cousin: relatable hook, app a few slides later as the payoff.
10. **Casual image, reason to swipe.** "nonchalant & casual images" and "give reader a reason to scroll" ([@shortformsavage](https://x.com/shortformsavage/status/2105988667118612765)). [@LinoLeighton](https://x.com/LinoLeighton/status/2102453470045327553) puts that reason in small type: "Swipe right →".

Largest ten in this list: [@ErnestoSOFTWARE](https://x.com/ErnestoSOFTWARE) (62,282, both), [@chesny](https://x.com/chesny) (36,675), [@_mattwelter](https://x.com/_mattwelter) (34,363), [@eliana_jordan](https://x.com/eliana_jordan) (32,918), [@jessibuilds](https://x.com/jessibuilds) (31,077, both), [@themariaines](https://x.com/themariaines) (15,266), [@sulfurscales](https://x.com/sulfurscales) (15,230), [@pounddz](https://x.com/pounddz) (13,311), [@GeorgeLampro20](https://x.com/GeorgeLampro20) (12,395), [@onlinedopamine](https://x.com/onlinedopamine) (10,337).

The accounts that post a fresh set of slideshow examples every week are [@onlinedopamine](https://x.com/onlinedopamine) and [@consumerxai](https://x.com/consumerxai). The accounts that stay on one skeleton and show it on a named app are [@_mattwelter](https://x.com/_mattwelter), [@adamtwtz](https://x.com/adamtwtz) and [@yassratti](https://x.com/yassratti).

Gaps: following lists and X lists were not visible logged out; search missed people who teach the same thing without the word "slideshow"; two strong May 2026 playbooks (tatealax, adriamatz) are outside the 60-day cut; UGC-only accounts were not collected here.
