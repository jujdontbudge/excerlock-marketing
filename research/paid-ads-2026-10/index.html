Meta and TikTok ads, explained

A plain-English map of how paid social ads work, and how subscription apps use them. Synthesised from ten research lanes run on 16 October 2026, each reading 25 to 40 sources: platform documentation, data reports, respected practitioners, YouTube teachers and founders on X.

Every claim is tagged by how much to trust it.

Official what Meta, TikTok or Apple document Data a measured report, with its sample Practitioner what a known operator teaches or observed Claim self-reported results, not verified

Ads advice goes stale fast. Dates are kept on every number. This page explains; it does not recommend what to do.

The chain from ad to paying user

Every paid user is the end of a chain. Each step has its own rate, and the cost of a paying user is the ad spend divided through all of them. A problem anywhere in the chain looks like "the ads don't work".

Worked example (invented inputs, only to show the arithmetic; from the Meta lane)

$20
CPM: cost per 1,000 views
$2.00
per click at 1% CTR
$6.67
per install at 30% click→install
$33
per trial at 20% install→trial
$83
per paying user at 40% trial→paid

Doubling CTR halves every later cost only if all the later rates stay the same, which they often don't: a different ad attracts different people.

MetricFormulaWhat it tells you
CPMspend ÷ impressions × 1,000What attention cost. Cheap CPM is not proof of a good ad.

Hold ratee.g. 15-second plays ÷ 3-second playsDid people keep watching.
CTRlink clicks ÷ impressionsDid the ad make people want to act.

Cost per trialspend ÷ trial startsCost of a trial. Still not revenue.
Paid CACspend ÷ new paying usersCost of a customer. The number that has to be lower than what a customer is worth.
ROASattributed revenue ÷ spendRevenue credited so far. Not profit, and not proof the ad caused it.

The auction

Every time someone scrolls, Meta and TikTok run an auction for that slot. The highest bid does not simply win. Official Meta names three inputs: your bid, the estimated action rate (how likely this person is to do the thing you asked for), and ad quality.

total value = bid × estimated action rate + ad quality

So a more relevant ad can beat a bigger bid, and a better ad effectively makes every impression cheaper. TikTok describes the same idea: bid plus relevance.

Official Practitioner Andromeda made Meta much better at matching many different ads to many different people. It is often blamed for every drop in results; Jon Loomer argues against that, and Meta's engineering posts don't support claims like "you must post a fixed number of ads a week".

Sources: Meta auction · Andromeda, 2024-12-02 · Loomer, 2025-12-03 · TikTok auction ads

Campaign, ad set, ad

Both platforms use three levels. Each level holds a different kind of decision.

CBO vs ABO

Official CBO (campaign budget) moves one budget between ad sets toward results, unequally on purpose. ABO gives each ad set its own budget. On Meta, ABO can still share up to 20% between ad sets unless sharing is off.

Advantage+ and Smart+

Official The automated campaign types. They pick audiences, placements and bids for you. You still supply the app, the event, the budget and the ads. "Advantage+ worked" means nothing without saying which event, controls and measurement.

Consolidate

Official Practitioner Both platforms warn that many small ad sets split the learning signal. Practitioners add a separate campaign or ad set only for a real business reason: a different event, country, budget commitment or a controlled test.

Daily budget isn't a cap

Official Meta can spend up to 75% over a daily budget on a good day, and up to 7× the daily budget over a full week. $100/day can be $175 one day.

Sources: Meta campaign budget · budget sharing · daily budgets · Advantage+ app campaigns · TikTok Smart+ App · TikTok CBO

What you ask the machine for

The optimisation event is an instruction: "find me people who will do this." Installers, trial starters and buyers overlap, but they are not the same people. A cheap installer is often not a subscriber.

Official Practitioner The common teaching: optimise for the deepest event that is tracked correctly and happens often enough. Cal AI optimised for trial starts and modelled trial→paid. Chart Detector AI (hard paywall, no trial) optimised for purchases. On TikTok, value optimisation (VBO) is Android-only inside Smart+ App as of August 2026.

Sources: TikTok iOS optimisation · TikTok app VBO · Cal AI interview, 2026-03-02

Learning and budgets

After launch or a big edit, the system explores who responds. Results are noisier and usually pricier in this "learning phase". It settles once it has seen enough conversions.

PlatformRough settling pointBudget floors and guidance
MetaOfficial about 50 results per ad set in the week after the last significant editGoal-based bidding works best with 50–100+ weekly conversions
TikTokOfficial volatility drops after about 25 results or 7 days; Smart+ docs say up to 50 events in 7 daysCampaign budget must exceed $50/day, ad group $20/day. Guidance: 20× CPI for install campaigns, or $200 with no history; Smart+ 10× historical CPA daily

The arithmetic that follows from "about 50 a week": the cost of each conversion sets the budget needed to learn.

Weekly spend for 50 conversions = 50 × cost per conversion. Hypothetical costs.

installs at $3$150
trials at $10$500
trials at $20$1,000
payers at $80$4,000

This is why many apps optimise for trials rather than purchases at first: the deeper the event, the more money it takes to give the machine enough examples.

Official Edits that restart learning on Meta: targeting, creative, optimisation event, adding an ad, bid strategy, a pause of 7+ days. Big budget changes too. Learning is a delivery diagnostic, not a profit certificate: "learning complete" doesn't mean it's working, and "learning limited" doesn't mean it isn't.

Sources: Meta learning phase · significant edits · bid strategies · TikTok learning phase · TikTok budget

Creative is the targeting

With broad audiences, the ad itself decides who it finds. A video about missing the gym finds people who miss the gym. This is why practitioners now spend most of their effort on creative, not on audience settings.

Diversify reasons, not pixels

Official Meta (Dec 2025): changing a CTA on the same visual is iteration; different customer situations, messages, visuals and formats are diversification. Both are useful; only diversification finds new people.

Say it in 3 seconds

Official TikTok: 9:16, at least 720p, introduce the proposition in the first 3 seconds, hook within 6. People, sound, a DIY feel, and keep text out of the UI "safe zones".

Native ≠ bad

Practitioner Barry Hott: "native" means it looks like what that audience already watches. Polished can be native; expensive-looking ads can trigger instant skipping.

Fatigue is a diagnosis

Practitioner Foxwell says keep a steady creative pipeline. Loomer says "fatigue" is often something else, like running out of easy buyers. Neither gives a fixed expiry date. Rising costs can also be auction prices or a broken step later in the funnel.

Sources: Meta creative diversification, 2025-12-16 · TikTok creative best practices · Barry Hott · Foxwell · Loomer on fatigue

Testing

Putting five ads in one ad set is not a fair test: the system pours money into whichever looks best early. An ad that got little spend is "unknown", not "lost".

MethodWhat it answersMechanics
Normal deliveryCan this set of ads find customers at all?Spend goes unequally, by design
Meta creative testDoes this new concept get a fair shot?Official 2–7 ads, ≤20% of budget suggested, results have no confidence level
Meta A/B testIs strategy A better than B?Official non-overlapping audiences, equal budgets
TikTok split testSame, on TikTokOfficial separate audiences; picks a winner at 90% confidence

Practitioner The famous debate (Faris vs Bachman, 2024): let the system allocate against cost controls, or pay for separate testing campaigns to learn faster. Both sides agree separate testing can waste money for inexperienced teams and pays off more at larger scale. Among app founders, a common shape is a testing campaign (ABO, a few concepts) and a scaling campaign (CBO) that receives the winners.

Sources: Meta creative testing · Meta A/B testing · TikTok split testing · Faris vs Bachman

Targeting and its limits

Broad is the default now

Official Practitioner Both platforms recommend few restrictions so delivery can explore. Interest and lookalike splits overlap heavily once the platform expands audiences anyway.

Suggestions vs hard limits

Official Meta's Advantage+ app campaigns list gender, interests, behaviours and lookalikes as unsupported. Country, language, OS and a minimum age are real controls. "Women 18–30" can describe who the ad talks to, but isn't enforced there.

Teens are different

Official Meta removed gender and interest targeting for under-18s and bans weight-loss ads to them. TikTok restricts detailed targeting of minors and allows weight-loss and muscle-gain claims to adults only, with no body shaming or guaranteed transformations.

iOS-only is about compatibility

Official An iOS-only app runs iOS app campaigns. TikTok's iOS Smart+ and Android Smart+ have different features; tutorials for one often don't apply to the other.

Sources: Advantage+ app campaigns · Meta teen ads · TikTok broad targeting · TikTok weight policy · TikTok under-18

iOS measurement

Since iOS 14.5, apps can't see who came from which ad unless the user allows tracking (ATT). Platforms get partial, delayed, privacy-safe signals instead. This is the single biggest difference between app ads and website ads.

ATT

Apple's permission prompt for cross-app tracking. Separate from Screen Time, Health or subscription permissions.

SKAN / AdAttributionKit

Apple's privacy-safe attribution. Sends delayed, coarse "postbacks". AAK builds on SKAN (iOS 18+ adds re-engagement).

Meta AEM

Meta's own aggregated measurement. Can run alongside SKAN on eligible campaigns.

MMP

AppsFlyer, Adjust, Singular: combine costs and attribution across networks. Not required to start; useful once several networks run.

Apple's SKAN timeline Official Conversion windows after first launch, then a random delay before the network hears anything.

TikTok real-time iOS

Official Since Aug 2026, eligible apps get real-time iOS reporting through major MMPs or TikTok's SDK 1.5.0+. TikTok suggests ~90 daily installs per SKAN AEO campaign to reduce withheld data.

Double counting

Official RevenueCat sends StartTrial and Subscribe (including renewals) to Meta. If the Meta SDK also sends purchases, they can double count. Meta and TikTok can both claim the same customer: never add their numbers together.

Sources: Apple ad attribution · Apple conversion windows · AppsFlyer AEM · TikTok iOS real-time · RevenueCat Meta

After the click: store, onboarding, paywall

A great ad sends people into the rest of the funnel. Practitioners repeatedly say the paywall can move customer cost as much as the ad does.

Custom Product Pages

Official Up to 70 App Store pages, each with its own screenshots and video, linked from a specific ad so the store continues that ad's promise. Apple reports an average +2.5 points conversion over a 1.6% default page (its own average, not a controlled test).

Product Page Optimization

Official Apple's built-in A/B test of icon, screenshots and preview: up to 3 variants, one test at a time, up to 90 days, 90% confidence suggested.

App preview videos

Official Up to 3 per language, 30 seconds each, autoplay muted. Must be on-device footage, not hands holding a phone.

Web-to-app funnels

Sending ads to a website (quiz, web paywall) instead of the App Store avoids Apple's commission and gives better tracking, but adds friction. Data RevenueCat's Dipsea experiment (~3,100 US users per arm): in-app purchase only converted 27.0% initially vs 18.1% web-only; paid 6.3% vs 5.3%; take-home $2.09 vs $1.96 per user despite Apple's 30% fee. Official Since May 2025, US apps may link to external purchase. Apple's Small Business Program cuts the fee to 15% for eligible enrolled developers under $1M; subscriptions pay 70% in year one and 85% after.

Sources: Apple CPP · Apple PPO · App previews · RevenueCat 2026 · Superwall · Dipsea test · Small Business Program

Unit economics

The whole game is one inequality: what a paying user costs must be less than what they bring in, within a time you can afford to wait.

cost per payer = CPI ÷ (install→trial × trial→paid)

Assumed inputs, from the app-ads lane. The $5 CPI row beats the $2 one: a pricier install can be a cheaper customer.

LTV and payback

Lifetime value is net money per user (after Apple's cut, refunds) over time. Payback is how long until a user has repaid their cost. A good long-term LTV can still run out of cash short-term.

Cohorts

Group users by the week they were acquired and compare groups at the same age. A two-week-old cohort can't be judged on renewals yet.

Two clocks

The attribution window (how long after an ad a conversion is credited) and the ROAS window (how long you wait to count revenue) are different things.

Trials aren't revenue

A trial start is an early signal. With a 7-day trial, the real answer arrives a week or more after the spend.

Measuring and scaling

Attribution asks who gets credit. Incrementality asks what would have happened without the ad. They can disagree a lot.

Platforms over-credit

Data Fubo (AppsFlyer case): one well-attributed campaign showed no incremental lift and was cut, freeing six figures a month. Sleep Cycle (Adjust): up to 50% of credited installs would have come organically.

Blended CAC

Total spend ÷ all new payers. A useful reality check on the business, though it can't say which ad caused what.

Vertical vs horizontal

Vertical: raise the budget on what works. Horizontal: new concepts, new countries, new platforms. The next dollar usually buys a pricier customer than the average so far.

Budget-change rules

Official TikTok's own docs disagree (July: ≤40% in learning, ≤30% after, 2 days apart; August: within 50%). Claim App founders commonly cite +20% every 3 days while LTV beats CAC.

When to kill an ad. A statistics rule of thumb from the scaling lane: if an ad spends 3× your target cost per conversion with zero conversions, that happens by bad luck only about 5% of the time when the ad is truly on target. That's grounds for a review, but delayed attribution breaks it if the spend is too recent.

Countries

A country isn't just a different media price. It's a different customer: different willingness to pay, language, App Store price and trial behaviour.

Cheap geos screen, they don't prove

Practitioner Creatives can be screened cheaply in Latin America or Southeast Asia, but a US winner still has to win in the US. Buying cheap events to "exit learning" doesn't transfer economics.

Country ≠ language

Official They're separate targeting settings. TikTok's account registration country can limit which countries you're allowed to target.

Price per country

Claim Mojo raised annual prices 50% in the US and Germany and lowered them in Brazil, reporting 60% ARPU growth. Currency conversion isn't price optimisation.

States and cities

Official US state and city targeting exists on both platforms. No evidence was found that it's a better place to start.

Sources: Mojo pricing · TikTok learning · full lane: lanes/app-geo-targeting.md

Hands-and-phone demo

One sec, Cal AI, Zaad: show the one action and its result. No face needed. Makes an unfamiliar rule concrete.

Talking head + demo

A person gives the frustration; the screen gives the proof. Data TikTok's Headway case: adding green-screen commentary lowered subscription CPA.

Skit / POV

Ladder's gym chat, WeWard's coffee: a small story where the app resolves the conflict.

Green screen

Creator in front of the app screen or a post. Data LC Waikiki on TikTok: early in-app UI 37% lower CPI; green screen 16% lower CPI.

Static and carousel

Cheap to test whether the promise is understood. Ladder runs a 5-card quote carousel. Official TikTok carousel ads now support App Promotion (Sep 2026).

Founder ad

One sec runs a 41-second "why I built this" with the intervention screens shown during the story. Earns its place by explaining an unusual product decision.

Practitioner Savannah Sanchez's point for apps: the product is an action on a screen. Relatable situations earn attention; real app screens explain the mechanism. Claim Alex Vech (Boost App Social) found one winner in a batch of 85 ads. Organic virality and paid performance are different tests: a slideshow with millions of views can convert poorly as an ad.

Competitor note. Claim WeWard announced on 8 July 2026 an optional iOS mode that blocks chosen apps until a step goal is hit. Its ads seen on 16 Oct still sell rewards, not this mode. On TikTok's ad library, searches for these competitors returned no ads clearly from them in the last 30 days (searches can miss alternate payer names). WeWard walking mode

Myths and what changed, 2024–2026

You'll hearWhat the evidence says
"Andromeda changed everything last month"Published Dec 2024; ranking upgrades followed in 2025 and 2026. It's not a universal explanation for any one account's drop.
"You must post X new ads a week"No platform requirement. Meta asks for genuinely different ads and also warns against too many ads splitting the signal.
"Interest targeting is how you reach your audience"Meta's Advantage+ app campaigns don't support interests or gender at all; both platforms now default to broad.
"A daily budget is a hard cap"Meta can spend 75% over on a given day.
"Run engagement ads to exit learning"Old advice. It teaches the system to find engagers, not buyers.
"TikTok iOS only reports through SKAN, days late"Since Aug 2026 eligible apps get real-time iOS reporting through MMPs or TikTok's SDK.
"Creative dies after N days"No source supports a fixed expiry. Fatigue shows up as worsening customer cost on comparable cohorts.
"Web checkout saves 30%, so it makes more money"Dipsea's test: in-app purchase earned more per user despite the fee.
"Long-running competitor ads are proven winners"They show the advertiser kept paying, not that it's profitable.
"Meta click attribution works the same as always"March 2026: for website and in-store conversions, click-through now means link clicks only.
