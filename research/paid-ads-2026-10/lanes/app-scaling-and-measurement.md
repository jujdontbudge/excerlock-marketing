# App scaling and measurement: knowing when paid growth is working

Research captured **2026-10-16**. Lane: `app-scaling-and-measurement`.

The decision is not “Which ad has the best ROAS today?” It is “Will the next money we spend bring enough additional contribution, soon enough, to justify the risk?” Start with collected subscription revenue and mature acquisition cohorts, use platform attribution to diagnose delivery, and treat incrementality as a separate question.

## The 20 things to understand

### 1. Define the unit before comparing LTV with CAC

Lifetime value means money earned from a customer over time. But “customer” may mean every person who opens the app, or only someone who pays. RevenueCat has separate realized LTV charts for those denominators. A trial-only user is not a payer. Compare revenue per acquired user with acquisition cost per user, or revenue per payer with cost per payer; mixing them produces meaningless profitability. **Official product definitions:** [per customer](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/realized-ltv-per-customer-chart), [per paying customer](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/realized-ltv-per-paying-customer-chart), read 2026-10-16.

**Judgment:** label every metric explicitly: `paid-media CPI`, `paid-media CAC per first payer`, `D30 contribution per new user`, `D30 contribution per first payer`. Define whether refunds remove a payer from the count; retain both original and refunded payer counts so a changing denominator cannot hide bad acquisition.

### 2. Gross subscription revenue is not the money available to buy ads

RevenueCat's standard realized LTV subtracts refunds but is before store commissions and taxes. Its Revenue chart separately offers estimated proceeds after those deductions. Proceeds estimates are also not the same as the cash already deposited by Apple. **Official:** [LTV definition](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/realized-ltv-per-customer-chart), [Revenue and Proceeds](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/revenue-chart), read 2026-10-16.

**Judgment:** use contribution before acquisition = customer collections minus refunds, taxes, store/payment fees and variable servicing costs. Then subtract acquisition cost. Keep fixed overhead separate, but require some margin to cover it. Do not subtract refunds twice when starting from a net-of-refund export, or apply one assumed commission to every transaction. Reconcile estimates to actual store reports.

### 3. Realized LTV and predicted LTV answer different questions

Realized LTV is what a cohort has generated by a named age. Predicted LTV estimates its future. A new app can measure early proceeds; it cannot already know its annual renewal rate. Nathan Hudson recommends observing revenue at successive cohort ages, payback and contribution rather than anchoring on one projected LTV:CAC number. **Practitioner:** [Hudson, published 2025-07-29](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/ltv-cac-subscription-apps-alternatives/).

**Judgment:** keep actual, conservative forecast and optimistic forecast in separate columns. Base the initial spending ceiling on first-term proceeds with a refund reserve. Only lend against renewals once comparable paid cohorts have actually renewed. An apparently attractive lifetime ratio can conceal years of cash tied up in acquisition.

### 4. A cohort is a group acquired together; compare groups at the same age

Comparing this week's revenue with last month's revenue confuses customer age with quality. RevenueCat groups its customer-LTV chart by first-seen date or an earlier qualifying purchase and marks immature periods. Its lifetime convention counts from day zero. **Official:** [cohort calculation and incomplete periods](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/realized-ltv-per-customer-chart), read 2026-10-16.

**Judgment:** group by acquisition week, country, channel where defensible, subscription plan and funnel version. Compare D14 with D14 and D30 with D30. Show cohort size and maturity beside every result. At low volume, start with weekly app-wide and single-channel cohorts; only split further when the samples support it. Preserve “unknown” attribution rather than forcing every payer into an ad.

### 5. The attribution window and the ROAS window are different clocks

An attribution window determines whether an ad interaction qualifies for conversion credit. A cohort ROAS window measures how much revenue acquired users generated by a particular age. The former is a credit rule; the latter is an economic observation period. TikTok documents click, view and engaged-view attribution separately. **Official:** [attribution overview, updated February 2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/attribution-overview?lang=en), [ad-group windows, updated July 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-attribution-windows-at-the-ad-group-level?lang=en).

**Judgment:** every ROAS screenshot needs its attribution setting, event definition, revenue basis, date basis and observation age attached. A trial becoming paid later can improve cohort economics without appearing in the same platform reporting window. Do not assume platform purchase ROAS means all lifetime revenue from its acquired users.

### 6. Payback is both an economic deadline and a cash problem

Economic payback occurs when cumulative contribution covers acquisition cost. Cash payback occurs when receipts actually reach the bank. Annual billing can accelerate collections, but cancellation, refunds and the cost of serving the remaining term still matter. RevenueCat distinguishes transaction revenue, normalized recurring metrics and estimated proceeds. **Official:** [Revenue chart](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/revenue-chart), read 2026-10-16. Phil Carter's framework emphasizes payback alongside product value and acquisition: **Practitioner summary:** [Meinertzhagen, 2024-04-23](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/the-subscription-value-loop-a-framework-for-subscription-app-growth/).

**Illustrative calculation, not ExcerLock data:** spending $100 daily with a 60-day cash recovery gap can tie up approximately $6,000 before allowing for uncertainty or other expenses. A profitable long-run model can still exhaust cash. Choose the acceptable recovery deadline from the captain's cash capacity before scaling.

### 7. Derive allowable CPI and trial cost from payer economics

A cheap install is valuable only if enough installers become profitable subscribers. RevenueCat explicitly links trial start rate with trial-to-paid conversion to explain overall conversion. **Official:** [Trial Conversion chart](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/trial-conversion-chart), read 2026-10-16.

**Illustrative calculation, 2026-10-16 judgment; all inputs invented:**

| Input or calculation | Example |
|---|---:|
| Contribution per payer by chosen payback deadline | $30 |
| Required contribution left after media acquisition | $10 |
| Maximum media CAC per payer | $20 |
| New-user-to-trial rate | 10% |
| Mature trial-to-paid rate | 40% |
| New-user-to-paid rate | 4% |
| Maximum media CPI: $20 × 4% | $0.80 |
| Maximum trial cost: $20 × 40% | $8 |

This assumes all first payers come through trials; add direct purchases separately if they exist. Creative fees and tools must fit inside the remaining margin or be included in fully loaded acquisition cost. Repeat the calculation with actual ExcerLock inputs and pessimistic conversion assumptions.

### 8. Trial starts are an early signal, not revenue

Two ads can generate equally cheap trials and very different paid conversion. RevenueCat's trial chart also counts a trial as converted even if the payment is later refunded. Therefore, high trial conversion alone does not establish good net economics. **Official:** [Trial Conversion definitions and refund FAQ](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/trial-conversion-chart), read 2026-10-16.

**Judgment:** measure cost per trial promptly, then mature trial-to-paid, first-payment proceeds, refunds and renewal. Trial-optimized campaigns may be a practical early compromise, but do not promote their success to a payer-CAC claim before the trials resolve. Match the waiting period to the actual trial length, billing retries and reporting delay.

### 9. Privacy measurement creates missing and delayed signals, not imaginary bank receipts

Apple's privacy-preserving attribution limits detail; missing conversion values need not mean missing conversions. AdAttributionKit builds on SKAdNetwork and does not require ATT permission for its own attribution, although tracking does require permission. **Official:** [Apple Ad Attribution](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ad-attribution/), read 2026-10-16.

Apple documents conversion windows of days **0–2, 3–7 and 8–35**, with randomized delays after the windows; the first delay is **24–48 hours**, later ones **24–144 hours**. Privacy tiers can limit fields and later postbacks. **Official:** [Apple multiple-window documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit/receiving-postbacks-in-multiple-conversion-windows), read 2026-10-16; raw Markdown recovered locally.

**Judgment:** same-day platform purchase ROAS is provisional. Maintain first-party subscription totals even when ad-level attribution is incomplete. Do not promise user-level joins from aggregated Apple postbacks, or assume a documented Apple capability is already fully supported by every network integration.

### 10. Platform-reported numbers can mislead without anyone falsifying transactions

Platforms allocate credit under their own rules and may estimate missing outcomes; different windows and view credit can produce conflicting stories. A user exposed on multiple networks can create overlapping platform credit. TikTok says each conversion is assigned to one touchpoint within its own attribution system; that is not a guarantee of cross-platform deduplication. **Official:** [TikTok attribution overview, February 2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/attribution-overview?lang=en). Apple's account of limited postbacks explains why individual-level reconciliation is incomplete: [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ad-attribution/), read 2026-10-16.

**Judgment:** reconcile spend and unique first payments across three views: platform delivery, attribution/MMP if used, and first-party financial cohorts. Investigate event duplication, trial-as-purchase values, currency, timezone, refunds, re-installs and view credit before blaming the ad. Never add Meta-attributed and TikTok-attributed revenue and call it total app revenue.

### 11. Blended CAC checks the business but does not prove causality

**Working definitions, judgment dated 2026-10-16:** blended media CAC = all paid media spend divided by all new first payers in the aligned acquisition cohort, including organic and unattributed payers. Fully loaded blended CAC also includes creative production and directly relevant acquisition costs. Neither should include old subscriber renewals in the new-payer denominator.

Zumba's operator reports that app campaigns had an organic App Store halo that helped blended CPA, illustrating why narrow attribution can miss wider effects. **Practitioner:** [Hudson, 2025-01-28](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/zumba-perceptycs-growth-case-study/).

**Judgment:** keep blended CAC beside channel CAC. But existing organic demand can make paid acquisition look affordable even if ads add little. Also distinguish calendar cash efficiency, total proceeds divided by current spend, from acquisition-cohort ROAS: old renewals can finance a losing new cohort.

### 12. Incrementality asks what would have happened without ads

Attribution asks who gets credit; incrementality asks how many extra outcomes advertising caused. TikTok's Conversion Lift uses test and control groups and reports incremental conversions, incremental ROAS and confidence. It is a managed service with eligibility requirements, not a universally available low-budget feature. **Official:** [Conversion Lift, updated April 2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-conversion-lift-study?lang=en).

Sleep Cycle's vendor case reports **up to 50% organic cannibalization** and different partner results on Android and iOS; its approach used **synthetic controls**, not necessarily randomized user holdouts. **Vendor case:** [Adjust/Sleep Cycle, undated; read 2026-10-16](https://www.adjust.com/resources/case-studies/sleep-cycle/).

**Judgment:** a small ExcerLock test should first establish reliable financial cohorts. Later, assess a holdout or matched-geo design with a pre-specified outcome, power calculation and stable comparison markets. A simple before/after pause is directional evidence, vulnerable to seasonality, delayed conversion and organic changes. No universal “minimum incrementality budget” was established here.

### 13. Model-based incremental attribution is not your own randomized experiment

Meta reported global rollout of incremental attribution in **Q2 2025** and later claimed a **24% increase in incremental conversions** from its Q4 model rollout versus standard attribution. **Official corporate claims:** [Q2 2025 remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_downloads/2025/META-Q2-2025-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), [Meta, 2026-01-29](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/2026-ai-drives-performance/).

**Judgment:** take modeled incrementality seriously as a delivery option, while keeping it distinct from a controlled experiment on ExcerLock. A platform's average test result neither guarantees the feature is available for our app setup nor validates our actual marginal profit. Record availability and reporting basis before comparing results.

### 14. Scale the next dollar, not the historical average

**Arithmetic, illustrative judgment dated 2026-10-16:** suppose comparable mature cohorts generated $1,500 contribution from $1,000 spend, then $1,900 from $1,500 spend. Average contribution return remains above break-even, but the extra $500 bought only $400 contribution: marginal return is 0.8×. Continuing that increase reduces profit. This is a comparison heuristic, not a causal estimate unless the other influences are controlled.

Zumba's team explicitly reports that increasing budget too fast raised CPA because its winning creatives could not perform consistently at higher scale. **Practitioner:** [Hudson, 2025-01-28](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/zumba-perceptycs-growth-case-study/).

**Judgment:** tag each budget step and judge its subsequent cohorts at equal age. Track incremental spend, additional first payers and additional contribution. A larger campaign is allowed to have a higher CPA if the added customers still satisfy the profit and payback constraint.

### 15. Vertical and horizontal scaling solve different limits

Vertical scaling raises an existing campaign's budget. Horizontal scaling adds genuinely different concepts, formats, audiences, markets or channels. Duplicating an ad set is only more structure unless it reaches productive additional demand. Zumba attributes recovery at higher spend to creative diversity as well as volume. **Practitioner:** [Zumba, 2025-01-28](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/zumba-perceptycs-growth-case-study/).

**Judgment:** expand within one proven market and channel first. Add a new creative promise before multiplying near-identical campaigns. Test new geographies and platforms as separate economic propositions: their payment conversion and value may differ. Do not carry one blended LTV into every expansion.

### 16. Learning status is a delivery diagnostic, not permission to lose money

TikTok's current general page says volatility typically starts declining after roughly **25 campaign results or 7 days**. That statement is not a universal guaranteed exit threshold. **Official:** [Learning Phase, updated June 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/learning-phase?lang=en).

TikTok's budget advice is internally inconsistent: the July page recommends at most **40% increases in learning, 30% after learning**, with at least **two days** between changes; the August best-practice page advises **no changes before exiting**, then staying within **50%** of the previous budget and waiting at least **two days**. **Official:** [Budget, July 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget?lang=en), [Budget best practices, August 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget-best-practices?lang=en).

**Judgment:** keep profitable tests stable, batch decisions and use conservative increases after mature evidence. Never spend to “buy” an exit badge if the cohort economics fail. Meta's detailed learning/edit pages were blocked; I cannot certify a universal Meta percentage or conversion threshold from them.

### 17. Creative refresh should respond to deteriorating economics

TikTok recommends refreshing when performance consistently declines or new-user delivery is low, and adding fresh creatives to an existing ad group. It recommends meaningfully different creative during testing. **Official:** [Creative best practices, updated June 2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en).

**Judgment:** review daily, prepare new concepts weekly, and replace based on evidence rather than an arbitrary expiration date. Examine whether higher cost comes from auction prices, attention, store conversion or subscriber quality. A low CTR ad with profitable payer acquisition can stay; an entertaining high-CTR ad with poor first-payment conversion needs work. Long-running ads demonstrate persistence, not independently verified profit.

### 18. Kill rules need a loss limit and enough time for outcomes to arrive

**Judgment, 2026-10-16:** stop immediately for broken destinations, invalid event values, runaway spend or a misleading product demonstration. For commercial underperformance, set a maximum loss before launch and separate “not enough evidence” from “failed.” An ad that the delivery system barely serves is untested, not disproven.

**Illustrative statistical check:** under a stable Poisson conversion assumption, spending three times target CPA and observing zero conversions would have probability exp(-3), approximately **5%**, if the target rate were true. That can justify a review, but is not a universal shutdown law: delayed attribution, nonstationary delivery and multiple comparisons break the simplification. Only use spend old enough for the relevant event and reporting delay. A portfolio of many weak ads can exceed its total loss limit before any one reaches its individual threshold.

This caution follows the documented reporting delays and cohort maturity definitions: **Official:** [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit/receiving-postbacks-in-multiple-conversion-windows), [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/realized-ltv-per-customer-chart), read 2026-10-16.

### 19. Product and paywall changes are acquisition changes

Zumba's team changed messaging across ads, store screenshots and paywalls, then moved the paywall earlier. Its story is about a connected funnel, not just auction tactics. **Practitioner:** [2025-01-28 case](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/zumba-perceptycs-growth-case-study/).

RevenueCat's Dipsea experiment found web-only paid conversion of **5.3%** versus **6.3%** for IAP-only, with lower take-home revenue despite fee savings. This is an in-app-to-web checkout experiment, not a direct test of cold-ad quiz funnels. **Vendor-run experiment:** [Eiting and Haandrikman, published 2025-05-14, trial results updated 2025-05-28](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/iap-vs-web-purchases-conversion-test/).

**Judgment:** log onboarding, permissions, trial and pricing versions beside each acquisition cohort. Avoid changing the funnel and budget simultaneously when trying to identify a cause. Better payment conversion can increase affordable acquisition spend; more trial starts without retained net receipts may not.

### 20. Benchmarks are a plausibility check, not a bid target

RevenueCat's **2026** report uses over **115,000 apps and $16B revenue**, primarily targeting **2025** performance. It reports Health & Fitness median revenue per install of **$0.48 at D14 and $0.66 at D60**. These are monetization benchmarks across its sample, not paid-US-iOS contribution ceilings. **Dataset:** [2026 report and methodology](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps), read 2026-10-16.

The **2025** edition reports Health & Fitness median trial-to-paid of **39.9%** and D60 revenue per install of **$0.63**. **Dataset:** [2025 report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025), read 2026-10-16. Edition changes also change samples; do not interpret the small RPI difference as a controlled industry growth rate.

**Judgment:** these figures should trigger hard questions about a proposed CPI and payback claim. They cannot tell us ExcerLock's LTV. Use paid-cohort actuals as soon as available, and distinguish first renewal, later conditional renewal and original-cohort retention.

## Disagreements and what changed during 2024–2026

**“Always keep LTV:CAC high” versus “stop focusing on LTV:CAC.”** Carter's framework uses the ratio and payback together; Hudson argues that one lifetime projection obscures subscription dynamics. These are compatible if the ratio is a clearly defined scenario and mature contribution/payback determine spend. Neither establishes a universal target ratio. [Carter summary, 2024-04-23](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/the-subscription-value-loop-a-framework-for-subscription-app-growth/); [Hudson, 2025-07-29](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/ltv-cac-subscription-apps-alternatives/).

**“Annual revenue is guaranteed” is too strong.** Zumba's operator favors upfront annual collection because it reduces renewal forecasting. That helps cash planning, but refunds, fees and ongoing service costs remain. Use first-term net contribution, not guaranteed gross lifetime receipts. [Zumba, 2025-01-28](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/zumba-perceptycs-growth-case-study/); [RevenueCat revenue definitions, read 2026-10-16](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/charts/revenue-chart).

**“Wait for 50 conversions” and “increase 20% daily” are not universal platform laws.** TikTok's current general learning page uses a different description, and its own budget docs conflict as detailed above. Some older guidance applies to a different objective or campaign type. Meta's corresponding help pages were blocked. This report deliberately does not promote remembered thresholds to current official rules. [TikTok learning, June 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/learning-phase?lang=en); [July budget](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget?lang=en); [August best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget-best-practices?lang=en).

**Privacy measurement has evolved beyond the earliest ATT playbooks.** Apple now documents AdAttributionKit, multiple windows and reengagement. That does not mean privacy aggregation is gone or all partner implementations match. Treat old single-postback explanations as incomplete, and check the integration actually shipped. [Apple overview](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ad-attribution/), [multiple windows](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit/receiving-postbacks-in-multiple-conversion-windows), read 2026-10-16.

**Meta's attribution products changed, so historical ROAS may not be comparable.** Its Q2 2025 remarks describe incremental-attribution rollout. A later measurement announcement changes click-through attribution to link clicks for **website and in-store conversions**, recategorizes other interactions and changes engaged-view reporting. Do not assume those announced website rules automatically apply to native app campaigns. The latter article's exact publication date was not recoverable from the extraction; its current text was read 2026-10-16. [Meta Q2 2025](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_downloads/2025/META-Q2-2025-Prepared-Remarks.pdf); [Meta measurement announcement](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/click-attribution).

**“Platforms always overclaim” is also too simplistic.** Fubo and LES MILLS report cases where attribution missed meaningful impact, while Fubo and Sleep Cycle also describe over-credit/cannibalization. Validate direction and magnitude instead of applying a fixed haircut to every network. [Fubo, 2026-04-20](https://www.appsflyer.com/customers/fubotv-incrementality-use-case/); [LES MILLS, 2024-08-21](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/les-mills-case-study); [Sleep Cycle, read 2026-10-16](https://www.adjust.com/resources/case-studies/sleep-cycle/).

**Benchmark editions are different datasets.** The newest edition supersedes the old one for current context, but changes in customer composition and observed periods prevent simple causal year-over-year conclusions. In the 2026 report, some adjacent summaries also use percentile labels inconsistently; this report uses the explicitly labeled Health & Fitness median in the D14/D60 sections. [2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025), read 2026-10-16.

## What this means for ExcerLock — my starting judgment, 2026-10-16

### Structure and bounded budget

Start with **one channel, one English-language market, one iOS acquisition campaign and one main prospecting ad set/group**, subject to the final settings lane and account eligibility. My preference is **Meta first**, because the most detailed applicable operator case here concentrated its initial learning there; this is not a verified Meta-versus-TikTok advantage for ExcerLock. Begin with adults **18–30** within the captain's broader audience; treat the **16–17** audience as a separate later setup decision, not pooled economics. This is an experiment design choice, not a claim about current teen advertising rules.

Propose a captain-approved media envelope of **$1,000–$2,000**, deployed in stages at approximately **$50–$100/day**, with a written maximum loss and end date. These are risk limits I propose, not a claim that those sums will exit learning, discover a winner or buy enough payers. Hold remaining funds while trials and postbacks mature when necessary. Do not start both channels just to spend the envelope faster.

If TikTok goes first instead, reconcile the actual campaign type with its current recommendations: the general August budget table suggests **20× current install CPA** for install campaigns and **10× current CPA** for deep conversions, or **$200** when no current CPA is known. Its separate July page lists lower delivery minimums. A platform minimum, recommended learning budget and a founder's affordable test are different things. If the chosen setup needs more than the captain can lose, change the test design or postpone it. [Official July budget](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget?lang=en); [August recommendations](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget-best-practices?lang=en).

### Creatives to make first

Make **three distinct concepts with two opening variations each** as a production inventory; initially deliver **three or four ads**, not all variants at tiny spend. These quantities are judgment, not validated creative-volume requirements. Test promises and user situations, not just colors:

- **Walking:** a real demonstration of selected apps becoming available under the chosen walking condition.
- **Gym follow-through:** a creator explaining the temptation to scroll and demonstrating one actual gym condition.
- **Step goal:** the chosen-app restriction and the specific goal-driven unlock, shown with verified current UI.

For each, show the behavior clearly enough that a downloader understands the commitment. These are concept descriptions, not finished hooks or scripts. Verify the build before filming: repository product facts are owner-described and current release status remains unverified in [core/product.md](../../../core/product.md). Do not turn movement motivation into an unsupported fat-loss outcome claim.

Record concept, creator, asset ID, opening variation, promised behavior and destination version. Evaluate downstream payer contribution, not the creator's organic view count. Keep the winning ad running while preparing alternatives.

### Measurement before the first dollar

This is my proposed minimum acceptance contract, not an SDK implementation guide:

| Layer | Record | Decision it enables |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Spend, impressions, CPM, outbound clicks, click definition, campaign/ad IDs, timezone, currency | Is delivery expensive or attention weak? |
| Acquisition | First opens/new users, available install attribution, unknown share, store/funnel version | Did traffic become actual users? |
| Activation | Onboarding completion, selected condition configured, first successful lock/unlock at aggregate level | Did users experience the advertised behavior? |
| Monetization | Trial start, trial due date, first paid transaction, plan, price/currency, renewal, refund and cancellation status | Are low-cost trials producing kept payments? |
| Economics | Cohort spend, estimated and reconciled proceeds, variable costs, contribution, fully loaded CAC | Does acquisition pay back within the chosen deadline? |
| Evidence | Actual versus predicted, cohort age, sample size, attribution method, reporting delay, last refresh | Is the result strong enough to act on? |

Keep product activation analysis first-party and aggregate; these proposed events are not instructions to send raw Health, location or Screen Time details to ad networks. Map only appropriate business events through the chosen supported integration, verify trial and paid events are distinct, and verify transaction deduplication before trusting ROAS.

Use an acquisition-week table with **D7, D14, D30 and D60** columns, plus longer observations aligned to the actual plan renewals. Those are proposed inspection ages; paid conversion must be marked immature until the trial/billing sequence can resolve. Include paid-media and fully loaded blended CAC, new first payers, refunds, new-user contribution, payer contribution and a separate cash forecast. Where paid revenue cannot be assigned at ad level, show that limitation instead of calculating false precision.

### An explicit scale / hold / stop policy

All thresholds below are proposed judgment, not platform requirements:

1. **Scale only after** tracking reconciles, trials have substantially resolved, and more than one mature acquisition cohort meets the allowable CAC and payback ceiling. As a practical early floor, seek roughly **20–30 first payers** across comparable cohorts before treating a winner as more than a lead. That is still noisy, not statistical proof; use uncertainty ranges and a downside scenario.
2. **First increases:** roughly **10–20%**, at most once every **two or three days**, only after the preceding cohort evidence supports it. Observe delayed economics before stacking increases. These are conservative operating choices, not a certified Meta safe-edit threshold; TikTok's conflicting official guidance is above.
3. **Hold** when the result depends on unresolved trials, a few annual purchases, missing events or an optimistic renewal forecast. Keep funding within the approved total loss envelope; waiting does not require continuing to spend.
4. **Reduce or stop** when matured CAC cannot fit a conservative contribution estimate, marginal contribution falls below the chosen hurdle, or the total loss cap is reached. Apply the zero-conversion check from lesson 18 only to mature event opportunities. Record “insufficient evidence” when spend is simply too small.
5. **Expand horizontally** after one repeatable pocket works: a new creative concept first, then a separately tracked market or channel. Carry forward the measurement contract, not assumed economics.

Choose the payback hurdle from actual pricing and cash. A **D30** primary check and **D60** follow-up are a reasonable initial reporting proposal, but not a promise that ExcerLock can afford delayed recovery. If the captain needs cash back inside the first paid term, that stricter constraint wins.

### Connect it to the existing operations system

Propose an independent paid-acquisition module owning spend imports, cohort calculations, uncertainty and proposed decisions. Reuse the existing dashboard's generic inbox-extension interface through a composition adapter outside both sections. Do not put ad logic in creator pipeline stages or make sections import each other.

A daily read-only job should import platform CSV/API exports and first-party subscription aggregates, then create one review card: **continue / hold / proposed increase / stop**, current versus proposed budget, mature cohort economics, worst-case exposure and data-quality notes. Budget changes remain captain-approved. Preserve the creative asset/creator ID so outcome summaries can feed the existing performance-feedback view without pretending paid attribution and organic creator performance are the same metric.

This reuses the dashboard, approval inbox and job infrastructure described in [creators README](../../../sections/creators/README.md); it is a concrete proposed connection, not implemented software. Begin with export imports if integrations would delay the first reliable report. Do not create another disconnected tracker.
