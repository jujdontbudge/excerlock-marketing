# Best videos and teachers for learning Meta and TikTok ads

Research date: **2026-10-16**. Lane: `best-videos-and-teachers`.

**Start with Ben Heath for the controls, Savannah Sanchez for app advertising, Dara Denney for deciding what to test, Meredith Unger for TikTok, and the Faris–Bachman debate for how to spend on tests.** Those five give different skills rather than five versions of the same advice. Read Jon Loomer's Master Brief alongside them, but treat its web-sales examples as examples, not an iOS implementation guide.

## Coverage and evidence limits

I read original practitioner articles, publisher-hosted educational transcripts, public course outlines, credibility profiles, and an official TikTok PDF. The source register below records every candidate used or rejected. More than 25 distinct resources supplied readable material; catalog pages and biographies are identified separately and do not establish the contents of a course. HTML/PDF responses, extracted text, YouTube captions and metadata are saved in `../raw/best-videos-and-teachers/`. `sources.json` maps numbered raw folders to URLs. No ad account was opened, no paid scraping was used, and no API key was used. External research spend: **$0**.

For the main video recommendations I read substantive transcript passages and the host's visual/slide descriptions. Direct YouTube captions and metadata were also recovered for most Motion talks and Ben's tutorial. This is educational-content research, not a frame-by-frame audit of the example ads. Motion's written summaries and visual annotations are publisher material, not independently verified campaign evidence. Their speaker labels sometimes misattribute dialogue. Their library recording month can differ from on-screen event dates; dates below use direct YouTube upload metadata when available, not an assumed recording date.

The sample is deliberately concentrated in teachers with inspectable teaching. It is also **Motion-heavy and ecommerce-heavy**. Motion sells creative analytics, features its partners repeatedly, and sponsors some podcasts. Several separate talks do not constitute independent evidence for a universal tactic. TikTok's own material is authoritative about its tools and biased toward selling advertising. None of the practitioner spend, revenue, team-size or performance claims was audited against an ad account. There is no defensible worldwide ranking of every course: this ranks the accessible material I could inspect for this founder's needs.

## Watch these five first, in this order

These are viewing assignments, not instructions to publish or spend. Stop after each and produce the small output shown.

1. **[The BEST Facebook Ads Tutorial for Beginners (Full Guide)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs)** — 2025-01-14; 74 min. Learn the interface and optimization objective. **After watching:** Draw campaign → ad set → ad, and write the business action each level controls.

2. **[Savannah Sanchez Shares Her TOP 43 Ad Hooks for Apps and Services](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jAc63K9lRIQ)** — 2025-02-28; 41 min. See how apps and services become understandable ads. **After watching:** Choose three applicable demonstration formats; record what each would prove about ExcerLock.

3. **[How to Diagnose a Brand & Prioritize Your Ideas | Dara Denney](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-E9sjb2msjo)** — 2026-06-22; 68 min. Turn customer evidence into a testing plan. **After watching:** Write one diagnosis and three testable customer motivations, with evidence or an explicit assumption.

4. **[Winning TikTok Ad Formats in 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tuqlIKBlTao)** — 2024-06-29; 47 min. Understand what native TikTok execution changes. **After watching:** Storyboard one walking-day video with sound, captions, product action and a clear next step.

5. **[DTC Ad Creative Testing Showdown: Faris vs Bachman 🥊](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yNHt7KR2HQ)** — 2024-07-17; 55 min. Understand the price of learning versus immediate efficiency. **After watching:** Decide whether the first experiment is buying conversions or buying a controlled comparison.

## Ranked learning library

Rank reflects beginner usefulness, inspected teaching, transfer to ExcerLock, and freshness—not subscriber count. Video runtimes and upload dates below come from saved YouTube metadata. “Transcript lesson” means I inspected the teaching text; it does not verify the speaker's underlying results. The three course/guide picks at the end are explicitly identified as such.

### 1. Ben Heath — beginner setup

[The BEST Facebook Ads Tutorial for Beginners (Full Guide)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs) — uploaded 2025-01-14; 74 min.

**Why this teacher:** Founder of Heath Media. His conference biography describes an established agency and substantial managed spend; that is promotional biography, not an audit. The video itself is a continuous worked account setup, useful evidence of teaching ability.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** He distinguishes objectives by the behavior Meta is asked to find: clickers are not automatically buyers. He separates business assets, campaign objective, ad-set delivery and the ad itself. He explains why automated placements need a meaningful conversion signal, and why generated copy still needs offer-specific judgment. The lead-generation walkthrough is especially clear around the objective and placement chapters.

**Limit:** Use it for vocabulary and navigation. Its worked example is an agency lead funnel, not mobile app acquisition; do not copy its Leads objective, website pixel setup, targeting screens or publish step into ExcerLock. Source and evidence: [S28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs); read 2026-10-16.

### 2. Savannah Sanchez — apps and services

[Savannah Sanchez Shares Her TOP 43 Ad Hooks for Apps and Services](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jAc63K9lRIQ) — uploaded 2025-02-28; 41 min.

**Why this teacher:** Founder of The Social Savannah. Her own demonstration includes creative made for Bumble, Noom and Jackpocket; a separate Triple Whale profile describes her Common Thread Collective background and TikTok creative-partner work. Client association is corroboration of operating experience, not profitability verification.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** The product can be an action on a screen rather than a physical object. She demonstrates a self-skit, walk-and-talk, Notes/checklist, slideshow, whiteboard, FAQ and greenscreen treatment. Relatable situations earn attention, while overlays and real app screens explain the mechanism. Her Q&A explicitly ties creative volume to client scale.

**Limit:** Best direct bridge to this app. Learn the construction of the examples. A staged street interview or a fake comment is not independent customer proof; do not turn actors into supposedly verified users. Source and evidence: [S4](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/savannah-sanchez-shares-her-top-43-ad-hooks-for-apps-and-services/); read 2026-10-16.

### 3. Dara Denney — diagnosis and prioritization

[How to Diagnose a Brand & Prioritize Your Ideas | Dara Denney](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-E9sjb2msjo) — uploaded 2026-06-22; 68 min.

**Why this teacher:** Former Senior Director of Performance Creative at Thesis; in this talk she identifies herself as a Point Guard Media co-owner. Her worked brand analyses and visible briefs are stronger evidence of expertise than the client logos alone.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Compare the customer motivations found in reviews with the motivations represented in existing ads. The gap becomes a concise diagnosis explaining why the next tests belong together. Ask AI for source quotes behind persona claims. Around 22:17 she explains the diagnosis; later she distinguishes budget-efficient delivery from paying for creative learning.

**Limit:** Start with observed problems, not demographic stereotypes. Inferred personas from public ad creative are hypotheses, not visibility into the advertiser’s actual targeting or customer mix. Source and evidence: [S3](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-stop-guessing-start-diagnosing-ad-creative-testing/); read 2026-10-16.

### 4. Meredith Unger — native TikTok formats

[Winning TikTok Ad Formats in 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tuqlIKBlTao) — uploaded 2024-06-29; 47 min.

**Why this teacher:** Introduced as a Tinuiti creative strategist. Her presentation shows named client-style formats and explains their construction; it does not expose a controlled account experiment.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Build around the feed’s viewing habits: sound, familiar creator behavior, early story payoff and continuing visual interest. GRWM, day-in-the-life, behind-the-scenes and transitions are durable containers. The GRWM explanation shows why an ordinary ongoing activity can carry a product story rather than waiting for a polished reveal.

**Limit:** Useful creative fundamentals from 2024. Her refresh cadence and minimum-video advice are practitioner heuristics, not current app-campaign requirements. Use licensed commercial sound and captions; verify current settings elsewhere. Source and evidence: [S6](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/winning-tiktok-ad-formats-in-2024/); read 2026-10-16.

### 5. Andrew Faris vs Jess Bachman, moderated by Barry Hott — testing debate

[DTC Ad Creative Testing Showdown: Faris vs Bachman 🥊](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yNHt7KR2HQ) — uploaded 2024-07-17; 55 min.

**Why this teacher:** Faris runs AJF Growth and describes prior operating roles at Common Thread Collective and 4x400; Bachman runs FireTeam. Both put an explicit operating method up for challenge. Their displayed account examples remain selected, self-reported evidence.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Faris wants the delivery system to allocate against cost controls rather than subsidize separate testing campaigns. Bachman values dedicated spend and faster creative learning. The best passage is their qualified agreement: Bachman says his method can waste money for inexperienced teams and emphasizes its usefulness at larger brand scale. They are optimizing different objectives, not merely disagreeing about buttons.

**Limit:** Watch both sides. An ad receiving little spend is evidence about delivery under those conditions, not a clean experiment proving the concept cannot work. Conversely, forcing equal spend has a real opportunity cost. Source and evidence: [S5](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dtc-ad-creative-testing-showdown-faris-vs-bachman/); read 2026-10-16.

### 6. Jon Loomer — The Master Brief

[Jon Loomer — The Master Brief](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-master-brief/) — 2026-07-02; July 2026 snapshot.

**Why this teacher:** Long-running Meta educator writing under his own name. This document explicitly discloses AI-assisted synthesis and his editorial approval; it is an inspectable statement of his views, not platform documentation.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** A coherent system: consolidate budget, optimize for the meaningful action, inspect attribution, question extra complexity and diversify the actual messages. It explains why a high-spend ad can have a higher average acquisition cost while still helping the total account. It also asks which concrete problem every manual intervention solves.

**Limit:** A July 2026 snapshot that explicitly will not update. Its web/lead settings, value rules, audience segments and new testing tools are not verified here for iOS app campaigns. Even its emphatic language about algorithms should be cross-checked against Meta’s engineering source. Source and evidence: [S1](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-master-brief/); read 2026-10-16.

### 7. Evan Lee — creative analytics and diagnosis

[How to Analyze Facebook Ads: Metrics & the Diagnostic Cheatsheet | Evan Lee, Motion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oO_9CefhuDE) — uploaded 2026-06-22; 66 min.

**Why this teacher:** Motion educator demonstrating actual report workflows and ad comparisons. Credible on the product and analytical workflow; not evidence that buying Motion is necessary or that every diagnosis is causal.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Start with a hypothesis, then distinguish attention, sustained viewing, clicks and conversion. Compare similar funnel contexts. Group results by creator, message or feature to develop the next brief. The teaching links an apparent ad problem to potential destination-message mismatch rather than assuming every low conversion rate needs a new hook.

**Limit:** Translate the destination into App Store page → onboarding → paywall for this app. Engagement ratios are diagnostic clues; they cannot prove a page caused the loss or establish the quality of future subscribers. Source and evidence: [S12](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/creative-analytics-explained-why-your-ad-won-or-lost-evan-lee-motion/); read 2026-10-16.

### 8. Evan Lee — the jobs and layers of a hook

[What Is Creative Strategy? The Foundations Every Creative Strategist Needs | Evan Lee, Motion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DqFjak694PQ) — uploaded 2026-06-22; 51 min.

**Why this teacher:** Motion bootcamp instructor using its own ads and consumer-brand examples. Public teaching and slide/transcript text were readable; the subtitle download was rate-limited, but publisher teaching text was readable.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Analyze the opening across visual, audio/text and psychological layers. His Ridge example shows that a recognizable identity cue can qualify a particular audience. Attention should connect to the subsequent sales story; a startling opening without that connection is a weak foundation for a conversion ad.

**Limit:** Library labels it June 2026; the bootcamp artwork says March 2026. Use the direct upload metadata for publication; the precise recording date remains uncertain. Do not treat its headline percentage about hooks as a measured universal contribution to results. Source and evidence: [S13](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/creative-strategy-explained-the-3-jobs-of-a-hook-bootcamp-week-1-evan-lee-motion/); read 2026-10-16.

### 9. Sarah Levinger — advanced customer research

[How to do ADVANCED Facebook Ad Research [ Best Tools + Methods]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lTW3iyjB118) — uploaded 2024-09-20; 49 min.

**Why this teacher:** Presented as a consumer-psychology and creative-research practitioner. The value here is an explicit question-to-tool workflow; several broad behavioral statistics in her slides are not independently sourced well enough to reuse.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Decide what you need to know before collecting data. Compare the words a brand uses with the words people search. Use search questions, adjacent communities and qualitative conversations to understand the emotional setting of a purchase. She explains why collecting a huge pile of facts without a decision attached does not produce strategy.

**Limit:** Use the research questions, not the old plugin stack. Her ranking of checkout research as “nice to know” is not appropriate when an app’s onboarding or paywall is the bottleneck. Source and evidence: [S10](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-do-advanced-facebook-ad-research-best-tools-methods/); read 2026-10-16.

### 10. Savannah Sanchez — repeatable production

[How I Make 200+ Winning Meta Ads Per Week (Full Production Process)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MT2MzrTGC5w) — uploaded 2026-01-30; 58 min.

**Why this teacher:** Same operator as rank 2, now exposing briefs, review checklists and production stages. The output volume in the title is her agency’s claim, not a founder’s required workload.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** Specify raw shots for the creator and line-by-line direction for the editor. Review footage before editing, then review final captions, sound and pacing. Keep a searchable record of tested hooks and usable footage. Avoid fleeting trends that expire before the production cycle ends.

**Limit:** Assign ownership of strategy, filming and editing even if a founder wears multiple hats. Her staffing and variation thresholds describe agency operations; they are not minimum hiring or ad-volume requirements. Source and evidence: [S7](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-i-make-200-winning-meta-ads-per-week-full-production-process/); read 2026-10-16.

### 11. Abir Syed — financial metrics for marketers

[The Most Important Financial Metrics Every Digital Marketer Needs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NP7gsiwoVpA) — uploaded 2024-09-23; 52 min.

**Why this teacher:** Finance-oriented practitioner teaching worked contribution-margin examples. The strength is transparent arithmetic and business reasoning; client attribution stories are anecdotes.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** ROAS is not cash flow or profit. Compare revenue with the variable costs of acquiring and serving the customer, then include creative production costs. New-customer performance can worsen while blended revenue hides it. Early enthusiastic organic customers need not predict the lifetime value of later paid customers.

**Limit:** For a subscription app, replace physical-goods assumptions with net subscription proceeds, refunds, service costs, retention and a chosen payback horizon. Do not import an ecommerce ROAS target. Source and evidence: [S9](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-most-important-financial-metrics-every-digital-marketer-needs/); read 2026-10-16.

### 12. Barry Hott — relevance and authenticity

[How to Make AI Native Ads Look Human (Barry Hott Method)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6jvIXIjieyA) — uploaded 2025-05-29; 55 min.

**Why this teacher:** Longtime paid-social operator and Adcrate adviser. He describes major personally managed spend, which I could not audit. His strongest teaching is the close comparison of feed-native and advertiser-native content.

**Lessons from the transcript or reading:** “Native” depends on what this audience consumes, not on making everything intentionally bad. Polished or AI content can still be relevant, while an expensive ad can trigger immediate avoidance. Preserve human authority and use AI deliberately rather than treating it as a strategy.

**Limit:** Important trap: he explicitly says the authenticity statistics and chart were made up with ChatGPT. They are demonstration material, not findings. His spend claim is also self-reported. Source and evidence: [S11](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-make-ai-native-ads-look-human-barry-hott-method/); read 2026-10-16.

## The 18 most important things to understand

**1. Choose teachers by inspectable reasoning.** A worked example reveals what someone does and why; a revenue screenshot does not. Give extra weight to explicit assumptions, examples and failure conditions. Even named agencies and client logos do not independently verify spend or results. Evidence: [S5](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dtc-ad-creative-testing-showdown-faris-vs-bachman/), [S9](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-most-important-financial-metrics-every-digital-marketer-needs/), [S28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**2. The platform learns the action you request.** A click objective can successfully buy people who like clicking while failing the business. Learn the objective first, then choose the deepest valuable event the actual app setup can support with usable signal. Evidence: [S28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs), [S1](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-master-brief/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**3. Do not confuse a beginner interface tour with an acquisition strategy.** The mechanics of campaign/ad-set/ad are transferable. A website lead form, a Shopify purchase, and an iOS subscription have different signals and destinations. Copy the logic and verify the implementation for the actual objective. Evidence: [S28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs), [S31](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/248614-ads-manager-basics), [S17](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/tiktok-fundamentals-unlock-full-potential-tiktok-advertising); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**4. Creative research starts with a decision.** Ask which motivation, objection or misunderstanding could explain the current result. Then choose the source that can answer it. Collecting more inspiration without that question can produce more work without a better test. Evidence: [S3](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-stop-guessing-start-diagnosing-ad-creative-testing/), [S10](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-do-advanced-facebook-ad-research-best-tools-methods/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**5. Personas need evidence.** A persona is useful when it describes a real trigger or obstacle that changes the ad. AI summaries should return supporting source material. Public creative can suggest intended audiences; it cannot reveal actual audience composition. Evidence: [S3](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-stop-guessing-start-diagnosing-ad-creative-testing/), [S34](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-reveals-the-biggest-creative-analysis-mistake-in-2026-most/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**6. A format is a container, not the strategy.** A skit, greenscreen or slideshow does not specify who should care or what the product proves. The same message may work in multiple containers, and a familiar container may work for several different messages. Evidence: [S4](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/savannah-sanchez-shares-her-top-43-ad-hooks-for-apps-and-services/), [S34](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-reveals-the-biggest-creative-analysis-mistake-in-2026-most/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**7. The hook must earn attention from a plausible buyer.** Attention disconnected from the product story can produce views without useful action. Visual, spoken and psychological elements should reinforce a relevant problem or desired outcome. Evidence: [S13](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/creative-strategy-explained-the-3-jobs-of-a-hook-bootcamp-week-1-evan-lee-motion/), [S6](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/winning-tiktok-ad-formats-in-2024/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**8. Make software visible through behavior.** People can understand an app by seeing a recognizable situation, a real screen action and the resulting access or capability. Abstract lists of features leave more work for the viewer. Evidence: [S4](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/savannah-sanchez-shares-her-top-43-ad-hooks-for-apps-and-services/), [S19](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_SMB_Creative_Playbook_2025.pdf); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**9. Native depends on the audience and placement.** “Ugly” is not a universal winning style. Familiar filming, speech, captions and sound can lower the feeling of interruption; unreadable screens and bad audio can still prevent comprehension. Evidence: [S11](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-make-ai-native-ads-look-human-barry-hott-method/), [S6](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/winning-tiktok-ad-formats-in-2024/), [S19](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_SMB_Creative_Playbook_2025.pdf); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**10. Diversification and iteration solve different problems.** A new motivation or concept explores a different opportunity. A controlled opening change examines a narrower question within an already promising story. An account needs an intentional balance rather than endless near-duplicates. Evidence: [S2](https://www.jonloomer.com/andromeda-3/), [S7](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-i-make-200-winning-meta-ads-per-week-full-production-process/), [S16](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-my-team-ships-2-000-ads-per-month-ft-mirella-crespi/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**11. Buying learning has an opportunity cost.** Equal exposure can make comparison easier but requires spending on weak ads. Algorithmic allocation can protect immediate efficiency but leaves some concepts barely explored. Choose which question the experiment is intended to answer. Evidence: [S5](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dtc-ad-creative-testing-showdown-faris-vs-bachman/), [S3](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-stop-guessing-start-diagnosing-ad-creative-testing/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**12. Low spend is not a randomized verdict.** Delivery depends on the available audience, auction, controls, competing creatives and signal. Little delivery is informative about that configuration; it does not isolate the creative’s intrinsic effect. Evidence: [S5](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dtc-ad-creative-testing-showdown-faris-vs-bachman/), [S1](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-master-brief/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**13. Separate success metrics from diagnostic metrics.** Subscriber acquisition economics define success. Hook rate, viewing depth and click-through help locate a possible problem. None guarantees valuable customers, and no single ratio establishes causation. Evidence: [S12](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/creative-analytics-explained-why-your-ad-won-or-lost-evan-lee-motion/), [S34](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-reveals-the-biggest-creative-analysis-mistake-in-2026-most/), [S9](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-most-important-financial-metrics-every-digital-marketer-needs/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**14. A profitable-looking ROAS can hide weak economics.** Attribution can claim revenue that would have occurred anyway; gross revenue can ignore costs and refunds. Subscription contribution and payback should determine allowable acquisition cost, with explicit retention assumptions. Evidence: [S9](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-most-important-financial-metrics-every-digital-marketer-needs/), [S1](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-master-brief/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**15. Ad and destination must keep the same promise.** If the ad sells one mechanism but the store page or onboarding does not show it clearly, the handoff may fail. Inspect the path before deciding the opening is the only problem. Evidence: [S12](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/creative-analytics-explained-why-your-ad-won-or-lost-evan-lee-motion/), [S28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrio_G_1Cs); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**16. Creative output is an operations problem too.** Clear briefs, raw-shot requirements, asset labels and review stages shorten the route from finding to next test. A volume target without these can multiply unusable work. Evidence: [S7](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-i-make-200-winning-meta-ads-per-week-full-production-process/), [S16](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-my-team-ships-2-000-ads-per-month-ft-mirella-crespi/), [S14](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/why-most-creative-strategists-are-set-up-to-fail-and-how-to-fix-it/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**17. AI capability is not performance evidence.** A generated asset demonstration establishes production capability. It does not show incremental subscribers or retention. AI-assisted research also needs source checks; even a persuasive lecture may include intentionally fabricated statistics. Evidence: [S15](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/ai-ads-vs-human-creators-which-performs-better-ugc-expert-ai-engineer-reveal/), [S11](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-make-ai-native-ads-look-human-barry-hott-method/), [S3](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/dara-denney-stop-guessing-start-diagnosing-ad-creative-testing/); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

**18. Every useful lesson has a scope and a date.** A production principle may survive interface changes. A 2024 setting, refresh interval or duration guideline may not. Check current official product material and keep platform recommendations separate from practitioner preferences. Evidence: [S1](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-master-brief/), [S17](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/tiktok-fundamentals-unlock-full-potential-tiktok-advertising), [S19](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_SMB_Creative_Playbook_2025.pdf); source dates in the register below; all read 2026-10-16.

## What this means for ExcerLock — my judgment, 2026-10-16

These are proposed starting choices, not platform requirements or benchmark promises. Nothing below authorizes launching ads or spending money.

**Learn by producing a reviewable test plan.** Complete the five viewing assignments, then write a short diagnosis such as: “People may understand app blocking but not why tying selected-app access to movement would help them follow through.” This is a hypothesis to check in actual customer language. Verify the current app build and release status before any download CTA. Product facts come from `core/product.md`, which explicitly marks several capabilities as owner-described rather than freshly tested.

**Start with one platform and a small structure.** My starting preference is Meta, with one app-acquisition campaign and one consolidated initial ad set for one chosen market, iOS, and a measurable downstream goal. Use the app-specific lane to choose trial versus purchase optimization, attribution and integration. Do not copy Heath’s lead campaign or Loomer’s website tracking instructions. Delay a second platform until there is enough budget and operational attention to interpret both. TikTok remains a good second test for creator-native movement demonstrations; that is a creative-fit hypothesis, not a claim it will be cheaper.

**Budget proposal: $1,000–$2,000 of media as a bounded first learning allocation**, roughly $50–$100/day over about two to four weeks depending on delivery. Production is a separate cost. This is my risk-allocation judgment, not a minimum to “exit learning,” an expected acquisition cost, or a proven profitable budget. If the captain can risk only $500, treat it as a narrower instrumentation/message pilot, not a reliable channel verdict. A hard stop should be tied to the agreed loss limit, broken tracking, or a materially false product promise; reassess commercial performance only after the relevant conversion and trial cohorts have had time to mature.

Before launch, choose a payback horizon and calculate allowable paid-subscriber acquisition cost from **net expected subscription contribution**, after fees, refunds and variable service costs. If trial-to-paid conversion is a probability `p`, allowable cost per trial is approximately `allowable paid-subscriber CAC × p`. This is arithmetic with uncertain inputs, not a benchmark. Do not finance the experiment using retention that has not been observed.

**Make a small, genuinely distinct first batch.** I would initially prepare four concepts, with enough reusable footage for a second opening on the strongest idea. These are concepts, not captain-approved scripts or invented quotations:

| Concept | What to show | Question it tests |
|---|---|---|
| Real selected-app demonstration | Choosing a nonessential app, seeing its locked state, then the actual movement-related access behavior in the current build | Does seeing the mechanism make the value understandable? |
| Walk-and-talk routine | A real creator explains a walking routine while demonstrating the relevant app condition | Does an existing routine make the app feel easier to adopt? |
| Same-person self-skit | A clearly performed contrast between wanting to scroll and wanting to follow through, followed by product proof | Is the internal conflict a relatable entry point? |
| Simple checklist or screen-led explanation | A readable choice → exercise condition → access sequence, with the real interface | Does a quieter explanation outperform personality-led creative for comprehension and intent? |

Use a few different motivations—walking consistency, keeping a gym commitment, getting started instead of scrolling—rather than making the whole batch one fat-loss promise. No weight-loss outcome is established by these sources or by the product record. Verify each condition independently; do not imply that the whole phone locks or that all movement modes behave identically. I would begin with adults while app positioning and campaign controls are being established; the brief’s younger audience remains a separate targeting and creative decision to check in the relevant platform lane.

**Measure the path to a retained subscriber.** Record spend; impressions and outbound clicks; installs; onboarding and relevant permissions; activation of a real restriction; trial starts; first paid conversions; refunds; subscription proceeds; and retention by acquisition cohort where the app can support it. Cost per trial and paid CAC are more decision-relevant than views. Watch retention and activation so a click-winning promise does not recruit people who never use the product. Keep platform-attributed and billing/attribution-system counts side by side with their windows and delays; do not assume they should match on the same day.

**Use one existing operations system.** Reuse creator intake, deal terms, draft hand-in and the approval inbox. Add a proposed paid-creative review card showing the asset, chosen app condition, target motivation, source lesson, permission/usage-rights scope, budget proposal and success criterion. Approval of a video should remain distinct from approval to spend on it. Capture campaign/ad IDs and later results in a separate paid-acquisition module that owns media data and decisions; expose a small adapter to the generic inbox and performance views through a composition layer outside both sections. Scheduled jobs could import approved read-only result snapshots, then propose the next creator brief after cohorts mature. The existing organic views/likes score should not silently become a paid-ad profitability score. This is a concrete extension proposal, not an implementation or deployment done in this lane.
