# App campaign targeting and setup: Meta and TikTok

Research captured 2026-10-16. Written for the captain before ExcerLock spends on ads.

**My recommendation:** prepare a direct-to-App-Store, iOS acquisition campaign with one consolidated audience, ordinary subscription events, and creatives demonstrating the actual app. Start with adults. Treat “women 18–30” as either an enforced test boundary or an audience hypothesis, and record which it is. Do not assume entering an age range or gender makes it a restriction.

This report explains setup, rather than estimating ExcerLock's acquisition costs. No app build, analytics implementation, ad account, campaign draft, permissions, prices, or current App Store availability was inspected. Nothing was launched or changed. Recommendations below are research judgments, not account-tested presets.

## The 18 most important things to understand

### 1. The objective chooses the destination family; the optimization goal chooses the people

An app campaign can seek installs, a particular event, or purchase value. These are different instructions to the delivery system. A cheap installer need not become a subscriber. Meta describes app event optimization as finding people likely to take the selected action after installation; TikTok similarly separates click, install, in-app event and value goals. **My inference:** use the deepest meaningful event you can measure reliably and fund adequately, rather than choosing the metric that looks cheapest. Sources: [Meta optimization, S03](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-ads/optimizing-your-app-ad/) (updated 2024-10-23); [TikTok objectives, S09](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/what-is-app-promotion-objective) (January 2026).

For a standard first campaign, select **App promotion**, identify the iOS app, then choose the performance/optimization goal. A Traffic campaign linking to the store does not thereby become an install-optimized campaign. TikTok specifically warns that Traffic campaigns can have incomplete iOS conversion data. Source: [dedicated iOS setup, S10](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-an-ios-14-5-dedicated-campaign) (February 2025).

### 2. Sending the ad to a website is a separate funnel decision

Web conversion campaigns need an actual web event and a deliberately designed route to the app, plus appropriate measurement. A website pixel cannot simply observe an App Store subscription. TikTok's Events API introduction is explicitly about web events; its App Events SDK is the app connection. Sources: [web Events API, S31](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/getting-started-events-api) (April 2025); [App Events SDK, S19](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-the-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (September 2025).

**My recommendation:** start with the direct app funnel unless a separate business case justifies a website, quiz or web checkout. Lucas Moscon describes “enhanced app campaigns” as web campaigns redirecting to stores through his company's infrastructure. That is a vendor-defined approach, not a platform objective or evidence that native app campaigns stopped working. Source: [Moscon/Appstack on RevenueCat, S36](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/enhanced-app-campaigns) (published March 16, updated March 18, 2026).

### 3. Install, trial and payment must mean different things in the implementation

A first app open establishes an activation/install event in SDK reporting. A trial should mean a confirmed trial beginning; paid subscription should mean payment, not opening the paywall. Meta explicitly defines Start Trial and Subscribe separately and requires value and currency for Purchase. Sources: [Meta optimization, S03](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-ads/optimizing-your-app-ad/) (2024-10-23); [Meta iOS events, S02](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-events/getting-started-app-events-ios/) (2026-10-16).

**Proposed contract:** log first open, confirmed trial, first paid transaction and renewal distinctly. Do not send an annual subscription's advertised price as revenue when its free trial starts. Keep refunds and cancellations in subscription reporting. Use one consistent revenue definition when comparing dashboards. These are implementation recommendations; ExcerLock's subscription products and actual event code remain unchecked.

### 4. SDK-only can be a valid beginning; an MMP solves a different problem

An SDK is code inside the app that sends events. An MMP, or mobile measurement partner, connects acquisition and app outcomes across channels. Meta supports its own App Events integration; Adjust documents a partner route including event mapping and SKAN configuration. TikTok offers a direct SDK and now documents adding it to an MMP-connected app in the same account. Sources: [Meta SDK, S02](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-events/getting-started-app-events-ios/) (2026-10-16); [Adjust Meta setup, S05](https://help.adjust.com/en/article/set-up-facebook) (undated; captured 2026-10-16); [TikTok SDK integration, S20](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-integrate-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (March 2026).

**My judgment:** price and evaluate an MMP before committing to two paid channels, but do not buy one solely because an old tutorial says it is compulsory. Native integrations can support platform optimization; consistent cross-channel reporting, SKAN ownership and subscription reconciliation are the reasons to consider an MMP. No vendor pricing or ExcerLock integration was checked here.

### 5. Duplicate events and conflicting conversion values can make correct targeting look broken

Adjust instructs customers forwarding events through its Meta integration to disable overlapping Facebook SDK logging. TikTok's hybrid instructions require a single SKAN conversion-value writer; they explain disabling TikTok SKAN support when the MMP owns the schema. These are integration-specific instructions, not a command to disable every SDK. Sources: [Adjust registration and event setup, S04](https://help.adjust.com/en/article/set-up-an-app-in-your-facebook-developer-account) (undated; captured 2026-10-16); [TikTok hybrid setup, S20](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-integrate-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (March 2026).

**Why it matters:** if a trial is counted twice, apparent cost per trial falls even though nothing improved. If multiple SDKs overwrite conversion values, reported downstream behavior can become inconsistent. Before spending, assign each event a producer, recipient and deduplication method, and name the conversion-value owner. Test actual transactions and restores, not just button taps.

### 6. ATT consent and privacy-preserving attribution are different systems

Apple requires ATT permission for tracking across companies' apps/sites; hashing an email does not create an exemption. Apple's attribution APIs can operate regardless of ATT authorization, with privacy-limited postbacks. Sources: [Apple privacy FAQ, S06](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/) (undated; captured 2026-10-16); [AdAttributionKit, S07](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit) and [SKAdNetwork, S08](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/skadnetwork) (undated; captured 2026-10-16).

**Practical consequence:** a person declining tracking is not a reason to discard every aggregate conversion signal. It is also not permission to replace IDFA with fingerprinting or an uploaded identifier. Confirm actual SDK collection, consent behavior, privacy manifests and App Store disclosures. Installing a vendor's SDK does not transfer the developer's responsibility to that vendor.

### 7. ExcerLock's movement data must stay out of the ad signal design

Apple prohibits advertising/marketing use of HealthKit and Motion & Fitness data, and prohibits using installed-app information for advertising analytics. Meta's app optimization documentation also says not to share sensitive user data. Sources: [Apple Review Guidelines §§5.1.2–5.1.3, S37](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy) (undated; captured 2026-10-16); [Meta optimization, S03](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-ads/optimizing-your-app-ad/) (2024-10-23).

**My recommendation for this app:** do not forward step totals, exercise completion, Health-derived events, gym locations or names of selected blocked apps. Restrict the initial ad schema to reviewed acquisition/subscription transactions. Inspect automatic metadata collection too: Meta documents collecting screen titles and app links by default. Source: [Meta SDK, S02](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-events/getting-started-app-events-ios/) (2026-10-16). A neutral-looking event name does not make prohibited underlying data acceptable.

### 8. “Broad” means fewer marketing guesses, while keeping real eligibility limits

Broad acquisition removes assumptions such as “only people tagged as gym enthusiasts.” It still needs the right store geography, app/OS, age eligibility and event. Marcus Burke recommends consolidating subscription-app campaigns rather than splitting early spend among many interest audiences. TikTok's AEO instructions recommend automatic broad targeting. Sources: [Burke/Sub Club, S32](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/marcus-burke-sub-club-podcast) (April 3, updated June 6, 2024); [TikTok AEO setup, S11](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-set-up-an-app-event-optimization-campaign) (September 2026).

**Reasoning:** splitting a small budget creates sparse observations in each segment; the creative and selected conversion event already help the system find relevant people. This is a starting hypothesis, not proof that interests never work. TikTok's own interest guide recommends relevant interests when audience knowledge is weak. Source: [interest targeting, S29](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/interest-targeting) (July 2025).

### 9. Meta's audience suggestions are not guaranteed boundaries

Meta's streamlined campaign setup brings budget, audience and placements together under Advantage+. Jon Loomer's firsthand UI walkthrough shows controls versus suggestions, including expandable age/gender inputs and a route to further limit reach. His examples are mainly Sales campaigns; do not assume every App Promotion account exposes identical controls. Sources: [Meta announcement, S35](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2025/02/impulsione-o-desempenho-da-sua-campanha-com-os-novos-recursos-de-ia-do-advantage-e-recomendacoes-testadas-e-comprovadas/) (February 6, 2025); [Loomer setup, S33](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-campaign/) (June 23, 2025); [Loomer targeting observations, S34](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-campaign-setup-targeting/) (May 5, 2025).

**Operational rule I propose:** record each audience field as “hard control,” “suggestion,” or “unavailable.” If the test requires women only, find a genuine gender restriction in the actual app campaign flow. If unavailable, explicitly choose between a different eligible setup and a broader test. Do not describe a suggestion-based campaign as women-only.

### 10. TikTok also distinguishes automatic guidance from custom targeting

The upgraded Smart+ targeting page puts location, minimum age, language and audience exclusion under controls; gender, interests and broader age guidance are suggestions. Custom targeting exposes additional controls. Source: [Smart+ targeting, S13](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-targeting-for-your-upgraded-smart-experience) (July 2026).

This is generic upgraded Smart+ documentation; confirm availability in an **iOS App Promotion** campaign. For manual targeting, combinations across dimensions narrow the audience together, whereas alternatives within a dimension expand it. Source: [targeting logic, S14](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/ad-targeting) (May 2025). **My inference:** combining female, a narrow age range, several device conditions and niche interests can starve delivery even if each selection seems individually reasonable.

### 11. “Women 16–30” cannot simply be entered as the same audience on both platforms

Meta's teen policy removed gender targeting and limits teen personalization to age and location. TikTok restricts gender, audiences and interests for underage delivery in covered regions including North America. Sources: [Meta teen policy, S30](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/01/age-appropriate-ads-for-teens/) (January 10, 2023); [TikTok under-18 restrictions, S16](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-advertising-to-people-under-the-age-of-18) (September 2026).

TikTok's standard age groups include 18–24 and 25–34, so selecting both yields 18–34, not 18–30; the teen band also does not isolate ages 16–17. Source: [TikTok age/gender, S15](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/age-and-gender-targeting) (March 2025).

**My judgment:** begin with adults, proposing women 18–30 on Meta where enforceable and women 18–34 on manual TikTok. This changes the brief's paid test audience, not the product's intended audience. If we choose automatic expansion, label the campaign accordingly and examine outcomes by demographic where reporting permits.

### 12. Weight-loss positioning changes which audience is eligible

TikTok requires weight-loss or muscle-gain claims to target adults and disallows harmful or exaggerated claims and body shaming. Meta's teen policy likewise excludes weight-loss products/services from underage ads. Sources: [TikTok weight/body-image policy, S17](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-weight-management) (September 2026); [Meta teen policy, S30](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/01/age-appropriate-ads-for-teens/) (January 10, 2023).

**My recommendation:** start with the demonstrable promise—chosen apps become available through the selected movement condition—rather than a fat-loss result. Recheck the ad, store listing and any intermediary page together. Adult targeting does not make an unsupported physical-outcome claim acceptable. Whether the platforms classify ExcerLock under a restricted health/weight category remains unknown.

### 13. iOS-only is a product compatibility decision, not an optimization trick

For an iOS app, select the iOS listing and supported devices/OS. Android inventory cannot install an iOS app. Meta's integration setup requires the correct bundle and store IDs; TikTok distinguishes iOS and Android app campaigns. Sources: [Adjust app registration, S04](https://help.adjust.com/en/article/set-up-an-app-in-your-facebook-developer-account) (undated; captured 2026-10-16); [TikTok Smart+ App, S12](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026).

**My judgment:** do not add Android to make installs cheaper. Do not split by iPhone generation, carrier, connection speed or device price initially. Restrict OS versions only for actual build compatibility and verified campaign requirements. TikTok's documented dedicated setup is needed for its modern iOS install flow; setting a device filter is not a substitute. Source: [dedicated setup, S10](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-an-ios-14-5-dedicated-campaign) (February 2025). The precise device/OS controls available in ExcerLock's account remain unverified.

### 14. Placement recommendations are specific to the campaign route

Meta's consolidated UI defaults to automated placement distribution; Loomer documents how device and placement settings can be buried in additional settings. **My default:** start with eligible Advantage+ placements, make suitable creative variants, and assess subscriber outcomes before excluding placements. Source: [firsthand UI walkthrough, S33](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-campaign/) (June 23, 2025).

TikTok's Smart+ App overview lists iOS delivery on TikTok and, where available, Lemon8, with automated placement selection. Manual AEO can use selected placements; App Profile Page AEO instructions recommend TikTok-only because other placements may deliver a different experience. Sources: [Smart+ App, S12](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026); [AEO, S11](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-set-up-an-app-event-optimization-campaign) (September 2026); [Profile Page setup, S23](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-add-an-app-profile-page-to-a-campaign) (May 2025). “Always use automatic placements” is therefore too imprecise.

### 15. Exclusions and lookalikes require usable signals

A custom audience represents known people; a lookalike finds people similar to a source audience. Neither repairs a weak or biased seed. TikTok requires at least 1,000 matched users to use a Custom Audience in an ad group; exclusions and lookalike creation are documented use cases. Source: [Custom Audiences, S27](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/custom-audiences) (November 2025).

**My recommendation:** initially use broad acquisition; exclude existing subscribers/installers only through supported, permissioned, sufficiently populated mechanisms. Record the audience's lookback and status. Do not promise perfect removal of all existing users. TikTok documents enhanced reinstall exclusions through its SDK, showing why integration matters. Source: [TikTok SDK, S19](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-the-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (September 2025). Later compare a payer-quality seed against broad under a controlled test; do not build many lookalikes from an unrepresentative handful of users.

### 16. Value optimization is a later goal, not an automatic upgrade

Value optimization needs accurate purchase amounts and currency. TikTok's current app VBO guide explicitly includes iOS and lists purchase-event eligibility, while its Smart+ App overview still says VBO is Android-only. Sources: [VBO setup, S25](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-promote-an-app-using-value-based-optimization) (September 2026); [VBO overview, S26](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/value-based-optimization-app) (May 2026); [Smart+ App, S12](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026).

**My inference:** technical eligibility does not establish adequate learning volume. Start with a reliable trial or paid-event goal if the budget supports it; otherwise use a clearly bounded install diagnostic. Move to value after verifying actual availability and meaningful revenue signals. Do not extrapolate a year's subscription value from a trial event and present that as realized ROAS.

### 17. An allowed budget is not a sufficient learning budget

TikTok's budget page says a campaign-level daily budget must exceed $50 and an ad-group daily budget must exceed $20. These are platform thresholds for the corresponding budget fields, not performance guarantees. Source: [budgets, S28](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget) (July 2026).

Its Smart+ App guide recommends at least 10 times historical CPA daily under Maximum Results, at least 4–6 initial assets and a learning period of at least seven days. The AEO guide instead recommends at least 20 times historical CPA for upper/mid-funnel events and 10 times for lower-funnel events. Sources: [Smart+ practices, S21](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/best-practices-for-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026); [AEO setup, S11](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-set-up-an-app-event-optimization-campaign) (September 2026).

**My interpretation:** use the guide matching the actual campaign and event; do not add these multipliers together. ExcerLock has no verified historical CPA in this research. A small test can diagnose tracking and creative response without being sufficient to establish subscriber economics.

### 18. Setup is complete only when the whole path is verified

A correctly configured campaign dropdown does not prove the store, app and billing system work. Meta provides event-testing tools. TikTok distinguishes debug/test pipelines from production reporting, and its Events Manager distinguishes event receipt from attributed events. Sources: [Meta SDK testing, S02](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-events/getting-started-app-events-ios/) (2026-10-16); [TikTok integration, S20](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-integrate-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (March 2026); [TikTok event reporting, S18](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/view-manage-app-data-events-manager) (February 2025).

**Proposed acceptance evidence:** a destination preview reaches the correct listing; install/open works on a supported device; trial and payment logs agree with transaction records; tracking-denied behavior works; no duplicate event or sensitive payload appears; test modes are off in the production build. Sandbox transactions establish implementation behavior, not advertising profitability or production attribution.

## Step by step: prepare a first Meta app campaign

This is a proposed configuration for later approval, not something created in an account. Labels vary by rollout. Technical setup follows [S01](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-ads/) (April 14, 2026), [S02](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-events/getting-started-app-events-ios/) (October 16, 2026), and the concrete Adjust route in [S04](https://help.adjust.com/en/article/set-up-an-app-in-your-facebook-developer-account) and [S05](https://help.adjust.com/en/article/set-up-facebook) (both undated; captured 2026-10-16).

1. **Confirm the product can accept traffic.** Verify the live iOS listing, target-country availability, supported OS/devices, subscription products and working onboarding. The repository's product description does not establish current release status.
2. **Map the assets and permissions.** Confirm the business/ad account, billing currency/time zone, Facebook Page, Instagram identity and developer app are owned/accessible to the right people. Do not confuse the Meta developer App ID with the App Store numeric ID or bundle ID.
3. **Register/associate the app.** Add the iOS platform and correct bundle/store IDs in the developer app, associate the authorized ad account, and resolve any live-mode, verification or review requirements shown for this integration. Adjust documents its expected registration/review sequence; this research does not establish universal review requirements for every account.
4. **Connect events through the chosen route.** Implement native Meta App Events or an MMP connection. On Adjust's route, connect the partner in Meta Events Manager, map subscription events, enable the network, and avoid duplicate SDK logging. Set up the appropriate SKAN conversion mapping and AEM integration. Adjust documents automatic MAI support after partner/event setup and additional linking steps for re-engagement; do not apply re-engagement instructions indiscriminately to acquisition.
5. **Test and release.** Review consent/privacy settings, event payloads and conversion ownership. Obtain both test evidence and normal event receipt from the distributed build. Confirm the intended trial/payment event is eligible to select.
6. **Create → App promotion.** Use the streamlined Advantage+ creation flow if presented. Select the actual iOS app/store destination in the app/ad-set settings. Do not switch to Sales/website merely to imitate a tutorial.
7. **Choose the performance goal.** Proposed first choice: trial event optimization if reliably received, selectable and plausibly fundable. If trials are unavailable or too sparse, use install optimization for a bounded diagnostic and record that it answers a narrower question. Leave value optimization and aggressive cost/ROAS targets for later evidence.
8. **Use one campaign and one ad set initially.** Proposed name: `EXL_META_US_IOS_TRIAL_BROAD_202610`. Use a daily campaign budget and a start/end review plan; start with the default volume-oriented bid rather than an invented cost target. This structure and naming are my proposal, not mandatory Meta settings.
9. **Set audience constraints explicitly.** Proposed location: US if the listing and support are ready. Minimum age: adult. For a strict audience experiment, enforce women 18–30 wherever the app flow supports it; inspect whether the fields are suggestions. Otherwise document an adult broad campaign with women/age as guidance. Leave interests and lookalikes empty initially. Record existing-user exclusions if supported and populated.
10. **Confirm iOS compatibility and placements.** Select the iOS app, inspect device/OS delivery settings and align with the build's minimum OS. Start with eligible automated placements. Preview vertical and feed variants; ensure text and the actual app demonstration remain legible.
11. **Build ads and reporting columns.** Use the correct Page/Instagram identity; attach cleared creative and a valid store destination. Record the attribution window offered and selected. Show spend, impressions, CPM, outbound/link CTR with its definition, installs, trial starts, paid events, revenue and acquisition costs. Inspect demographic/placement breakdowns where available; do not assume all attributed revenue is immediately observed or incremental.
12. **Prepare a review packet, then seek launch approval.** Include configuration screenshots, destination, event-test proof, creative previews, spend limit and owner of daily monitoring. The captain must approve publishing/spending; this research does not authorize it.

## Step by step: prepare a first TikTok app campaign

Official navigation comes from [App Promotion usage, S24](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-app-promotion) (October 2025), [AEO setup, S11](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-set-up-an-app-event-optimization-campaign) (September 2026), and [SDK integration, S20](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-integrate-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (March 2026).

1. **Register/connect the iOS app in Events Manager.** Use the actual App Store listing. Choose the approved MMP or direct App Events SDK route. For the latter, create/use the app data source and obtain its TikTok App ID/secret; keep secrets out of screenshots and research notes.
2. **Implement events and attribution.** Map the subscription contract, check purchase auto-logging, assign the SKAN conversion-value owner and review consent. Hybrid SDK+MMP is documented, but do not let both own conversion updates.
3. **Verify test and production signals separately.** Debug events do not populate normal reporting. Ship the integration and verify normal receipt before considering paid delivery.
4. **Campaigns → Create → App Promotion → App install.** “App install” here is the acquisition campaign type; it can still optimize for an in-app event. Select the iOS app and the dedicated modern-iOS configuration when offered. Old dedicated-campaign guidance says it requires verified SKAN/MMP support; reconcile this with the newer SDK-only route in the actual product rather than concluding that SDK-only is impossible. Sources: [S10](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-an-ios-14-5-dedicated-campaign) (February 2025), [S20](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-integrate-tiktok-app-events-sdk) (March 2026).
5. **Choose one of two routes deliberately.** For an initial adult women audience test, my preference is manual/custom targeting with no interest stack. If using Smart+ App, confirm the particular upgraded targeting controls, supported event and iOS reporting eligibility; do not assume the generic Smart+ controls are all available. Source: [Smart+ App, S12](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026).
6. **Keep the structure consolidated.** Proposed name: `EXL_TT_US_IOS_TRIAL_BROAD_202610`; one campaign and one ad group to begin. Proposed manual audience: US, female, 18–24 plus 25–34; no interests/lookalikes, and no unnecessary device-price/carrier filters. Use a real adult minimum control for an automated route; maximum-age/gender suggestions do not establish the manual audience above.
7. **Select placements for the route.** For the proposed manual App Profile Page AEO test, choose TikTok-only where offered. Under Smart+ App, accept only the placements the product actually supports and record them; do not promise a TikTok-only switch if it is unavailable.
8. **Set budget, schedule and goal.** Select In-app event and the mapped trial event when eligible and affordable, otherwise Install for a diagnostic. Start with Maximum Results; do not invent a tight CPA bid without history. Use a daily budget, verify the account's displayed minimum and schedule in its recorded time zone. Avoid dayparting initially. Budget choice is a judgment; platform minimums and learning guidance are in point 17.
9. **Prepare the destination and identity.** Create/select the App Profile Page if the route calls for it; review automatically imported store information. Use either owned/licensed non-Spark ads or properly authorized Spark posts. The current Smart+ App overview says Spark and non-Spark cannot be mixed in one campaign. Source: [S12](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026).
10. **Preview and review automation.** Check app links, CTA, overlays, translations, generated carousels and any automated creative additions. Do not assume an auto-generated variant retains a truthful product demonstration. Use only creatives and creator permissions cleared for paid usage.
11. **Prepare reporting and approval.** Record event-counting settings and attribution windows; separate total received events from ad-attributed events. Review spend, installs, trials, paid conversions, revenue and the delay before judging them. Put the draft, tracking proof and spend limit before the captain before submission.

## Disagreements and changes to watch, 2024–2026

- **Meta's old campaign-choice screenshots are stale.** Meta announced streamlined Advantage+ setup in February 2025; Loomer's June 2025 walkthrough shows the resulting flow. Follow the current objective and actual controls rather than hunting for an old separate manual/automated entry screen. Sources: [S35](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2025/02/impulsione-o-desempenho-da-sua-campanha-com-os-novos-recursos-de-ia-do-advantage-e-recomendacoes-testadas-e-comprovadas/) (2025-02-06), [S33](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-campaign/) (2025-06-23).
- **Generic controls do not establish app-specific controls.** A historical Loomer observation even showed account-wide audience controls excepting Advantage+ App campaigns. I read that page as a reason to verify app delivery settings, not proof of the current exclusion. Source: [historical UI observation](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/account-wide-audience-controls/) (undated; screenshot dated April 2024; retained in raw, included as S38 in the source table).
- **TikTok Smart+ is changing, and its own documents conflict.** August 2026 Smart+ App documentation says iOS VBO is unavailable; September 2026 VBO instructions include iOS and suggest Smart+ if offered. The newer, narrower VBO instructions justify checking availability; they do not prove universal rollout. Sources: [S12](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns), [S25](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-promote-an-app-using-value-based-optimization).
- **“MMP required” is no longer a safe blanket claim for TikTok.** February 2025 dedicated instructions mention an MMP; March 2026 integration instructions explicitly describe direct SDK and hybrid connections. Sources: [S10](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-an-ios-14-5-dedicated-campaign), [S20](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-integrate-tiktok-app-events-sdk). Verify the new route instead of following only the old prerequisite list.
- **Apple now directs developers toward AdAttributionKit, while network documentation still uses SKAN.** Apple's SKAdNetwork reference points to AdAttributionKit and interoperability. This is not evidence that a marketer should switch off existing SKAN integration. Sources: [S07](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit), [S08](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/skadnetwork) (undated; captured 2026-10-16).
- **Broad versus interests is not unanimous even within TikTok.** Its AEO guide recommends automatic broad targeting; its interest guide recommends relevant interests for exploration. My first-test choice is broad with real eligibility limits, followed by a funded comparison if needed. Sources: [S11](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-set-up-an-app-event-optimization-campaign) (September 2026), [S29](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/interest-targeting) (July 2025).
- **An older operator's demographic preference is not ExcerLock's product strategy.** Burke's 2024 summary recommends older audiences and discusses SKAN 3. Its consolidation reasoning remains useful; I would not adopt its under-30 exclusion or treat its numeric SKAN heuristic as a universal current threshold. Source: [S32](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/marcus-burke-sub-club-podcast) (April–June 2024). Apple documents privacy-dependent data tiers, not a universal install count guaranteeing detailed postbacks: [S07](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit), [S08](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/skadnetwork) (captured 2026-10-16).
- **“Better attribution” vendor claims do not override Apple.** Moscon's enhanced-campaign article promotes probabilistic matching and enriched identifiers. Those claims are commercially interested and were not independently validated. Hashed identifiers and server APIs do not exempt app-collected tracking data from ATT. Sources: [S36](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/enhanced-app-campaigns) (March 2026), [Apple privacy, S06](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/) (captured 2026-10-16).

## Concrete starting recommendations for ExcerLock — my judgment

These are a proposal for approval after readiness checks, not permission to spend.

| Decision | Proposed start | Reason / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Funnel | Direct app promotion to the verified iOS listing | Minimizes the number of new systems required for the first experiment. |
| Geography | US only if launch/support/store availability are ready | A clean initial cohort; the geo lane should revisit country economics. |
| Structure | One campaign, one audience/ad set or ad group per platform when that platform is tested | Consolidates sparse feedback. Avoid separate campaigns for walking, gym, each interest and each creative. |
| Audience | Adult women; Meta 18–30 if enforceable; manual TikTok 18–34 | Keeps the first test near the intended audience while acknowledging platform age bands. Use explicit labeling if automation expands it. |
| Optimization | Confirmed trial if verified, eligible and affordable; otherwise a limited install diagnostic | Avoid buying cheap installations and calling them subscribers. |
| Bid | Default volume-oriented/Maximum Results initially | No verified historical CPA exists to support a cap or ROAS target. |
| Budget | **$75–$150/day on one platform for an initial seven-day window: $525–$1,050 total** | My planning range, not a forecast or learning guarantee. Test platforms sequentially if total funds are limited. |
| TikTok budget qualification | Increase the range or defer the trial test if event costs imply insufficient volume | Example only: at an assumed $15/trial, the official 10× guidance implies $150/day; at $30/trial it implies $300/day. Arithmetic examples, not ExcerLock benchmarks. [S21](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/best-practices-for-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026). |
| Creatives | **4–6 distinctly different assets**, with platform-appropriate crops | My proposed first batch, consistent with TikTok's asset guidance; it does not establish the right Meta count. [S21](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/best-practices-for-smart-plus-app-campaigns) (August 2026). |
| Exclusions | Only usable, permissioned existing-customer/install audiences; otherwise record unavailable | An empty or unmatched audience cannot establish clean acquisition. |
| Next experiment | One meaningful audience or event change after data matures | Do not simultaneously change creative, paywall, event, audience and bid. |

**Creative directions to produce first, not finished scripts:** a real phone demonstration of choosing an app and walking to access it; an adult creator showing the urge to scroll before a walk; a verified step-goal lock/unlock demonstration; a verified gym-arrival demonstration; a founder explanation with the actual screen recording. Use the current build and only the condition the video proves. Make alternatives from approved creator assets only when paid usage rights cover them. Product behavior is owner-reported in [`../../../core/product.md`](../../../core/product.md), not freshly tested here; gym, step and treadmill behavior must be verified before any claim is filmed. The creative lane should own final concepts and policy review.

**What to measure:** spend and delivery first; then cost per first open, confirmed trial, and new paying subscriber. Track install-to-trial and trial-to-paid by install cohort, plus realized subscription revenue and refunds. Record platform attribution windows and distinguish modeled/platform-attributed figures from subscription records. Keep the subscription conversion delay visible: a newly acquired free trial is not yet a failed paid conversion. No target CPI/CPA is defensible until price, trial length, conversion rate, retention and cash-payback preference are known.

**Before launch, stop and fix:** wrong destination or unsupported OS; missing production subscription events; double-counted purchases; a trial recorded as paid revenue; Health or location data leaking into payloads; an adult minimum that is merely a suggestion; missing paid creator rights. These are readiness failures, not targeting experiments. After launch, pause for tracking faults or the approved spending limit rather than spending more solely to make a learning label disappear. Launch remains subject to the captain's approval.

### Connect this to the existing operations system

Propose an independent paid-acquisition module owning campaign drafts, event-readiness evidence and spend/result imports. Use a composition adapter **outside the sections** to surface its launch approval cards in the existing creator dashboard's generic inbox. The adapter can link approved creator asset IDs and paid-use terms to campaign/creative IDs and return trial/subscriber outcomes to creator performance reviews.

Keep movement data and user-level identifiers out of this interface. Suggested record: platform, campaign ID, creative/creator asset ID, country, objective/event, control-versus-suggestion snapshot, rights expiry, approved limit, spend, installs, trials and new payers with attribution/cohort labels. A scheduled read-only import can flag expired usage rights, missing events or spend approaching the approved ceiling; publishing and budget increases still require approval. This reuses the approval inbox and feedback cycle described in [`../../../sections/creators/README.md`](../../../sections/creators/README.md) without making the creators section own paid acquisition or creating another manual tracker. Nothing in this proposal was implemented.

Still missing: the Meta Business Help article's full current app-specific targeting UI, a substantive Meta app-targeting reference (the fetched targeting URL yielded only a title), the TikTok device-targeting page, and the proposed older app-tracking URL. These failed/thin reads do not count toward the 39. The AppsFlyer Instagram page was only seen in search extraction and is not used as implementation evidence. The TikTok quota page was additionally read and saved (`web-open-17.txt`), but is not used to prescribe campaign counts because this report recommends a consolidated start rather than using available quota.

Questions to resolve before a concrete launch configuration:

1. Is ExcerLock live and purchasable in the intended country, and what OS/devices does the shipping build support?
2. Which subscription/attribution tools are already installed? Who currently owns conversion updates? Can real trial/payment events be demonstrated without duplicate SDK/server reporting?
3. What are the price, trial length, retained revenue and acceptable payback window? What actual cash amount can the captain authorize losing while learning?
4. Does this Meta App Promotion account offer enforceable gender and maximum-age controls, or only suggestions? Which current Advantage+ generation is enabled?
5. Which TikTok iOS route is offered: manual dedicated, upgraded Smart+, direct SDK, MMP or hybrid? Are VBO, real-time reporting and requested placements actually available?
6. Does platform classification of ExcerLock restrict events, optimization or health-related creative? Has automatic metadata collection been checked against actual screens?
7. Is a strict women-only test necessary, or should the campaign prioritize subscriber economics across eligible adults? This is a business decision; “creative aimed at women” and “delivery restricted to women” are different experiments.
8. Are usable exclusion audiences available, with the permissions and matched populations required? Is there enough payer-quality history for a later lookalike test?
9. Do creator contracts authorize these specific paid placements and identity uses? Can campaign outcomes be joined to the asset without sharing sensitive user data?

No independent source can answer those account/build questions from public documentation. The next useful work is a read-only readiness audit of the real app and account settings, followed by a reviewable launch proposal for the captain.
