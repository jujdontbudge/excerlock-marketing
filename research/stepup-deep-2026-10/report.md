## The company

StepUp is a free social pedometer: friends, family, coworkers and communities compete on steps, chat, cheer and taunt one another. Its origin story is a founder wanting an engaging step app for his mother and siblings. [Official site](https://thestepupapp.com/), captured 2026-10-12. His [historical growth essay](http://www.pratikstephen.com/2015/07/notes-on-growth-from-facebooks-growth.html), captured 2026-10-12, hypothesizes an “aha” moment when a leaderboard prompts someone to talk to a friend or walk more to beat them. It explains the product intent, without proving a measured activation threshold.

- **Operator and founder:** current terms/footer name **Marshmallow Toys LLC**. iOS seller is **Pratik Stephen**, with StepUp Labs copyright; Android uses **StepUp Inc**. Indexed founder/company LinkedIn identifies Pratik and associates Nikhilesh P. and Rohit Kowadkar; precise current staffing and roles are unresolved. Seattle appears in founder/developer context, not as proof of a staffed office. Bootstrapping and large active-user scale are founder claims without an audited cap table or user cohort. ## How it spreads (the engine)

1. **An organizer brings the people; the app supplies the shared scoreboard.** The founder's [viral-growth LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pratikstephen_stepup-my-social-step-challenge-app-went-activity-7204937787790274560-YuLl) thanks Ahlaam Ibraahim and links the unavailable original TikTok, saying she “may have kicked things off.” That is direct first-party attribution, cautiously phrased, not audited causality. A [Play review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thestepupapp.stepup&hl=en&gl=us) says “Only here cause of the Somalis,” consistent with the community pathway. Sources captured 2026-10-12. Later German, Tagalog, Somali, Nairobi and workplace examples show recognizable communities carrying the app; they do not establish geographic market share.
2. **Joining is an immediate social task.** Unique friend/group links, desktop QR and share copy tell recipients whom to join, with a fallback to tap again after installation or paste the invite into the app. Groups offer chat, cheers/taunts, custom challenge dates, rankings, Mystery Mode and exportable reports. The captured [store descriptions](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id979101825) advertise groups of up to **15,000 people** (2026-10-12); that current limit should not be projected back onto earlier creators’ overflow stories. Organizers receive ready-to-send guidance. Groups persist beyond a challenge; native team-vs-team scoring was not documented, with separate groups/exports suggested instead. Sources: [FAQ](https://thestepupapp.com/), [organizer guidance](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sWPuoR_GZgQHK2Ss7dRhtVwHq9U8Yt8TS1CVYQ_nOaM/edit?usp=sharing), [public invite](https://join.thestepupapp.com/nJq00b), captured 2026-10-12.
3. **The activity makes another piece of content.** Rankings create taunts, walking detours, chat screenshots, before/after receipts and recaps; organizers add certificates, celebration and prizes. [University of Tartu's challenge](https://sotsiaalteadused.ut.ee/en/event/university-tartu-step-challenge) supplies a current institutional example, with installation, permission, syncing and prize instructions. [Morenike's milestone](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeOwBUMhf3S/) then generates [another creator's celebration](https://www.instagram.com/p/DePaPY1MR-F/). Sources captured 2026-10-12. External prizes belong to organizers, not a verified app-owned cash/coin economy; [terms](https://thestepupapp.com/terms/) allocate prize responsibility accordingly.
4. **Comments become recruitment and support.** King's accountability invitation produces an overflow/error reply with alternate links; viewers post their own invites. Haki asks viewers to comment for a DM group link. Tutorial replies answer app-name and setup questions. These are observable joining paths, not evidence of affiliate commissions. Sources: the Organic records below, captured 2026-10-12. Brand encouragement appears in community comments despite its sparse own feed.
5. **Earned and paid distribution extend the same loop.** Founder LinkedIn packages community milestones and workplace use; indexed Reddit posts recruit groupmates, while YouTube tutorials address adding friends/groups. The confirmed paid creatives below recruit into an event, cause or creator challenge. No StepUp-owned creator network, formal paid ambassador program, install bounty or affiliate payout was verified. Invite-path identifiers are not discount codes. [Founder LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pratikstephen), [Reddit group request](https://www.reddit.com/r/Somalia/comments/1d9dfio/), [friend tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qqCCa2aAN50), [terms](https://thestepupapp.com/terms/), captured 2026-10-12; platform-access limitations above apply. Repeated personal galleries and similar layouts do not establish a centrally owned slideshow farm.

## What this means for ExcerLock (principles, not copies)

- **Make the consequence understandable before explaining features.** StepUp's best evidenced stories show what rivalry made somebody do. ExcerLock can show a specific moment when chosen-app access depends on movement, then the actual selected-app result. That is a hypothesis to test, not a StepUp leaderboard feature to import into ExcerLock's promise.
- **Put proof where the story needs it.** A readable lock → movement → unlock receipt can pay off the interruption. A tutorial can instead answer a real setup question. Use the current build and the particular condition demonstrated; [product rules](../../core/product.md) distinguish active walking, step targets and gym conditions. Do not turn a general step-counter screen or an edited overlay into proof of app access.
- **Give the viewer a usable next action.** StepUp's organizer invites connect interest to a named group. ExcerLock's creator handoff should make obtaining the app and trying the demonstrated condition equally clear. The transferable principle is low-friction participation; it does not require a social challenge, extreme step total or prize economy.
- **Let people and their own audiences carry the situation.** Real family/community stories and continuous selfies can travel without polished brand artwork or a prescribed cutting rate. StepUp does not establish the necessity of a controlled ambassador network. Treat unknown commercial relationships as unknown and select creators for audience fit rather than imitating an unverified account farm.
- **Build the next explanation from observed questions.** App-name requests, setup errors, compatibility and fairness concerns are evidence of what the first story left unresolved. An ExcerLock reply should demonstrate the answer to its own viewers' question; StepUp's wearable capabilities are not ExcerLock claims.
- **Measure interest, trust and use separately.** Negative commentary can earn views; paid distribution can inflate them; provisional Instagram cannot certify unpaid success. Keep those lanes distinct when deciding what to renew. Where a creator materially contributes, clear terms and reliable payments matter; Ahlaam's complaint is a reason to value the existing process, not a proven finding about StepUp's contracts.
