### Tool limits that affect interpretation

- **Organic TikTok:** relevance pagination sometimes returned empty despite indicating more results. Item/user/hashtag routes and later invite-code/commenter searches were bot-blocked. Public browser searches yielded shells/errors; early comment calls were empty and panels hit CAPTCHA before a working public endpoint recovered the confirmed threads. Returned comments exclude deleted/moderated material. Personal-account history was incomplete; captions/covers were inspected, not every full video. A blocked contextual video remains unreviewed. Private commenter profiles and unresolved aliases prevent a creator-network absence claim.
- **External search and Instagram:** Bing ignored some query restrictions; DuckDuckGo challenged automation; robots exclusions/timeouts reduced discovery. Initial Exa calls timed out, followed by bounded retries. Instagram public pages returned unusable shells despite HTTP success; Instaloader returned authorization/rate-limit messages after retries. ScrapeCreators/Apify were deliberately not used given known credit/auth failures; those tools supplied no additional coverage. One ads-agent fetch exposed candidate profile metadata, but no confirming bio/domain or post corpus. Direct Medium/AppBrain fetches were blocked; indexed extracts were used and cannot establish full-page coverage. APKPure was unavailable; archived Android screenshots required paid access. Apptopia exposed no usable current scale/revenue estimate.
- **Meta:** direct HTML was forbidden; the TLS-compatible collector worked. The CLI's all-country limitation was bypassed through the client API. Empty typeahead was corroborated with identifier searches, but no app-page ownership was established. Some structured rows preserve a last-seen cursor even where findings describe narrow enumeration as exhausted; they should not be treated as proof of global exhaustion.
- **TikTok libraries:** ordinary API calls were forbidden; compatible requests partly recovered access. CCL returned busy/rate-limit errors; full-history and future/end-of-day windows were rejected. An initial seconds-versus-milliseconds UI date error was discarded. Final browser retries returned no response/locator timeout, adding no coverage. Creative Center returned application-level “no permission” despite HTTP success.
- **Apple/Snap:** obsolete Apple REST paths were unsupported; invalid multi-country requests were discarded, including positive-control failures. Working per-country GraphQL calls recovered transient server failures. Snap's initial “search” screen/Enter key did not reliably issue a request; only actual API responses count. Rate-limited spellings remain missing.
- **Method:** public fetches and isolated browsers, no shared logged-in Chrome, paid scraping credits or Gemini API calls. Video transcripts used local tools and were checked alongside frames/captions. No new collection or runtime product testing was performed for this synthesis.

The strict ranks are [Organic, Promoted and Meta longevity](../README.md). Website/store/review facts and off-platform posts support identity, use cases and distribution mechanisms; they are not elevated into verified TikTok Organic performance evidence.

## What this means for ExcerLock (principles, not copies)

- **Give movement a consequence the viewer understands.** Social Steps makes another person's progress relevant to yours. ExcerLock can make access to selected apps the personal stake. Show the actual chosen-app lock and the verified movement condition that unlocks it, rather than relying on a general “be active” promise. This is a hypothesis from use-case evidence, not a transfer from a demonstrated viral Social Steps format.
- **Make product proof carry the mechanism.** A readable step receipt starts an accountability conversation but does not explain friend competition. An ExcerLock demonstration should visibly connect movement to app access; a caption-only explanation or generic progress total can leave the central promise unstated. Verify the particular condition in the current build, following [product facts](../../core/product.md).
- **Treat the CTA handoff as part of the creative.** Invitation comments reveal enthusiasm alongside link failures and device confusion. Before approving a creator draft, verify its destination and supported device, and make the next action understandable. App-name questions alone are not successful installs.
- **Use human accountability without inventing a social feature.** A creator can invite viewers into their own movement routine without claiming ExcerLock has leaderboards, friend races or app-funded prizes. Social Steps' organizer supplies the social structure outside the product; the principle is a concrete person/action, not copying their cash challenge.
- **Keep the evidence bar visible.** This collection offers a product-led recruiting mechanism and practical friction, but no proven high-reach creator format, Instagram benchmark or paid winner. Use it to define testable briefs, then judge ExcerLock's results through its own performance feedback. Do not turn an old user weight-loss caption into an app outcome claim.
