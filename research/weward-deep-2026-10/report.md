### Tool and interpretation limits

- **TikTok:** the research CLI failed with `KeyError: id`; seeded TikTokApi calls returned `EmptyResponseException`. Profile hydration/embeds exposed pinned/latest pages; `?page=2` repeated the first page. Individual yt-dlp downloads worked, profile iteration stalled/failed. Isolated headless Playwright showed “Something went wrong”; signed upload requests returned empty/non-JSON bodies. The [official repost feed](https://www.tiktok.com/@weward) yielded 25 creator posts with `hasMore=true`; later cursors failed. Direct, signed, curl_cffi and browser comment attempts recovered **no actual top comments**. Counts are available; sentiment, app-name questions and comment referral codes are unmeasured. Google challenges, noisy Bing/Exa results and Urlebird Cloudflare limited discovery. Guessed regional/founder/employee handles and name collisions do not establish ownership.
- **Instagram/web:** feed/reels/tagged routes returned empty content; APIs/Instaloader were unauthorized; isolated public rendering did not recover history. Individual captioned embeds worked, often with views/likes hidden. Mirrors' placeholder metrics were discarded. Historical Spain/Italy profiles were inaccessible, not proven deleted. X returned JavaScript shells; Reddit JSON was blocked, so thread evidence is indexed excerpts. A candidate Product Hunt URL returned 404. Several press/registry pages, including the B Corp registry, were blocked; Exa calls timed out. HTTP success alone did not establish usable content. The TikTok collector's direct Walk Mode/referral FAQ fetches were challenged; the web collector recovered Walk Mode's full help text. Referral terms remain indexed-only evidence.
- **Ad libraries:** CCL returned HTTP 421 “system busy,” region endpoint 429 and advertiser-request failures; its retention is one year after last delivery. Creative Center navigation failed and its HTTP 200 API body said code 40101 “no permission.” Initial blocked Snapchat requests were not counted as absence. The promoted TikTok's fresh CDN fetch returned 403; the paid collector reused the sibling collector's successful download with provenance. Meta's ALL/ACTIVE/INACTIVE queries occurred separately, so their status union is not a simultaneous live inventory.
- **Media analysis:** frames, subtitles, ffprobe and local Whisper supplied the existing reviews. Tiny English Whisper is unreliable for French, names and numbers; visible wording and native subtitles take precedence. The subscription Gemini CLI failed `IneligibleTierError`; no paid analysis API was used. Timings are approximate; automated scene-change rates are proxies, not exact cut counts; music was not independently fingerprinted. No shared Chrome or paid scraper credits were used.
- **Deduplication:** the paid collection has 658 platform IDs, including the same promoted TikTok counted in the organic folder; do not add the folders as unique campaigns. Its 47 video records cover 44 reviewed concept families. The wider 597 family identifiers include provisional, unreviewed static/asset groupings, not 597 proven ideas. Reach, impression ranges, ad-ID counts and collation counts are different signals; overlapping audiences and multi-variant aggregates are never summed.

The [evidence ranks](../README.md#evidence-ranks) stay separate: Organic is TikTok `isAd=false`, which does not prove the creator was unpaid; Promoted is `isAd=true`; Meta longevity is paid-use evidence, with a 30+ day threshold. Instagram remains provisional. Google measured disclosure and Apple analogous longevity are additional paid context, not new versions of Organic or Meta evidence. Public metrics do not reveal installs, CAC, ROAS or retention.

## Walk Mode: direct overlap with ExcerLock

WeWard is now more than a walking-rewards competitor. Its [July 8, 2026 launch](https://www.wewardapp.com/blog/walking-mode) and [current FAQ](https://faq.weward.fr/hc/en-us/articles/37952500463378-Discover-Walk-Mode), captured 2026-10-12, describe **blocking selected apps until a chosen daily step goal is reached**. This is primary product documentation, not a demonstrated current build.

- Users enter Settings, choose distracting apps and a step goal. Suggested thresholds are **3,000, 5,000 or 7,000**, or a custom number. Selected apps immediately gray out; reaching the goal automatically unlocks them; the rule resets at midnight. Sources: launch/FAQ above, capture 2026-10-12.
- It is optional and can be disabled manually before completing the goal. The FAQ says reinstalling, clearing cache or restarting does not lift the device-level block. **iOS only; Android planned.** No captured evidence establishes gym gating, active-walking-only access, mandatory use or a Premium requirement. No live permission/override walkthrough was performed.

## How it spreads (the engine)

1. **A walking entertainment account that people respond to.** The official English account's rating-walkers series makes ordinary street behavior a game; creators make their own “don't rate me” responses, which the brand reposts. [Cameron Roh's LinkedIn explanation](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cameron-roh-4a4938226_tiktok-weward-activity-7358945394799640576-lREY) identifies an in-house US social operator; [Kim Rosario's spotlight](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/wewardapp_employee-spotlight-meet-kim-rosario-activity-7395169739909574656-hx61) identifies French community management. Captured 2026-10-12. These substantiate employee-led official content, not ownership of lookalike personal accounts.
2. **Product habits create distribution material.** Daily step conversion, leaderboards, collectibles and charities supply receipts, reminders and events. [Friends Streak](https://faq.weward.fr/hc/en-us/articles/35060443268370-Friends-Streak-a-new-way-to-stay-motivated-together) allows **up to five friends**, each reaching and converting their target daily. [Communities](https://faq.weward.fr/hc/en-us/articles/27615276352402) have invite links/QR codes and shared standings; pooled community Wards were removed **2025-11-26**, so old pooled-donation material is not current behavior. [WeCards wishlists/trading](https://faq.weward.fr/hc/en-us/articles/29259275911058-WeCards-Wishlist-let-your-friends-know-which-cards-you-re-looking-for) extend into [Reddit swap communities](https://www.reddit.com/r/WewardWecardsSwap/). All captured 2026-10-12.
3. **Sharing and referral are coupled.** The [Olympus giveaway](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeJd6XGlxSx/) asks users to share an in-app step card to Stories and tag WeWard; the card includes a referral-code field. User-run referral photo accounts exist, but a brand-controlled slideshow farm was not established. The [referral FAQ](https://faq.weward.fr/hc/en-us/articles/360017901560-Referring-Your-Friends), indexed capture 2026-10-12 with direct fetch blocked, describes country/rollout differences. Do not collapse historic screenshots, user codes and indexed terms into a universal bonus.
4. **Press and celebrity supply credibility.** In the [September 2026 founder interview](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/yves-benchimol-weward-sub-club-podcast-2026), Yves says the first **four years** had no Meta/Google direct acquisition, with a roughly **€3,000–€5,000 monthly PR retainer** and a TV appearance producing **300,000 downloads in under five minutes**. These are retrospective founder claims, not recovered analytics; organic press need not be free. He says that playbook failed initially in the US. Venus brought athlete credibility, equity alignment and press/hiring access, with impact difficult to quantify. Capture 2026-10-12. The [YouTube Venus announcement](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fAhGLaIZS-E) has a rounded **1.9M tab views**, also captured 2026-10-12; paid contribution unknown, not Instagram views.
5. **Localized creator supply feeds paid acquisition.** [Ramdam](https://www.ramd.am/success-story/weward-global-expansion-at-less-than-one-dollar-cpi) claims expansion across **20+ countries, 10+ languages, CPI below €1 and 3× ad spend**; page copy uses euros despite the dollar headline. [Kroma's Alberico case](https://kromastudio.it/lavori/weward-campagna-influencer-marketing/) claims **3.2M views, 303K likes, 29.9K saves, 3K shares** and free-app rank **124→9**. Captured 2026-10-12. Exact campaign posts, paid amplification, dates and attribution are missing; neither joins the Organic ranking. The [Chief Walking Officer campaign](https://www.wewardapp.com/blog/weward-is-hiring-a-chief-walking-officer) offered **$10,000** for a **30-day** walking/content assignment with an **April 30, 2026** deadline, captured 2026-10-12. This was publicity/creator recruitment, not a current open salaried job.

## What this means for ExcerLock (principles, not copies)

- **App blocking is no longer an exclusive claim.** WeWard documents the daily step gate and markets attention recovery directly. ExcerLock needs a precise, visible account of its own chosen-app behavior and the condition demonstrated. Its [product record](../../core/product.md) also describes active-walking and gym conditions; verify the current build before treating those as a filmed differentiator.
- **Make the movement worth watching, then show the consequence.** WeWard's strongest captured official organic examples entertain through a social rule, specific place or human story. For ExcerLock, the natural receipt is the selected app becoming accessible after the demonstrated condition. The walk and receipt should belong to the same real sequence.
- **Distinguish spread of a premise from understanding of a product.** Rating-walker responses are strong diffusion evidence, but most do not explain WeWard. Track app recognition and qualified intent alongside shares; a memorable series needs a measurable bridge to the actual benefit.
- **Give creators a mechanism they can express in their own setting.** Localized paid creators vary scene, voice and format while preserving the walking-to-reward logic. ExcerLock can preserve its actual lock/move/unlock logic across creator contexts without copying the rating series, payout hooks or celebrity campaign.
- **Keep the promise verifiable and the action appropriate.** Reward ads attract economic skepticism; reminders and giveaways have different objectives from acquisition. ExcerLock's visible unlock avoids an unverified income promise, but only if the demonstration clearly explains which apps and exercise condition apply. No unsupported timing or exclusivity claim is needed.
- **Select references by their evidence.** Organic views/shares suggest creative diffusion; Promoted views show mixed distribution; Meta spans and EU reach show paid use/exposure; Google aggregates show scoped delivery. None supplies conversion economics. Supplier CPI claims can suggest an operational hypothesis, not a budget or expected result.
