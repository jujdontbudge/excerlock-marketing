# Pain funnel: what the X accounts say to put on each slide

Only what the sources say, with who said it. Not invented.

## The skeleton (@aleksascales, @ceonadz: same structure in both)
- Slide 1 hits a specific pain *visually*: "a photo of the problem" plus the line. Not "acne" but a photo of the problem with "no matter what I tried, gym sessions kept wrecking my skin".
- Slides 2–3 "twist the knife: more angles, more relatability, the viewer thinking this is literally me".
- Slide 4: solution, softly. "so I gave this a go, seen everyone using it."
- Slides 5–6: "show it working and the result". Last slide: one clean CTA.
- @LinoLeighton: "I always put the CTA on slide 4."

## Slide 1 image options
| Option | Who says it |
|---|---|
| A photo of the problem itself, matching the pain line | @aleksascales, @ceonadz |
| A close-up emotional face (crying/stressed). Same story: generic photo 650k vs crying close-up 3.3M | @ceonadz |
| Pretty person, nonchalant & casual photo | @shortformsavage ("pick pretty people", "nonchalant & casual images work best") |
| Mirror selfie of a fit girl (the diet account: "omg I could never eat like you", 2.3M / 1.1M) | @_mattwelter examples |
| Gym selfie meme ("you've changed", "I don't feel like going to the gym today"; Stronger app, up to 70.1M) | @_mattwelter |
| "Slightly wrong / absurd" AI image (skeleton at a register) for a "wait what" pause | @oganicboss |
| Image proves the claim (borrowed credibility: "twin sister is chic" → photo shows she is) | @ceonadz |

| Option | Who says it |

## Slide 4 (CTA) image
| Option | Who says it |
|---|---|
| 2 app screenshots + the App Store screenshot (logo + name), so viewers recognise it next time | @LinoLeighton |
| A screen that stops the scroll on its own; a widget showing a number/streak is "instantly legible" | @ceonadz |
| Show someone *using* it, a screenshot with a wow moment. "Make the user feel they found your app" | @ceonadz |
| Detailed dashboard screenshot as the soft sell (nutrition app, 2.3M) | @_mattwelter |
| Text: "I've been using this app AbMaxx to help me finally lose lower belly fat." | @LinoLeighton |

## Text rules on every slide
- Each slide under ~25 words, readable in 1–2 seconds (@LinoLeighton).
- Every slide gets its own hook; "step 1, step 2" kills the swipe (@ceonadz).
- Slide 2 must work as a hook on its own; 3–5× engagement (@adriansolarzz). Four ways: alternative angle, consequence, identity address, contrarian restatement.
- Write like you'd text a friend (@shortformsavage). Capitalise one word ("BEGGING") (@shortformsavage on "5 signs your body is BEGGING you…").
- Type text in TikTok, not baked in (@ceonadz, @aleksascales).
