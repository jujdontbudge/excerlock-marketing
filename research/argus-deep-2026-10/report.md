# Argus: how it markets (captured 2026-10-12)

## Coverage

This combines every `findings.md` in `raw/competitor-deep/argus/{organic-tiktok,instagram-web,ads}/`, using their `data.json` files for numerical records. Raw evidence and media stay in those gitignored folders. No new collection was performed for this synthesis. Collection counts describe these agents' observations, not the total inventory that exists.

**The strongest recovered Argus evidence is store positioning, historical distribution and a documented friend/challenge loop. There is no attributable TikTok performance sample or readable Instagram post sample. No current direct Argus acquisition ad was verified in completed library subsets. Blocked searches remain missing coverage.**

All source snapshots, counts and measurements below were captured **2026-10-12**, unless explicitly stated otherwise. Historical publication, release and ad-start dates are separate from capture dates. Stores are US/English unless specified. Discovery searched whole accessible history; TikTok/ads mark the recent window as **2026-07-14–2026-10-12**, while the Instagram/web assignment starts **2026-07-15**. Neither window has a complete social census. These scope dates and collection totals come from the respective JSON coverage records and request manifests.

## The company

[Argus](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id624329444) is **Azumio Inc.'s all-in-one health tracker**: automatic steps, food/calories, workouts, sleep, weight and health data, with friends and challenges. It is a mature utility/community product rather than a demonstrated current creator-led acquisition engine.

- **Team and funding:** founded in **2011** by Peter Kuhar and Bojan Bernard Bostjancic; the **2012 SkyHealth acquisition** brought Tom Xu into the combined business. The [current about page](https://www.azumio.com/about) lists Kuhar as CEO/CTO, Bojan Kahvedzic as COO, and Bostjancic/Xu as advisors. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/azumio-inc-/) gives Redwood City and an **11–50** company-size band, not audited staffing. A [primary announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/azumio-raises-25-million-in-series-a-funding-126243973.html) records **$2.5M Series A on 2011-07-27**, from Founders Fund, Accel and Felicis. Sources captured 2026-10-12; current funding/headcount beyond these disclosures are unverified.
- **iOS:** launched **2013-07-03**; **36,349 US ratings**, precise mean **4.8614**; version **5.458**, updated **2026-09-29**. Source: [Apple lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=624329444&country=us), captured 2026-10-12. [Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id624329444) title “Argus: Pedometer Step Tracker,” subtitle “Walking & Calorie Counter”; screenshot sequence moves from fitness goals to honeycomb data, friends' activity and Workweek Battle. Its **10M+ users** is a publisher claim, not MAU (same store/capture).
- **Android:** **500K+ installs**, **5,350 ratings**, scraped score **4.0434785**, header rounded **4.0**; phone subpanel shows **4.1**. Last displayed update **2024-07-15**; release metadata **2015-03-31**, distinct from the [2015-04-14 announcement](https://www.azumio.com/blog/azumio/argus-on-android). Source: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azumio.android.argus&hl=en_US&gl=US), captured 2026-10-12. The scraper's **776,432 realInstalls** is unofficial, not active users. Screenshot rating proof is reused marketing material, not the live Android rating.
- **Money:** the [iOS description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id624329444) advertises **$9.99/month or $29.99/year**. The [web premium page](https://www.azumio.com/apps/argus/premium) instead shows **$9.99/month or $59.99/year**, a **7-day** CTA trial and a **one-month** FAQ trial. All captured 2026-10-12. ## How it spreads (the engine)

1. **The product connects people.** [Support](https://support.azumio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034410311-How-do-I-add-friends) documents search/email invitations; [friends versus followers](https://support.azumio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034038632-What-is-the-main-difference-between-my-Argus-friends-and-my-followers) distinguishes reciprocal access from public following. [Sharing](https://support.azumio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034410411-How-do-I-share-my-activities-on-other-social-media-platforms) exports activity to other platforms. The [invite landing](https://www.azumio.com/openin/) asks users to install to see who invited them. Sources captured 2026-10-12. This is a documented social-to-install bridge, without measured conversion or a tested current flow.
2. **Challenges recruit through existing relationships.** The [2015 challenge announcement](https://www.azumio.com/blog/azumio/introducing-argus-challenges-and-audio-coach) describes **up to 9 participants**, a **5-concurrent-challenge** pack and premium expansion to **20 friends**; these are historical caps. A [trainer story dated 2015-09-03](https://www.azumio.com/blog/fitness/getting-back-to-shape) describes client adoption and **20-person** private-chat competitions, including informal **10-vs-10** teams. Sources captured 2026-10-12. The anecdote suggests trusted coaches can seed groups; it does not measure creator installs or establish formal team features then.
3. **A portfolio supplies cross-promotion.** The [current US publisher lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=439290207&entity=software&country=us&limit=200), captured 2026-10-12, returns **8 listings**: Argus, Sleep Time, Sleep Time+, Glucose Buddy, Glucose Buddy+, Calorie Mama AI, Instant Fitness and Food Lens AR. [Premium access](https://www.azumio.com/premium-access) bundles benefits, while [Android copy](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azumio.android.argus&hl=en_US&gl=US) promotes Sleep Time. Website navigation still includes historical products: [Fitness Buddy](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id514780106) is now Kodeon, and [Instant Heart Rate lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=409625068&country=us) names Bodymatter. All captured 2026-10-12. Historical ownership cannot authenticate current Argus creators or paid funnels.
4. **Stores and platform exposure provide discovery.** Keyword-rich step/calorie positioning is observable; acquisition share is not. [9to5Mac's historical coverage](https://9to5mac.com/guides/argus/), captured 2026-10-12, records **2013** M7/iOS featuring and **#1 free Health & Fitness** visibility. Apple's commercials below put Argus UI inside hardware marketing. Neither establishes present store ranking or that ASO is the main channel.
5. **Content, press and community campaigns broaden reach.** The [2015 blogger recruitment page](https://www.azumio.com/blog/azumio/guest-blogger) sought health/fitness writers. The [2015 anniversary giveaway](https://www.azumio.com/blog/azumio/celebrating-4-anniversary) asked Instagram/X followers to post portfolio-app photos with #azumioturns4, offering **10 battery packs**, **5 winners per platform**. Sources captured 2026-10-12. These verify historical contributor/engagement programs, without current contracts, compensation, affiliate codes, roster or measured installs. Historical [podcast discussion](https://bengreenfieldlife.com/podcast/biohacking-podcasts/episode-253-full-transcript/) covers coach access to client data; sponsorship is unknown.
6. **Research creates earned credibility.** [Stanford's 2017 coverage](https://med.stanford.edu/news/all-news/2017/07/counting-steps-via-smartphones-leads-to-clues-about-obesity.html), captured 2026-10-12, discusses **717,527 users across 111 countries**, a historical research sample rather than current adoption. A [competition paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.07437), captured 2026-10-12, reports about **23%** higher activity during competition. This is observational product evidence, not campaign performance or a promise ExcerLock can claim.

**The observable engine has several plausible channels, but no measured channel mix.** No current owned-creator/ambassador network was authenticated. A [publisher-hosted recovery story](https://www.azumio.com/blog/azumio/feature-spotlight), captured 2026-10-12, says the user encountered Argus “in an ad”; platform, payer and creative are unknown. It prevents interpreting present library gaps as “Argus never advertised.”

## What this means for ExcerLock (principles, not copies)

- **Show the product changing a decision.** Chase makes a leaderboard the reason a runner continues. ExcerLock can test its own causal sequence: a blocked chosen app, movement, then the real unlock. This is a hypothesis from an observed demonstration, not a proven acquisition format.
- **Make the receipt readable.** Argus's leaderboard/goal screens make motivation concrete. For ExcerLock, the equivalent is the actual blocked state and earned access, with a clear relationship between them. The celebrity, production set and exact challenge copy are not the principle.
- **Keep the first promise narrow.** Argus's broad utility supports store positioning, while dense tutorial navigation obscures a quick benefit. A creator's first frame should communicate a recognizable phone/movement problem; the app demonstration should answer that promise. Argus supplies no TikTok winner proving a particular hook.
- **Borrow distribution logic only where the product supports it.** Coaches and existing groups can seed a social challenge. ExcerLock's confirmed consequence is the user's own apps remaining locked until movement; a friend race or team referral feature cannot be implied from this report.
- **Align creative, listing and offer.** A clear movement-to-access promise should continue into the landing/store experience. Argus's public price/trial discrepancies are a reason to verify ExcerLock's offer, not evidence of an effective paywall strategy.
- **Keep tests and evidence separate.** Do not claim an Argus creator farm, current ASO dominance, high-performing health scare, or a profitable Argus Meta strategy from these records. Measure ExcerLock's own posts and acquisition before adopting any of these hypotheses as a rule.
