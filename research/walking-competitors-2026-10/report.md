# Walking apps with a hook: competitor coverage and size

## Coverage first

**Captured 2026-10-12; US App Store ratings by default; Google Play install bands are worldwide lifetime Android installs visible in a US/English listing.** Started from the 333-name competitor-universe report and the walking-apps-30d report. Those were discovery inputs only: their old size/social/ad numbers were not carried forward. Output and raw evidence are confined to `raw/walking-competitors/`; nothing was committed or changed in tracked files.

**Result: 104 admitted app identities/market variants, including Lockt via active-ad evidence and two near-rating-guide cases, plus one sub-threshold fast riser (WalkScape) below.** Stompers is shown separately as the benchmark. This is a broad, bounded census of verified candidates, not proof that every app in every country was discovered. An “unknown” field is a coverage gap, not evidence that the channel/platform does not exist.

### Sources, queries, dates and limits

- **Apple:** public [US Search API](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=walking%20game&country=us&entity=software&limit=100), country `us`, software entity, requested 100 results in first sweep and 50 in follow-up; exact seed-ID lookups. Saved **89 search responses** and **2,813 distinct iOS candidate records**. All numeric ratings here are from fresh Apple responses on 2026-10-12, or explicitly marked JP/KR/SG direct-page values. Search is relevance-ranked and can truncate; an empty lookup/blocked response was not interpreted as zero ratings. Later JP/KR lookup attempts returned non-JSON bodies; direct regional listing HTML supplied the marked regional counts instead. An initially wrong Dragon Quest iOS ID was discarded and corrected against Square Enix’s official release link.
- **Apple charts:** US top-free Health & Fitness (`6013`), Games (`6014`), Adventure (`7002`) and Action (`7001`), [RSS](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/topfreeapplications/limit=200/genre=6013/json). Requested 200, received **100 entries per chart**. Chart positions are a one-day discovery signal, not a user count. Found/check-confirmed StepUp, WeWard, Stompers, Sweatcoin, Scrambly, Steppa and Pikmin Bloom.
- **Google Play:** public search plus direct package pages, `hl=en`, `gl=US`; JP/KR-language searches also attempted. Saved **120 search responses**, **1,180 successfully parsed app detail records**, and **4 error records**. Requested 25–30 relevance-ranked hits per search. Search often returned unrelated namesakes; direct package/developer/description checks decided inclusion. The scraper’s unsupported `collection` import blocked automated Play category-chart collection; official [“Games that get you moving” editorial](https://play.google.com/store/apps/editorial?id=mc_editorialevergreen_games_to_get_you_moving_fcp) and indexed category/listing pages provided partial category discovery instead. No full Play chart census is claimed. Install bands are minimums, not exact downloads or monthly active users.
- **Keyword families:** walking game, walking adventure/RPG, virtual walk/journey/race, walking pets/characters, step rewards, walk earn/get paid to walk, step betting, step challenge, step counter friends, walk with friends, walk unlock/apps/screen time; exact brands Stompers, Sweatcoin, WeWard, Macadam, CashWalk, Winwalk, StepUp, StepBet, Walkr, Wokamon, Pikmin Bloom, WalkScape, Fantasy Hike, Genopets, STEPN, Step App, Walken, sMiles, Fitmint, Optimity, YuLife, charity/tree planting. Regional queries included `歩数 ゲーム`, `歩いて ポイント`, `トリマ`, `aruku&`, `おぢポ`, `楽天ヘルスケア`, `楽天シニア`, `dヘルスケア`, `プラリー`, `Coke ON`, `만보기 돈`, `캐시워크`, `걷기 리워드`, `步数 游戏`, `走路赚钱`. Exact machine-query strings and result counts are retained in `collect.py`, `extra.py`, `last-sweep.py` and the corresponding logs/search files.
- **Beyond stores:** searched “apps like Stompers”, “walking rewards apps Sweatcoin WeWard alternatives”, “best walking games”, store install-band searches, and founder/download milestone searches. Read publisher sites and help pages, Google Maps’ Dragon Quest publisher interview, relevant press, [Reddit walking-game discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/walking/comments/1wst3k8/best_apps_to_gamify_your_daily_steps_wokamon/), [walking motivation suggestions](https://www.reddit.com/r/walking/comments/1pvttrm/app_suggestions_to_stay_motivated_to_walk/), and reward-app comparison discussions. Reddit and app-authored “alternatives” lists generated leads; they did not establish size or payment reliability. Saved pages live in `sites/`; discovery search extracts in `search-*.json`.
- **TikTok/X discovery:** indexed TikTok/X queries and public profiles were searched alongside Reddit. [TikTok live keyword search](https://www.tiktok.com/search?q=walking%20game) showed a login-only search shell without results. [X live search](https://x.com/search?q=%22walking%20app%22&src=typed_query&f=live) redirected to sign-in. Those are explicitly missing live-search coverage; public profile data and indexed founder/social pages remained usable. No complete in-platform search or trend-feed census is claimed.
- **Social profiles:** followed verified outbound links from publisher sites, then fetched public Instagram/TikTok profile HTML. Saved **62 official/linked or candidate profile attempts** in `social-log.json`, plus a separate guessed-handle pass in `social-more-log.json`. Guesses were not promoted to verified brand accounts merely because a name matched. TikTok counts came from embedded profile JSON; Instagram counts from the profile’s own meta description and are often rounded (e.g. `1M`, `2M`). A retry overwrote some original profile HTML with empty/login responses; the original first-pass counts observed in this session were recovered in `social-first-pass-recovered.json`, with that raw-HTML limitation explicitly recorded per entry. All table counts are public-page captures on 2026-10-12; exact counts from old research or older analytics snippets were not reused. A publisher-wide social is identified by its handle and does not imply app-specific audience size. No verified outbound handle means **unknown**, not “no social account”.
- **Paid-service limits:** ScrapeCreators TikTok and Instagram profile calls returned **HTTP 402**; existing credits were unavailable to these requests. Apify account/usage calls returned **HTTP 401, invalid token**. Raw failures are saved; no credits were bought and no keys were printed. Public pages supplied the usable social snapshots.
- **Meta:** applied the ad-research skill: first inspected the official library in the collaborative browser, then ran **60 bounded keyword queries**, plus 19 smaller-app keyword checks and a Macadam page-ID pass. Country **US**, status **active**, all media, capture 2026-10-12; cap **15 records/query**, normally 1–2 pages. **Yes** only when the active record landed on the exact store ID or a verified publisher domain; e.g. Sweatcoin, WeWard, Stompers, Pikmin Bloom, Winwalk, AHEALTH, Perfit, Motiv, Scrambly and Conqueror. Many searches returned unrelated advertisers, and 15-hit caps could bury the real app. Thus no-match/zero keyword returns and the ambiguous Macadam page pass remain **unknown**, not “no ads”. Newly discovered regional candidates without a query are also unknown. Raw JSON/logs and positive ad IDs are retained. This establishes presence only, not spend or successful performance.
- **Availability/reward limits:** being listed and having historical installs does not establish active users, ongoing acquisition, available US rewards, or real payments. Reward mechanics are publisher claims unless otherwise stated. Prices are the observed business model; quoted prices come from current listing descriptions, and actual paywalls can vary by country/offer.

### Inclusion and ranking

Include a substantial walking/steps loop **and** an explicit extra hook. Daily goal rings, basic charts or a cosmetic profile alone do not qualify. Mixed products are included where walking is a central advertised loop (e.g. AHEALTH, Argus, Healthy 365, Trima); they are identified below. Location games Pokémon GO and Dragon Quest Walk are walking-adventure boundary cases, not passive step counters. This preserves broad coverage without treating every GPS, running, wellness or tourism app as a walking competitor.

**Ranking is provisional evidenced reach, biggest first:** use a sourced cumulative user/download claim or Play install minimum; break ties with US ratings. Apps qualified only by ratings follow the reach-backed group, ordered by US rating count. Ratings, downloads, signups and active users are different quantities: no values are added across stores or market variants, and this ordering does not imply exact market share. Steppa’s fast growth is founder-supported; WalkScape’s smaller but demonstrated growth is kept separately. GO Club’s 1,954 and StepBucks’ 1,832 US ratings are within the requested “about 2,000” guide.

Closeness is an analyst judgment: **Stompers high** = social step competition and/or a substantial character/game loop; **medium** = rewards, challenges or virtual journeys with fewer direct social-race mechanics; **low** = peripheral resemblance. **ExcerLock high** requires walking to unlock distracting apps/screen time; **medium** shares the walking incentive/goal loop; **low** is predominantly an outdoor/location adventure. Lockt is admitted via active-ad footprint and has a verified app-blocking loop, but its installed-user scale is unknown. The other direct feature competitors and Unrot’s broader habit gate are listed in the rejected/watch section.

## Rejected, deferred and boundary decisions

Rejection means failure to establish all requested criteria on this run, not a claim that an app is worthless, unavailable everywhere or permanently small.

### Plain counters / basic tracking / excluded prior-work set

- **StepsApp, WalkFit, Pedometer++, Pacer, Map My Walk, Stepz, Appenvision’s Pedometer - Run & Step Counter, Leap’s Pedometer & Step Counter, and ITO’s Pedometer α:** excluded as instructed from walking-apps-30d, including both alternatives for its ambiguous eighth row. Pacer does have challenges/virtual events, but the assignment explicitly excludes that prior set; it is not silently reintroduced. WalkFit’s marketing challenges do not override the exclusion.
- **MySteps, PaceFit, Steps - Activity Tracker, Stepwise, Track My Steps, Accupedo, Walking Distance Tracker/Vima, Step Counter Maipo, Steps+, Steps - Simple Pedometer, ZuStep, the Leap/ITO Android counters and numerous generic “Step Counter/Pedometer” listings:** tracking, personal goals, charts and cosmetic avatars do not by themselves provide the specified extra hook. Their store records remain in the raw candidate corpus.
- **Walkster, Walking by Slimkit, Walking Tracker by GetFit, Walk At Home, walking weight-loss/meal-plan apps, Walking Yoga:** walking workouts/training/weight-loss plans rather than a verified game/social/reward/unlock/virtual journey loop of the requested kind.

### Titles that overpromise the verified description

- [Pengo](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.iw.peng.walk.aos) (500K+), [WalkLite](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yispark.walk.lite.aos) (500K+), [StepReward](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.iwanmaket.walk.stepreward) (1M+), [StepRush](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jk.step.rush.aos) (100K+), [Mystep](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yispark.my.step.aos) (100K+), [WalkWin](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.walk.walkwin) (500K+): size clears the guide, but current descriptions predominantly explain plain counting. A reward-themed title or an active generic install ad alone does not verify the actual extra mechanic. Deferred pending product-screen/redemption verification. **WalkStep** is included separately because its current description expressly promises tangible walking rewards; its thinner mechanic evidence is disclosed.
- [Walk to Unlock: Walk Lock](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1423009524) has 2,307 US ratings, but its current description explains a weight-loss walking tracker rather than selected-app blocking; the title is not sufficient mechanic evidence.
- [Cash Step](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cashstep.pedometer.stepcounter) is **included as a simulation/competition**, not rejected, because its description verifies rankings/challenges; it expressly disclaims cash conversion. This prevents an implied “pays money” claim.

### Large but walking/steps are ancillary to the core

- **Strava, Garmin Connect, Fitbit/Google Health, Samsung Health, adidas Running, Nike Run Club, Runna, Runtopia, AllTrails, komoot, Wikiloc, HiiKER, Footpath, Kinomap, Peloton:** broad sports, running, hardware or route products; walking/steps are a supported activity rather than the product’s central hook. **Evidation** currently foregrounds research/health insights; its step Circles are adjacent rather than its core. **GymRats**, Challenges - Compete Get Fit, general workplace-wellness portals and habit trackers have broader activity commitments. **ChallengeRunner** (8,731 US ratings; verified Android package `com.ChallengeRunner.www`, 50K+ installs) supports step leaderboards but is a general employee health-challenge logging platform; rejected on the walking-core criterion, not because it is small.
- **Finch, Plant Nanny, Pocket Plants:** self-care/hydration or plant-game cores; walking/steps are optional inputs rather than the core. Pocket Plants has a real step-energy integration, so it is a useful adjacent game reference, not mistakenly “no walking feature”. **Walking Hero - Idle Battle RPG** currently describes an idle RPG, not real-world step-powered play; do not infer physical walking from its name.
- **Coke ON:** huge drink-purchase/vending-loyalty app with a walking stamp side feature, not a walking-core app. **Point Income, Cashmore, Fetch, Powl, Rakuten Super Point Screen:** offerwalls, receipt/shopping/survey rewards with walking as an additional input. **ANA Pocket**: transport/mobility-first including vehicles; less walking-centric than the included step-led Trima/Prally descriptions. These boundary choices are stated so a broader movement-rewards brief can reintroduce them.
- **WalknTours, GuruWalk, GPSmyCity:** guided tourism/booking products without a verified walking game/progression mechanic. **Questo** stays included because real outdoor movement solves game/story clues.
- **Ingress, Jurassic World Alive, Monster Hunter Now, Orna, location-property games:** geographical play/exploration, but the central verified loop is not step counts or walking-specific progression. Pokémon GO is admitted as a clear walking-adventure boundary case because eggs/buddies/routes create explicit walking-distance progress; include/exclude it consistently in any narrower step-only comparison.
- **Wag!, Rover, Sploot, PetBacker:** booking pet-care providers, not the user walking for a game/reward. **Walk Master, Steppy Pants, Silly Walks, Walking Dead titles, Horizon Walker, Star Walk, walking simulators:** namesakes or on-screen game actions, not real-world walking/steps.

### Sunset / unavailable evidence

- [Treecard](https://www.treecard.org/): current homepage is a closing/thank-you notice and directs walkers to Treekly. Old Walk to Plant pages and old app statistics remain online; excluded from the current competitor table, preserved as a historical marketing reference.
- [Sweat Wallet](https://help.sweatco.in/hc/en-us/articles/51460165943569-What-s-Happening-to-Sweat-Wallet-and-Sweat): a large companion historically, but the current support notice announces the wallet’s wind-down (December 2026). Excluded from the ongoing-app table; **Sweatcoin and its marketplace are explicitly unaffected**. It should be monitored as a sunset competitor until shutdown, not described as already shut down.
- **Lucky Step / WalkingJoy**: historical multi-million install claims exist, but exact current package checks returned 404 for the tested IDs. Different namesakes exist; no active same-identity app was verified. [Current unavailability record for Lucky Step](https://www.appbrain.com/app/lucky-step-walking-healthy/com.game.realmoneyplay.app). Historical download reports are not current install numbers.
