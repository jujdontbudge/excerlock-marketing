# Pikmin Bloom: how it markets (captured 2026-10-12)

## Coverage

This report combines the organic TikTok, Instagram/web and advertising collections in `raw/competitor-deep/pikmin-bloom/{organic-tiktok,instagram-web,ads}/`. Each folder's `findings.md` supplies the analysis; its `data.json` supplies post/ad numbers. Bulk evidence is gitignored. Collection was worldwide discovery from launch through **2026-10-12**, with stronger coverage of named profiles and retained libraries than of search-discovered mentions. It is not a global census or a ranking of every post ever published.

**Capture convention:** every source and metric below was captured **2026-10-12**, unless another capture date is explicitly stated. Publication, delivery and measurement-period dates are separate from capture. Counts in this section describe the saved collection, not platform totals. Source URLs accompany the tables and claims; per-item URLs, timestamps, null fields and requests remain in the three `data.json` files and their evidence archives.

### Tool and coverage limits

A failed fetch means **missing coverage**, never evidence of no posts, no ads or no activity.

- **TikTok discovery:** TikTokApi hashtags hit bot detection/EmptyResponseException; public search/tag pages returned shells, direct search and ordinary post-list endpoints returned empty. Creator item-list pagination worked. Exa sometimes timed out or returned irrelevant leads; discover crawls sometimes returned CRAWL_EMPTY_CONTENT. The web connector was robots-blocked; Google supplied JavaScript redirects, DuckDuckGo CAPTCHA, Jina unauthorized responses, Bing poorly constrained results and Tikwm challenge pages. Primary post timestamps replace misleading indexed publication dates. Uncaptioned creator mentions, deleted/private posts, historical sponsorships, regional accounts, bio-link destinations and full threads remain incomplete.
- **Profile gaps:** [@mia__lind](https://www.tiktok.com/@mia__lind) yielded **1,282 / 1,366** displayed posts; [@liezyboy](https://www.tiktok.com/@liezyboy) **1,611 / 1,803**; [@h4lo_0f_the_0c34n](https://www.tiktok.com/@h4lo_0f_the_0c34n) **41 / 69**; [@ezeekat](https://www.tiktok.com/@ezeekat) **2,068 / 4,823**; [@superplexis](https://www.tiktok.com/@superplexis) **813 / 821**. Other smaller mismatches and an inconsistent profile count remain in `organic-tiktok/accounts.json` and `findings.md`; matching counts mean current retrievable profiles, not deleted history. All ratios: linked profiles, capture 2026-10-12.
- **Instagram:** public feed/profile APIs returned 401, Instaloader hit schema errors and tagged pages supplied a shell. OG pages reveal dates/captions/likes/comments, not verified reel views. ScrapeCreators had no credits and Apify was unauthorized, so neither was used. Third-party Pictame view figures had caption/creator mismatches and blocked pages: excluded. No shares, saves, impressions, full founder/tagged feeds or verified boosting status. Local OG dates and UTC upload dates can differ; direct metadata supersedes rounded/stale OG counts where available.
- **Meta retention and identity:** page/name discovery covered corporate, regional, Nintendo, agency, fan and misleading names; discovered page-ID cursors were exhausted. Outside-brand keyword matches for merchandise, console games, spoofing tools and unrelated businesses are excluded. ALL-status does not restore expired history, especially inactive ordinary commercial ads outside Europe. The [Japanese page query](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=all&ad_type=all&country=ALL&view_all_page_id=108342894965927) returned no retained ads in that query; that is not lifetime absence. No exact spend, conversions, global reach or usable impressions. Numeric EU reach is parent-ad delivery, not asset-level performance.
- **Current TikTok ads:** CCL keyword/advertiser requests repeatedly returned 421 “system busy,” including isolated-browser attempts. Creative Center also failed. [CCL](https://library.tiktok.com/ads) keeps ads for one year after last delivery and its supported-region option does not imply complete US/Japan/global coverage. [Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en) is curated even when accessible. The earlier ads-side Spark scan used a smaller sibling snapshot than the final Organic dataset; both are bounded and neither verifies a promoted post. Pipiads cards lack original post/ad IDs and click destinations; cover identity is not guaranteed video identity. Its estimated impression totals can overlap or be inconsistent; no cost model is used.
- **Google:** **2,367 initial previews** remain unresolved; corporate UI/suggestion count ranges and retrieved RPC totals differ. Additional Nintendo/agency classification is unfinished. [Nintendo of America](https://adstransparency.google.com/advertiser/AR04688237127509475329?region=anywhere) pagination stopped after **15,800 mixed-game records** on 429; subsequent domain-filter requests failed. Those records are not Bloom counts. The longest-lived confirmed ad's media returned 403; a decoded preview is not a full-video analysis. Counts: saved Google discovery/previews, capture 2026-10-12.
- **Apple/Snapchat/other ads:** Apple has a documented reporting delay ([API documentation](https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/Ad-Repository.pdf)); retain the observed cutoff instead of inventing delivery dates. No public US Apple keyword census or comparable spend/impressions. [Snapchat](https://adsgallery.snap.com/) exact-name/legal-name queries found no identified Bloom ads; generic Scopely Explore/Niantic International variants remained rate-limited. Mixed-game Scopely previews were excluded. Its supported-country/time-window gallery cannot establish global lifetime absence. Reddit's ad library was not audited in these folders.
- **Off-platform:** Reddit JSON returned policy 403; X timelines were sparse/login shells. Appfigures was Cloudflare-blocked; Sensor Tower lacked usable estimates. Direct hiring URLs redirected to generic careers content, so indexed roles are not verified current vacancies. ENJIN's embedded video is private. YouTube subtitle requests hit 429 before successful video downloads. Correct quiz pages are JavaScript shells: scripts were saved, but no quiz/share flow tested. No full podcast audio analysis, historical price census, in-app onboarding or attribution audit.
- **Media interpretation:** scene-change detection is a proxy, not exact edit counts; beat times are approximate. English-only transcripts are unreliable for Japanese/Korean/Mandarin. The Instagram/ads analyses did not independently audition audio, so exact tracks and disputed spoken hooks are unverified. Only selected Meta videos, statics and low-delivery contrasts were visually reviewed; whole-corpus semantic-family grouping is provisional. No shared Chrome, paid scraping credits or image-generation API was used for these collections.

## The company

[Pikmin Bloom](https://pikminbloom.com/en/) is a walking and collecting game built by Niantic's Tokyo team with Nintendo. The current store seller is **Scopely Explore, Inc.** Scopely announced its purchase of Niantic's games business on **2025-03-12**, for **$3.5B**, and completed it on **2025-05-29**. This is a portfolio transaction, not Bloom's valuation or marketing budget ([announcement](https://www.scopely.com/en/news/scopely-to-acquire-niantic-games-business-which-includes-pokemon-go-one-of-the-most-successful-mobile-games-of-all-time), [completion](https://www.savvygames.com/news/scopely-completes-acquisition-of-niantic-games-business), capture 2026-10-12). The studio identity changed to Scopely Explore on **2026-07-06**, with the same Bloom team; Scopely describes it as a studio identity, while stores use the legal seller name ([Bloom announcement](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/introducing-scopely-explore), [corporate clarification](https://www.scopely.com/en/news/introducing-scopely-explore), capture 2026-10-12).

John Hanke founded Niantic; Tatsuo Nomura led the original Tokyo studio. Historical interviews identify engineering lead Hiroki Asakawa and marketer Kento Suga, then director/product lead Tomo Yamazaki, UX lead Akari Sora and senior product manager Rika Nakajima. They describe Tokyo-led development/marketing/operations and close Nintendo collaboration, not a verified current roster ([launch interview](https://k-tai.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1361575.html), [developer interview](https://www.nintendolife.com/features/pikmin-bloom-devs-on-the-joy-of-human-connection-and-the-absence-of-bulborbs), capture 2026-10-12). Exact current headcount, dedicated funding and MAU were not verified.

Launch rolled out locally on **2021-10-27** in Australia/Singapore, **2021-10-28** in the US, **2021-11-01** in Japan and **2021-11-02** elsewhere. The announcement's US date is earlier than some local releases ([Nintendo FAQ](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/56914/~/pikmin-bloom-faq), [launch news](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/blog-2021-1026), capture 2026-10-12).

Sampled reviews support that some users walk more for the collectibles, while reporting step-sync/loading failures and expensive or scarce resources. These are user accounts, not measured health effects or representative satisfaction rates.

### Movement rewards and app-blocking check

No app-blocking or selected-app walk-to-unlock feature was evidenced in the captured stores, official site/help, screenshots or creative records. Installed-app behavior was not tested. “Unlock” here concerns growing companions, decor and game rewards; it should not be described as phone access control.

The marketing makes that reward loop explicit: [“Walk and let Pikmin grow”](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=821093947654668) pairs walking feet with seedling progress; [routine destinations](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2698594350499441) turn errands and commutes into collectibles; [Mia's invitation](https://www.tiktok.com/@mia__lind/video/7511404365232508191) attaches steps to buddies and a joinable walk (all captured 2026-10-12). This is adjacent motivational evidence for ExcerLock, whose described mechanism is access to selected apps under an exercise condition ([canonical product](../../core/product.md)).

## How it spreads

1. **An outing produces something worth keeping or sharing.** Location-associated Decor gives a café, pizza shop or neighborhood walk a purpose. Flowers change the map; AR photos, postcards and lifelog memories turn the outing into a souvenir. The [pizza adventure](https://www.tiktok.com/@liezyboy/video/7478933735530908959) shows that loop changing a person's route, while virtual expeditions give fans a named character to care about. Capture: 2026-10-12.
2. **The product supplies invitation and coordination paths.** Onboarding invite codes reward both sides after tutorial completion and add them as friends; friend codes serve existing-player connection. Official limits include **four rewarded invitations per month**, not unlimited paid affiliate commissions ([invite help](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/23-pikmin-bloom/faq/3303-invite-code-and-rewards/), capture 2026-10-12). [Party Walk](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/june24-partywalk) lets hosts share a link/QR for aggregate steps across remote participants. Weekly challenges, postcards and mushroom cooperation give the relationship ongoing work.
3. **Creators can bring an existing community into one activity.** The official creator-program announcement names Alan Yu, Bloom Buds and Mr Mole, with staff contact and early update information; payment terms and a complete current roster are unknown ([program](https://community.pikminbloom.com/t/niantic-content-creators-program-x-pikmin-bloom/95), capture 2026-10-12). [Hot Girl Walk](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/june25-hotgirlwalk) connected a Santa Monica outing on **2025-06-08** to a remote walk on **2025-06-02–15**, with QR joining and mementos. The verified partnership does not establish paid delivery of each post.
4. **Local partners supply the destination and audience.** Zoo, Little Tokyo, convention and city routes attach in-game rewards to places people already visit. Current examples include [Nashville](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/oct26-nashville), [PAX Aus](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/oct26-paxaus) and [Kobe Journey](https://pikminbloom.com/en/pikmin-journey-2026-kobe) (capture 2026-10-12). Events also create reusable photos/video; Kobe's page explicitly discloses filming for marketing. Social follow/reward promotions connect participants back to official accounts ([historical anniversary campaign](https://pikminbloom.com/en/news/nov24-partywalk)); historical codes are not asserted active.
5. **A regional publishing operation and fans carry the assets.** Official Instagram profiles show **175K followers / 1,280 posts** globally, **154K / 1,272** in Japan, **31K / 442** in Korea and **164K / 348** in Traditional Chinese ([global](https://www.instagram.com/pikminbloom/), [Japan](https://www.instagram.com/pikminbloom_jp/), [Korea](https://www.instagram.com/pikminbloom.kr/), [TC](https://www.instagram.com/pikminbloom_tc/), capture 2026-10-12; followers rounded and overlapping). Pikmin Informer reposts official media but is not verified as a paid ambassador. App-owned regional websites did not establish an official Bloom TikTok profile; the same-name handles found were personal or unverified. No verified TikTok account farm/shared ownership network emerged.
6. **Commissioned lifestyle work and paid acquisition explain the mechanic.** [ENJIN](https://enjintokyo.com/en/works/pikminbloom/) describes **six groups documenting 14 days** of everyday walking changes; [Make Believe](https://makebelieve.com/case-study/pikmin-bloom/) describes **16 weeks** of social/community work (capture 2026-10-12). These prove production intent, not conversion. Meta, Google, Apple search ads and historical TikTok formats add paid distribution, detailed separately below.

Earned press framed a gentle walking game ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/newsbeat-59065097), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/22746461/pikmin-bloom-hands-on-niantic-iphone-android)); [Reddit coordination threads](https://www.reddit.com/r/PikminBloomApp/comments/1wusnz6/rpikminbloomapp_megathread_friend_codes_party/) and creator-hosted walks help players find people. These are visible distribution/retention surfaces, not measured acquisition attribution (capture 2026-10-12).

**The clearest human-walking story has a small personal boundary and a destination.** Liezy's vulnerable opening leads to crossing beyond the usual route for pizza Decor, then the actual seedling receipt. Product appears after the personal setup, rather than replacing it. **Utility and fandom perform different jobs.** Tips, historical naming tricks and feeding optimization help existing players. The vocal loop and instrument edits entertain fans; edited instrument sounds are not all genuine app behavior. The launch makes a personal walking promise and offers a next action; the recap is a faster event montage, posted after the originally announced virtual window. A sampled recap commenter reports beginning to walk/play: small reach does not prove no conversions.

**Current evidence is narrower than lifetime evidence.** The recent top entries in the table are instrument humor and another expedition episode. The sparse current-window sample cannot establish that the global ecosystem is inactive. Nor does older launch-era reach estimate current weekly distribution. Bloom commercial reposts also carry `isAd=false`; professional production/store badges do not change their captured distribution rank.

## What this means for ExcerLock (principles, not copies)

- **Make the consequence of moving easy to see.** Bloom's clearest paid walking explanation places feet and progress together. ExcerLock can show a selected app restricted under a verified exercise condition, real movement, and the actual access change. Check the particular condition in the current build against [product facts](../../core/product.md) before filming; active-walking access and step-target unlocking are distinct demonstrations.
- **Give the walk a personal purpose.** The pizza story is useful because someone crosses a small boundary for an immediate reward. An ordinary errand, neighborhood loop or gym visit can make ExcerLock's access consequence meaningful. The creator's actual experience supplies the stakes; a generic list of fitness benefits does not.
- **Pay off a promise across episodes.** Expedition posts create anticipation and show the return. ExcerLock can document a real attempt and subsequent outcome, with readable app evidence. This borrows continuity and suspense, not a virtual pet or a mental-health claim.
- **Separate entertainment, explanation and participation.** Nintendo affection helps Bloom's character loops travel. ExcerLock must build its own reason to care and ensure viewers understand the access mechanism. Compare qualified questions, shares and attributable activation where available; high views on a joke or high likes on a fan craft are not walking demand.
- **Let creators offer a joinable action using real capabilities.** Bloom's Party Walk lowers the gap between watching and participating. A creator-led walking commitment is a testable community principle, but this report does not establish that ExcerLock has collective totals, friend races, rewards or QR challenges. A brief must not promise unbuilt features.
- **Keep reward reliability part of creative credibility.** Bloom reviews pair walking motivation with sync/resource frustrations. For ExcerLock, capture actual lock/access behavior and use one clear condition, rather than editing a simulated unlock or claiming unmeasured timing. The receipt must pay the opening promise.
- **Treat localizations and distribution as context.** Meta's routine variants show why geography and paid delivery matter. Test a principle with the audience and product condition it fits; do not infer success from longevity alone, low EU reach alone, or historic archive estimates.
