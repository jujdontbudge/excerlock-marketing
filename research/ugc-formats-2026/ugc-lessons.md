# UGC formats, hooks and why they work (2026)

Taken from the best articles the 31 UGC accounts wrote this year. Every point says who said it. View and download numbers are the authors' own claims.

---

## Part 1 — Why videos work at all

**1. Stop me → make me care → make me stay.** ([jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2069420103247003873))
Something unexpected stops the thumb, but that only buys half a second. Relevance buys the next five: the viewer has to think "this is about me". After that, every line has to make the next one feel necessary.
*Attention = interrupt × relevance × emotion × curiosity × clarity.*

**2. Only three reasons people engage.** ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2052166609100300755), from 207K videos)
- **Emotion or opinion:** they feel strongly enough to comment.
- **Relatable or useful:** they send it to a friend.
- **Reference value:** they save it for later.

If a video triggers none of these, the views stay flat even when it gets pushed.

**3. People drop their guard for a friend, not an ad.** ([adriansolarzz](https://x.com/adriansolarzz/status/2076057600907444398), [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100408960327032917))
People are reflexively skeptical of anything that looks like an ad. A normal person filming on their phone reads as a friend sharing something. That's why the casual look beats the polished one: every bit of polish pushes the video toward "ad".
Jonny's test: *would this feel normal if they sent it privately to a friend?*

**4. The story comes first and the product earns its screen time.** ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2050602294064845175), [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657))
In every top performer, the product shows up after the viewer already cares. Videos that open on the product do worse. The product should be the hero of the story, not what the story is about. In the winning workplace skits it's on screen less than 25% of the time.

**5. Negative beats neutral.** ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2050602294064845175))
Same app, same feature, same steps:
- "STOP letting insta RUIN your horizontal pics": **4M views**
- "Don't let insta ruin…": 2.1M
- "How to post horizontal pics…": 475K

A hook that implies conflict or a problem beats one that promises a feature.

**6. Specific beats vague, every time.** ([adriansolarzz](https://x.com/adriansolarzz/status/2068380581453312245), [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2102879882153730442))
"You're studying wrong" slides past. "You're spending 3 hours rewriting notes instead of testing yourself" stops the right person. A general hook is aimed at everyone, which means it lands with no one.

**7. Make it a bit extreme.** ([Jibran_05](https://x.com/Jibran_05/status/2041189670432784551))
Every viral example had something polarizing or exaggerated in it: "$1,000", "60k in debt", "a BIG startup". Calm and reasonable gets scrolled past.

**8. Shorter wins.** ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2050602294064845175), [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657))
The best tutorials run 15–16 seconds, the best skits under 40. Text-and-face reactions should be **under 6 seconds** so people rewatch while they read, and the rewatches count as extra watch time. End the moment the payoff lands.

**9. A few videos do almost all the views.** ([ColinMaddenUGC](https://x.com/ColinMaddenUGC/status/2104494232975061425))
About 96% of videos get under 10K views and about 0.3% break 500K. That 0.3% brings in about 65% of all views. So the job is to take more shots and learn from each one, not to make one perfect video.
In his data, one creator with 1,800 followers posted the same hook 4 times and got 12.2M, 4.2M, 2.4M and 890K. Follower count barely matters.

**10. Copy the mechanism, not the video.** ([jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100408960327032917), [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2069420103247003873))
Break a viral video into hook → story → emotion → pacing → setting → format → when the product shows up → editing → CTA. The thing that actually won is usually something like *fear hook → personal story → late product reveal → visual demo*. That combination carries over to any product, and formats move freely between niches.

**11. Every viral format is new + old combined.** ([Jibran_05](https://x.com/Jibran_05/status/2036118330004636070))
Example: 2022's "covering my face, hiding a shocking stat" combined with 2024's Wrapped trend. Your best format may come from another category or another year.

**12. Formats die, so keep 5 or more running.** ([jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657), [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2085794926248305137))
Every format eventually wears out. Run a winner until it stops working, and have the next one ready. When a format wears out, change the format, not the hook.

## Part 2 — The formats

| Format | How it goes | Why it works | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talking head ("FaceTime")** | Creator talks to camera like a FaceTime with a friend: a painful problem, an embarrassing story, a strong opinion, a realization or a before/after. The product is worked into the story. | Reads as a friend, not an ad. A face builds trust fastest. Fails if the creator has nothing worth saying. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657), [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2077363054094991373) (6.4M dating app) |
| **Text on screen + face reaction** | One line of text that makes people think "that's literally me", "i'm cooked" or "wait, you can do that?", over the creator's expression. Under 6 seconds. | The face is part of the hook. Short length means people rewatch while reading. The easiest format to make in volume. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657) |
| **Hook + hidden product demo** | hook → problem → tension → demo → result. Never opens with "here's this cool app". | By the time the product appears, the viewer already knows why they'd care. The best view-to-install rate. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657), [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2077363054094991373) (35M demo) |
| **Shocked face → demo** | 1–2 seconds of a tight close-up shocked face plus text ("my boyfriend sent me this…"), then over the shoulder onto the phone screen. | The face and the curiosity do everything. One creator posted the same hook 3 times and all 3 hit. | [mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2104672704271974904) |
| **Snapchat style** | Looks exactly like a snap from a friend (Snapchat caption bar), then often cuts to a demo. | People's ad guard drops because it looks like a friend's snap. | [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2077363054094991373) (1.6M) |
| **Green screen reaction** | Creator in front of a screenshot, video or app, face moving in and out of frame. Can also react to *your own* winning video. | The text hook opens a question and you watch the reaction to answer it. Reacting to your own winner turns one format into two. | [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2077363054094991373) (16.3M), [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657) |
| **Skits** | A scenario the audience recognizes immediately, with tension (an argument, awkwardness, a hot take). The product is tucked inside it. Under 40 seconds. | People don't share ads, they share situations. Tension keeps them watching and commenting. Hardest to make, biggest reach. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657), [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2077363054094991373) (8.5M), [alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2050602294064845175) (14.6M workplace skit, app on screen <25%) |
| **Multi-creator Q&A** | Creator 1 asks ("how much weight did you lose?"), creator 2 answers, creator 3 gives the surprising answer and casually name-drops the product. | No story needed. Wanting to hear the next answer keeps people watching. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2099489975272116657) |
| **Street interview** | Mic, public place, one sharp question. The answer becomes the pitch. Number questions work best ("what do you spend…"). The product comes from the person answering, never the interviewer. | Reads as a stranger's opinion, not a claim. People watch street interviews for fun, so they don't notice it's an ad. | [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2092663408214540318) |
| **Listicle** | "3 tools I use to…" / "5 things nobody tells you". The product is one item, ideally between two ordinary ones. | Opens a question you need closed. The most saved and shared format. Sandwiching the product makes it feel like a genuine tip. | [jaxxdwyer](https://x.com/jaxxdwyer/status/2077363054094991373) (726K), [mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2104672704271974904) |
| **"Testing it so you don't have to"** | "Day [X] testing [X] so you don't have to…", then the screen, then the real result. | Removes skepticism. People love watching someone else take the risk. | [mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2103160089918193952) |
| **ASMR yapping** | Talking head for 45–60 seconds while the hands do something satisfying (slime, cutting fruit, skincare), a 5–10 second screen cut, then back. | The hands stop the scroll. Attention is split, so people miss words and rewatch. The movement keeps eyes on screen for a full minute. | [mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2103160089918193952) |
| **Before / after** | Show the "before" immediately → the process → the "after". | The gap between the two is the story, and people stay to see it close. Fails if the change looks fake. A modest, honest change beats a dramatic one. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2102879882153730442), [adriansolarzz](https://x.com/adriansolarzz/status/2080395086379061587) |
| **Product as the punchline** | The product creates the funny moment itself, e.g. an alarm that makes you take a selfie to turn it off. | Take the product out and there's no video. That's why it converts and doesn't just get views. | [alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2052166609100300755) (15.9% engagement) |
| **Film where the problem happens** | Shoot in the exact place the viewer has the problem: a lecture hall, a gym, a parked car. | The setting makes the problem obvious in one frame before anyone speaks. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100936269026984160) (Turbo AI, 300K users before ads) |
| **Same scenario, many versions** | One relatable situation (e.g. "essay due at midnight") posted with new hooks, creators and settings. | When something works, don't drop it, make variations. Jenni AI's deadline series got hundreds of millions of views. | [jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100936269026984160) |
| **Cringe filter → demo** | An old, cringe face filter looking shocked + "wait TikTok has this now?", then a 1-second cut to the phone. No talking, trending audio. | It looks completely out of place in the feed, which stops the scroll. A creator can make 10–15 in an afternoon. | [mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2104672704271974904) |
| **"-A" callout** | 6 seconds of an everyday moment, a fake notification, and a savage callout signed "-A" (the Pretty Little Liars stalker). | Nostalgia plus being called out. It feels like a meme, not an ad. | [mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2104672704271974904) |
| **Physical embarrassment** | A theme-park bar that won't close, a seatbelt extender, the face dropping in a changing-room mirror, being tagged in a photo (never show the photo). Silent for 3 seconds, the face carries it. | The viewer feels the moment before they realize it's an ad. | [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2092021634982871550) |
| **Social moments** | A double take, hiding at the edge of a group photo, a family member saying it out loud at dinner, running into an ex. | Uncomfortable and recognizable, so people feel it personally. | [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2092021634982871550) |
| **Split-screen day** | Two versions of the same person living the same day differently. | Shows a benefit that adds up over the day (energy, focus) without anyone saying it. | [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2092021634982871550) |
| **Overheard conversation** | Camera placed like you're listening from the next table, audio slightly distant. | Feels like you *found* the recommendation instead of being sold it. | [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2092021634982871550) |
| **Split screen with a satisfying loop** | The main content on one half, gameplay or soap cutting on the other. | Two things to watch leave no gap for the urge to scroll. | [johnvirality](https://x.com/johnvirality/status/2085036781787852823) |
| **"Found footage" look** | Deliberately rough: slightly off framing, natural light, no polished captions. | The more it looks like an accident, the longer people watch. | [johnvirality](https://x.com/johnvirality/status/2085036781787852823) |
| **"What if" story** | A wild question followed scene by scene, ending at the product. | People watch to the end to get the answer, and the brand is the answer. | [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2105073246970196252) |

## Part 3 — The hooks

**Hook types** ([jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2102879882153730442), the hook companion to his formats article)

| Hook | Formula | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Listicle | [number] + [specific things] + [outcome] | You know right away what you're getting |
| Mistake callout | "you're making [mistake] that's causing [bad outcome]" | Nobody wants to find out they've been doing it wrong for months |
| Don't do this, do this | "stop [old way]. start [new way]" | The contrast explains why the new thing exists |
| Contrarian | "everyone thinks X, but Y" | Contradicting a belief makes people check. It needs a real payoff. |
| Secret insider | "the [trick] every [group] should know" | Feels like being let in on something |
| Transformation | "from [bad before] to [good after]" | The change is the content |
| Bold claim | a strong, specific claim + immediate proof | You want to know if it's true. The bigger the claim, the more proof you need. |
| Relatable pain | "you know when [specific frustration]…" | Describes exactly how they feel |
| Question | "what if you could [outcome] without [pain]?" | People answer it in their heads |
| Curiosity gap | an intriguing statement + one thing held back | They stay to find out. Pay it off fast. |

**Hooks that keep going viral, with numbers**
- "I could LITERALLY KISS the [flight attendant / Google employee] who showed me this": 9.5M ([themariaines](https://x.com/themariaines/status/2080640679496003834)). A Spanish copy also went viral.
- "when you're too tired to study but can't sleep 'cause you need to study": 18.6M ([themariaines](https://x.com/themariaines/status/2080640679496003834))
- "what's wrong with my AI 😭": 15+ creators in one month, 50K–2.7M each ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2050602294064845175))
- "10 years on Amazon and I just found this": about 7M ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2052166609100300755))
- "imagine being scared of [X] when this literally exists" ([jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100936269026984160))
- "watch me realize I have [deadline] in [X] minutes" ([ErnestoSOFTWARE](https://x.com/ErnestoSOFTWARE/status/2033178137924612137))
- "Have been keeping this a secret. Deleting this in 24 hours…" ([mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2103160089918193952))
- "my boyfriend sent me this…" + a shocked face ([mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2104672704271974904))
- "This is your friendly reminder that…" / "Things I wish I knew before…" ([mikey_starts](https://x.com/mikey_starts/status/2103160089918193952))

**Rules for hooks**
- A hook can be **visual, spoken, audio or text, or several at once**. The face counts as part of it. ([jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2098412997211267467))
- The first 2 seconds decide almost everything. No "hey guys" warm-up. Open mid-action. ([adriansolarzz](https://x.com/adriansolarzz/status/2068380581453312245))
- Test 5–10 hooks on the **same body**. Changing the hook is the cheapest test there is. ([jasonzhou1993](https://x.com/jasonzhou1993/status/2099837130927427989), [CEO_Vlad](https://x.com/CEO_Vlad/status/2085794926248305137))
- Find hooks by searching TikTok, filtering to most-liked in the last month, and reading the comments: the audience's own words make the best hooks. ([themariaines](https://x.com/themariaines/status/2080640679496003834))
- Old hooks that went viral can be reused months later. ([themariaines](https://x.com/themariaines/status/2080640679496003834))
- Comment questions only work if people already have an opinion: "which of these 3 would you kill?" works, "what do you think of this app?" doesn't. ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2052166609100300755))

## Part 4 — Telling what's broken ([alexolim_](https://x.com/alexolim_/status/2052166609100300755))

| What you see | What's broken |
|---|---|
| 6+ videos stuck under 100 views | The account, not the content |
| Under 0.5× your normal views | The hook or the format: redo the first 1–2 seconds |
| 0.5–1.5× normal | Retention: the hook worked but people left before the payoff, so cut the middle |
| Over 3× normal | A breakout: copy it and change as little as possible |
| Engagement under 2% | No emotion: add something debatable or a question people can answer |
| Lots of views, no downloads | The video would make sense without the product in it |

Change **one thing at a time**, otherwise you can't tell what worked. ([jonnyscales](https://x.com/jonnyscales/status/2100408960327032917))
