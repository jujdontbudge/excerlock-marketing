## What repeats

1. The slideshow is the cheap scale unit. Several slides of ordinary niche content, then the app. Opening on the product is what the writers say loses.
2. Volume is the system. Sulfur and Mufasa both say most posts stay small and a few carry the result, so they run many accounts and repeat a format that already moved.
3. Views are not the score. Mufasa's stated conversion gap is the reason to watch comments and installs.
4. The app still has to be recognisable. A late slide, a comment keyword, or a screen recording of one action. A bare link is what these posters avoid on TikTok.
5. The same pictures get reused. Pinterest is named as the image bank. Instagram is named as another place new accounts get suspended, not as a proven second growth engine in this sample.
6. The listing is the other free surface. Hand-made screenshots (Ferx) and a muted autoplay preview made from a screen recording (Zach Witzel). Aryan's posts are the ASO-only case, and he says it plateaued.

Paid creator networks, clipping campaigns and Apple or Meta ads show up as the step after a format already converts. They are outside a no-spend start.
