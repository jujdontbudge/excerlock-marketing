## The white space (verified across all 595 records)

The phone is named as a gym problem — distraction, doomscrolling, "8h41m
screen time" — but **no competitor sells focus/lock-in as the mechanism.**
That is ExcerLock's unoccupied position.

## Hooks that repeat (first 3 seconds)

- **Callout:** conditional viewer callout over a live set ("If you…")
- **Tangible object in frame 0:** reward merch/challenge card before any pitch
- **Timestamp/clock stamps:** suffering → reward, product as the receipt
- **Comment-sticker drama:** real question/critique on screen, answered bluntly
- **Cheat-sheet/printable hook:** chart or calendar in frame 0, day-peels after
- **Sensory GRWM opener:** drink crack / garment / micro-action before talk

## Audience split

385/595 adaptations route to **gym girls** (primary), 122/595 to **fitness
girls** (beginner/returner), 72/595 to **walking girls** — the thinnest

## Shoot first (cheapest, strongest organic traceability)

1. **T3** — five-move demo chain: one implement, burned-in labels, 3–4s cuts
2. **T11** — realistic place-walk: named location, micro-observations, step-count reveal, next-route question
3. **T6** — comment-proof skeleton: reply-sticker → demo → streak/reward screen proof

Every walking post gets a burned-in reply action (number/place/time
fill-in-the-blank) — never a bare invitation.

## Do NOT carry over (report §10)

Extreme transformations ("30 lbs in 28 days", "melt fat in 9 minutes"),
precision-body claims from one photo, body-warping filters as enhancement,
unsubstantiated authority ("Duke neuroscientists", "#1 app"), medicalized
shortcuts, AI actors + "hypothetical story" disclaimers as claim cover,
unlicensed footage/music. ExcerLock rule: mechanisms it can prove (sessions
locked, streaks kept), timelines it can defend, real people, licensed audio.
